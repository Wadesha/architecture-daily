城镇聚落

Urban Settlements (19 sites)

陕西是中国古代城市发展最早的地区之一。从新石器时代的石峁遗址，到西周丰镐、秦咸阳、汉长安、隋唐长安，陕西的古代城市见证了中华文明的发展历程。明清时期，陕西形成了以西安为中心，包括榆林卫城、府谷府州城、吴堡石城等在内的城镇体系。西安古城墙、钟楼、鼓楼是古代城市建筑的杰出代表。

Shaanxi is one of the earliest regions of ancient Chinese urban development. From the Neolithic Shimao Site to Western Zhou Fenghao, Qin Xianyang, Han Chang'an, and Sui-Tang Chang'an, Shaanxi's ancient cities witnessed the development of Chinese civilization. In the Ming-Qing, Shaanxi formed an urban system centered on Xi'an, including Yulin Garrison City, Fugu Fuzhou City, and Wubuo Stone City. Xi'an Ancient City Wall, Bell Tower, and Drum Tower are outstanding representatives of ancient urban architecture.

石峁遗址原文详解 Detailed
榆林市神木市

石峁遗址位于榆林市神木县高家堡镇洞川沟附近的山梁上，属新石器时代龙山晚期至夏代早期超大型聚落遗存，是目前所见中国史前时期面积最大的城址。2006年公布为第六批全国重点文物保护单位。石峁遗址距今约4000年，面积约425万平方米。其大型'石城'使用寿命超过300年。遗址地处黄土高原北部，由'皇城台'、内城和外城构成，是探索中国文明起源的重要遗址。

Shimao Site, located on a ridge near Dongchuangou, Gaojiabao Town, Shenmu County, Yulin, is a super-large settlement relic from the late Neolithic Longshan to early Xia dynasty, currently the largest prehistoric city site in China. Listed as a Major Historical and Cultural Site under National Protection in 2006 (6th batch). Dating to about 4,000 years ago, it covers about 4.25 million sq m. Its large 'stone city' was used for over 300 years. Located in the northern Loess Plateau, it consists of 'Imperial Platform,' inner city, and outer city—an important site for exploring the origins of Chinese civilization.

丰镐遗址原文详解 Detailed
西安市长安区

丰镐是西周王朝的都城，由丰京和镐京组成，位于西安市长安区沣河两岸。丰京在沣河西，镐京在沣河东，两京隔河相望。丰镐是沿河谷阶地发展起来的城市，城市设计因地制宜、朴素自然，强调实用功能。广义的丰镐地区西起涝河，东至橘水，北达渭水，南及秦岭北麓，可划分为都邑区、王陵区、农业种植区、游乐渔猎区和放牧区等不同功能区域。秦始皇修阿房宫与汉武帝修昆明池时，镐京遗址遭到毁灭性破坏，整个城址规模、布局已难考据。

Fenghao was the capital of the Western Zhou dynasty, consisting of Fengjing and Haojing, located on both banks of the Feng River in Chang'an District, Xi'an. Fengjing was west of the Feng River, Haojing east, facing each other across the river. Fenghao was a city developed along river valley terraces, with city design adapting to local conditions, simple and natural, emphasizing practical function. The broad Fenghao area extended from the Lao River in the west to the Jue River in the east, north to the Wei River, and south to the northern foothills of the Qinling, divided into capital, royal tomb, agricultural, recreational hunting, and grazing zones. When Qin Shi Huang built Epang Palace and Emperor Wu of Han built Kunming Lake, the Haojing site was devastatingly destroyed, making its scale and layout difficult to verify.

秦都咸阳城原文详解 Detailed
咸阳市

秦咸阳城遗址是战国后期秦国都城遗址，中心地处陕西省咸阳市以东约15公里处的窑店镇牛羊村一带。遗址北至高干渠，南到渭河北岸，东到柏家嘴，西到任家嘴，东西长6公里，南北宽3公里，面积18平方公里。1988年公布为第三批全国重点文物保护单位。秦孝公十二年（公元前350年），秦都城自栎阳迁至咸阳。秦始皇统一中国后，在渭北'因北陵营殿'，扩建先王宫室，营建风格各异的六国宫殿。秦始皇三十五年（公元前212年），营造咸阳最大宫殿——朝宫，其前殿阿房宫面积约87万平方米，现存总面积约65万平方米。

Qin Xianyang City Site is the capital site of the State of Qin in the late Warring States period, centered at Niuyang Village, Yaodian Town, about 15km east of Xianyang City. The site extends north to Gaogan Canal, south to the north bank of the Wei River, east to Baijiazui, west to Renjiazui, 6km east-west and 3km north-south, covering 18 sq km. Listed as a National Protection Site in 1988 (3rd batch). In the 12th year of Duke Xiao of Qin (350 BCE), the Qin capital moved from Liyang to Xianyang. After unifying China, Qin Shi Huang 'built palaces on the northern mounds' north of the Wei River, expanding former royal palaces and building six different styles of palaces. In the 35th year of Qin Shi Huang (212 BCE), the largest palace in Xianyang—the Chaogong—was built; its front hall, Epang Palace, covered about 870,000 sq m, with about 650,000 sq m surviving.

汉长安城原文详解 Detailed
西安市未央区

汉长安城遗址是西汉时期的都城遗址，位于西安城西北郊约10公里处的未央区汉城乡，面积约34.39平方公里。1961年公布为第一批全国重点文物保护单位。西汉长安城是在秦咸阳兴乐宫基础上建立起来的。汉高祖五年（公元前202年）刘邦决定修复兴乐宫，并改名为长乐宫，并以此为基础兴建都城，名为长安城。汉高祖七年（公元前200年）二月，长乐宫建成。汉高祖八年（公元前199年），又在长乐宫西侧兴建未央宫，作为西汉长安的主要宫殿。汉武帝太初元年（公元前104年）兴建建章宫，太初四年（公元前101年）营建桂宫、明光宫，扩建北宫，开凿昆明池和上林苑，使汉长安城的建设达到极盛。

Han Chang'an City Site is the Western Han capital site, located at Hancheng Township, Weiyang District, about 10km northwest of Xi'an, covering about 34.39 sq km. Listed as a National Protection Site in 1961 (1st batch). Western Han Chang'an was built on the basis of Qin Xianyang's Xingle Palace. In the 5th year of Emperor Gaozu of Han (202 BCE), Liu Bang decided to restore Xingle Palace, rename it Changle Palace, and build the capital on this basis, named Chang'an. In the 7th year (200 BCE), Changle Palace was completed. In the 8th year (199 BCE), Weiyang Palace was built west of Changle Palace as the main palace of Western Han Chang'an. In the 1st year of Taichu of Emperor Wu (104 BCE), Jianzhang Palace was built; in the 4th year (101 BCE), Gui Palace and Mingguang Palace were built, North Palace expanded, Kunming Pool and Shanglin Park excavated, bringing Han Chang'an's construction to its peak.

隋唐长安城原文详解 Detailed
西安市

隋唐长安城是在隋大兴城基础上发展起来的。隋文帝开皇二年（582年），因汉长安城制度狭小、水污染严重，在汉长安城东南的龙首原南麓兴建新都，名大兴城。唐建立后改大兴城为长安城，逐步扩建。唐长安城由外郭城、皇城、宫城三大部分组成，面积约84平方公里，人口逾百万，是当时世界上最大的城市。唐长安城采用中轴对称布局，南北中轴线为朱雀大街，东西两市对称分布，108坊排列整齐。唐长安城的规划不仅在当时影响到日本的平城京、平安京及唐渤海上京龙泉府等，而且对新中国成立后的西安市总体规划，也起到很大的借鉴作用。

Sui-Tang Chang'an City developed on the basis of Sui Daxing City. In the 2nd year of Kaihuang of Emperor Wen of Sui (582 CE), because Han Chang'an was cramped and had severe water pollution, a new capital named Daxing City was built at the southern foot of Longshou Plateau, southeast of Han Chang'an. After the Tang was founded, Daxing City was renamed Chang'an City and gradually expanded. Tang Chang'an consisted of outer city, imperial city, and palace city, covering about 84 sq km with a population exceeding one million—the largest city in the world at the time. Tang Chang'an used central axis symmetry, with Zhuque Avenue as the north-south axis, east and west markets symmetrically distributed, and 108 wards neatly arranged. Tang Chang'an's planning not only influenced Japan's Heijokyo, Heiankyo, and Balhae's Shangjing Longquanfu at the time, but also served as an important reference for Xi'an's master plan after the founding of New China.

府谷府州城原文详解 Detailed
榆林市府谷县

府州城位于榆林市府谷县，2006年公布为第六批全国重点文物保护单位。府州城山河襟带，北枕长城，西濒神木。整座城的平面呈曲尺形，面积约23200平方米。城墙内夯黄土，外以石砌，周长2320米，高7.2米。府州城共有六门，东、南、西、北四面均辟一大门，西、南两面均增设一小门。六座城门上均设有城楼，大南门、小西门，外筑瓮城。府州城内现存有许多古建筑。城中心有钟楼，其东有文庙、城隍庙、崇圣祠、明伦堂、魁星楼、鼓楼；西有关帝庙、二郎庙、观音殿、祖师坛等。府州城南部有千佛洞、荣河书院和南寺；北部有上帝庙。另外，城中有飞檐斗拱牌楼六座缀饰其上。

Fuzhou City is located in Fugu County, Yulin, listed as a National Protection Site in 2006 (6th batch). Fuzhou City is embraced by mountains and rivers, with the Great Wall to the north and Shenmu to the west. The city plan is carpenter's-square shaped, covering about 23,200 sq m. The walls are rammed with yellow loess inside and stone-faced outside, 2,320m circumference, 7.2m tall. Fuzhou City has six gates: one large gate on each of the east, south, west, and north sides, plus one small gate on the west and south sides. All six gates have gate towers; the Great South Gate and Small West Gate have outer barbican entrances. Many ancient buildings survive inside Fuzhou City: a bell tower at the center, with Confucian temple, City God temple, Chongsheng Shrine, Minglun Hall, Kuixing Tower, and drum tower to its east; Guandi Temple, Erlang Temple, Guanyin Hall, and Zushi Altar to the west. In the south are Thousand-Buddha Cave, Ronghe Academy, and South Temple; in the north is Shangdi Temple. Additionally, six flying-eave dougong archways decorate the city.

榆林卫城原文详解 Detailed
榆林市

榆林卫城为明长城九边重镇之一，现存古城大体为明代建制，是陕西省保存较完整的古城。2006年公布为第六批全国重点文物保护单位。明永乐元年（1403年）在今榆林市设置榆林寨，正统初年（1436年）改建为堡，成化七年（1471年）增立榆林卫，成化九年（1473年），延绥镇迁驻于榆林卫城。受河水和山势所限，明代曾三拓榆林城：明成化二十二年（1486年）扩北城，弘治五年（1492年）扩南城，正德十年（1515年）建南关外城，均为向南、北两面发展，因而形成南北狭长的布局。卫城地处半山坡处，东高西低，平面呈不规则矩形，南北较长，近3公里，东西略窄，约1公里多，周围约7公里。因建于长城脚下驼峰山上，又名'驼城'。

Yulin Garrison City was one of the nine strategic towns of the Ming Great Wall. The surviving ancient city is largely of Ming construction, one of the better-preserved ancient cities in Shaanxi. Listed as a National Protection Site in 2006 (6th batch). In the 1st year of Yongle of the Ming (1403 CE), Yulin Fort was established at present-day Yulin; in the early Zhengtong era (1436 CE) rebuilt as a fort; in the 7th year of Chenghua (1471 CE) Yulin Garrison was established; in the 9th year of Chenghua (1473 CE), the Yansui Military Command moved to Yulin Garrison City. Limited by rivers and mountains, the Ming expanded Yulin City three times: north city in 1486, south city in 1492, and outer south city in 1515—all expanding north and south, forming a long north-south layout. Located on a hillside, high in the east and low in the west, the plan is an irregular rectangle, nearly 3km north-south, about 1km east-west, about 7km circumference. Built at the foot of the Great Wall on Tuofeng (Camel Hump) Mountain, it is also called 'Camel City.'

宁强青木川古镇原文详解 Detailed
汉中市宁强县

青木川古镇位于汉中市宁强县，回龙场老街始于明代，形成于清中叶，繁盛于1940年代。它曾是陕甘川三省交界地带最繁荣、最负盛名的商业、文化交流场所。古街上近百户人家的房子大多都是四合院，雕梁画栋，四水归堂，二进二出的两层结构，建筑风格有明清时期的旱船屋，也有西方建筑元素。魏辅唐统治时期，这里是非常繁华的集贸市场，烟馆、茶馆、宾馆、酒楼、饭店，应有尽有，店铺林立，商贾云集，远近闻名的唐世盛、辅友社、辅仁剧社、荣盛魁、荣盛昌等商业文化机构场所都在这条街上。老街总长近600米，一直贯通没有分支，从北至南分为下街、中街和上街。

Qingmuchuan Ancient Town is located in Ningqiang County, Hanzhong. Huilong Old Street began in the Ming, took shape in the mid-Qing, and flourished in the 1940s. It was once the most prosperous and prestigious commercial and cultural exchange hub at the Shaanxi-Gansu-Sichuan border. Nearly all hundred households on the old street have courtyard houses with carved beams and painted rafters, four-water-gathering halls, two-story two-courtyard structures, with architectural styles including Ming-Qing dry-boat houses and Western elements. During Wei Futang's rule, it was a bustling market with opium dens, teahouses, inns, restaurants, and wine shops, with shops lining the street and merchants gathering. Famous commercial and cultural institutions like Tang Shisheng, Fuyou Society, Furen Drama Club, Rongsheng Kui, and Rongsheng Chang were all on this street. The old street is nearly 600m long with no branches, divided from north to south into Lower, Middle, and Upper Streets.

西安古城墙原文详解 Detailed
西安市

西安城墙是我国现存最完整的古代州府级城墙。古城墙始建于明洪武七年（1374年），系在唐长安城皇城和元代奉元城基础上修筑，至洪武十一年（1378年）竣工。初建时为夯土墙，四面均开辟一座城门，东为长乐门，西为安定门，南为永宁门，北为安远门。明嘉靖五年（1526年）重修城楼。明隆庆二年（1568年）对城墙外壁包砌青砖。崇祯末年，为防农民起义军，又增筑四关郭城，今四'梢门'即四关郭门位置，形成今西安旧城关区。清乾隆年间（1736-1795年）大规模维修城墙，再次在外壁包砌青砖，增设排水道、垛口和女儿墙，疏浚护城河。1961年被公布为全国第一批重点文物保护单位。城墙平面为长方形，东西长4256米，南北宽2708米，周长13.74公里。墙高12米，底宽18米，顶宽15米。

Xi'an City Wall is the most complete surviving ancient prefectural-level city wall in China. Construction began in the 7th year of Hongwu of the Ming (1374 CE), built on the basis of Tang Chang'an's imperial city and Yuan Fengyuan City, completed in the 11th year of Hongwu (1378 CE). Initially rammed earth walls with four gates: Changle (east), Anding (west), Yongning (south), and Anyuan (north). Gate towers were rebuilt in the 5th year of Jiajing (1526 CE). In the 2nd year of Longqing (1568 CE), the outer wall was faced with blue brick. In the late Chongzhen era, four outer barbican cities were added to defend against peasant rebels, forming today's Xi'an old city gate districts. During Qianlong of the Qing (1736-1795), major repairs re-faced the outer wall with blue brick, added drainage channels, battlements, and parapets, and dredged the moat. Listed as a National Protection Site in 1961 (1st batch). The wall is rectangular, 4,256m east-west, 2,708m north-south, 13.74km circumference, 12m tall, 18m base width, 15m top width.

西安钟楼原文详解 Detailed
西安市

西安钟楼始建于明洪武十七年（1384年），是我国现存规模最大、保存最完整的钟楼。钟楼整体以砖木结构为主，由基座、楼体及屋顶三部分组成，通高36米，建筑面积1377.64平方米。基座平面呈正方形，每边长35.5米，基座高8.6米，基座下有高与宽均为6米的十字形券洞与东、南、西、北四条大街相通。钟楼木质阁楼分两层，平面呈正方形，每边长21.2米，面阔、进深各三间，高27.4米。每层四角均有明柱回廊，下檐斗拱为三踩单昂，上檐斗拱为五踩双昂。梁枋彩绘华丽，隔扇雕饰精美。屋顶为重檐三滴水攒尖顶，覆绿色琉璃瓦，置鎏金宝瓶刹。

Xi'an Bell Tower was founded in the 17th year of Hongwu of the Ming (1384 CE), the largest and best-preserved surviving bell tower in China. The whole structure is mainly brick-wood, consisting of base, tower body, and roof, 36m total height, 1,377.64 sq m building area. The base is square, 35.5m per side, 8.6m tall, with a cross-shaped arched passage 6m high and wide underneath connecting to the four main streets. The wooden pavilion has two stories, square plan 21.2m per side, three bays in width and depth, 27.4m tall. Each corner has open column corridors; lower-eave dougong are three-step single ang, upper-eave dougong five-step double ang. Beam painted decorations are gorgeous, lattice carvings exquisite. The roof is a double-eave three-water-drop pyramidal roof covered with green glazed tiles, with a gilded bottle finial.

西安鼓楼原文详解 Detailed
西安市

西安鼓楼位于钟楼以西，位于西大街北口。始建于明洪武十三年（1380年），清康熙三十八年（1699年）、乾隆五年（1740年）曾两次重修。至今仍较完好地保留明代建筑的面貌，为国内现存最大的鼓楼建筑。1956年公布为陕西省重点文物保护单位，1996年公布为第四批全国重点文物保护单位。西安鼓楼是一座重檐三滴水歇山顶的阁楼式建筑，整体以砖木结构为主，由基座、楼体及屋顶三部分组成，通高33米。基座用青砖砌成，平面呈长方形，底边长52.6米，宽37.8米，高近9米，建筑面积1998.8平方米。中间辟6米高南北向券洞贯通街道，形制如同门楼。

Xi'an Drum Tower is located west of the Bell Tower, at the north entrance of West Street. Founded in the 13th year of Hongwu of the Ming (1380 CE), it was twice repaired in the 38th year of Kangxi (1699 CE) and 5th year of Qianlong (1740 CE) of the Qing. It still preserves its Ming appearance relatively intact, and is the largest surviving drum tower building in China. Listed as a Shaanxi Provincial Protection Site in 1956 and a National Protection Site in 1996 (4th batch). 彬县大佛寺石窟位于咸阳市彬州市，始凿于唐贞观二年（628年），是陕西最大的摩崖造像石窟。大佛寺石窟有大佛洞、千佛洞、罗汉洞等洞窟，大佛洞内的阿弥陀佛高20米，是陕西最大的石刻佛像。大佛寺石窟是唐代皇家石窟的代表，造像精美，是中国古代石窟艺术的重要遗存。

Great Buddha Temple Grottoes are located in Binzhou City, Xianyang, first carved in the 2nd year of Zhenguan of Tang (628 CE), the largest cliff-carving grotto in Shaanxi. The grottoes include Great Buddha Cave, Thousand-Buddha Cave, and Arhat Cave; the Amitabha Buddha in Great Buddha Cave is 20m tall—the largest stone Buddha statue in Shaanxi. Great Buddha Temple Grottoes are representative of Tang royal grottoes, with exquisite statues, an important surviving example of ancient Chinese grotto art.

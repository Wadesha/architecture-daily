概说 | Introduction

上党地区雄踞太行山上，地势高峻，物产丰富。这里有着淳朴刚健的人民，在历史上，尤其是抗日战争时期，曾作出重要贡献。在建筑方面，由于历代劳动人民的创造，遗留下了很多优秀的古代建筑，从战国到明清，从石窟寺塔到殿宇桥梁，从城镇规划到民居装饰，类型丰富，数量众多。

The Shangdang region sits atop the Taihang Mountains, with rugged terrain and abundant resources. Its hardworking people have made important contributions throughout history, especially during the Anti-Japanese War. In architecture, generations of craftspeople have left a rich legacy spanning from the Warring States period to the Ming-Qing dynasties, encompassing grottoes, pagodas, halls, bridges, town planning, and vernacular decoration.

1962年秋，古建筑学家张驭寰（梁思成先生助手）赴晋东南地区考察，专署邀请留下编写此书，一个月完成，北京科学出版社初版。47年后天津大学出版社再版。本书收录70个建筑项目、134幅图版，是研究中国北方古建筑的重要资料。

In autumn 1962, architectural historian Zhang Yuhuan (assistant to Liang Sicheng) conducted fieldwork in southeastern Shanxi. Invited by the local government to compile this book, he completed it in one month; it was first published by Beijing Science Press. Forty-seven years later, Tianjin University Press reissued it. The book records 70 architectural sites with 134 photographic plates, making it an essential resource for studying ancient architecture in northern China.

法兴寺
Faxing Temple
图版10, 12, 13, 24, 34, 36-38
位置: 长子县庄头慈林山 | Cilin Mountain, Zhuangtou, Zhangzi County
历史背景
法兴寺现存的平面布局形式为唐代规模，经楼、佛塔在殿之前。经楼为石造，是唐时原物。石塔一造于咸亨四年(673)，平面八角形，三层，檐做叠涩式，塔顶仰莲座上置覆钵及相轮五层，此种八角形唐塔在国内很少见。石塔二平面方形，五层，各檐做叠涩式，塔顶山花蕉叶上置半开莲层、重重莲层、仰莲层、宝瓶，与晋城县青莲寺唐乾宁二年(895)所造的慧峰石塔之塔刹形制相同。第一层塔身嵌有元至元二年(1265)墓碑一块，似为蒙古建元之前数年统治这一地区时，修建庙宇者所换之物。
Faxing Temple preserves a Tang-dynasty layout with scripture towers and pagodas before the main hall. The stone scripture tower is an original Tang artifact. Pagoda 1, built in 673 (Xianheng 4), is octagonal with three stories and corbeled eaves — an octagonal Tang pagoda is rare in China. Pagoda 2 is square with five stories and corbeled eaves, its finial matching that of the Huifeng Pagoda at Qinglian Temple (895). A Yuan-dynasty tomb stele of 1265 is embedded in the first story.
长明灯 | Eternal Lamp
长明灯造于唐大历八年(773)，灯身做八角形，有中空的灯室，置于基座上，基座分为八角形基座、圆形莲座，上覆攒尖八角顶、大仰莲层、宝珠。长明灯亦称灯幢或灯台，在佛教中是属六种供具之一，梵名弥播，其遗物目前所知除此台外，仅太原市龙山童子寺有北齐石灯台和黑龙江省宁安县隆兴寺渤海国石灯两处，故弥足珍贵。
The eternal lamp, built in 773 (Dali 8), has an octagonal body with a hollow lamp chamber on an octagonal base and circular lotus pedestal, topped by a pyramidal octagonal roof, large lotus layer, and jewel. One of the six Buddhist offerings (Sanskrit: Dipamkara), only three survive in China — this one, a Northern Qi lamp at Tongzi Temple in Taiyuan, and a Balhae lamp at Longxing Temple in Ning'an, Heilongjiang.
中大殿 | Main Hall
中大殿宋元丰三年(1080)重建，平面三间，进深六架椽屋，六椽栿南北贯通，并自栿上置驼峰、大斗，中间置直斗实拍拱，上承四椽栿，四椽栿上各材均为后换物。不过上平榑缝有替木、令拱、大斗、攀间等，悉如宋制。
The main hall was rebuilt in 1080 (Yuanfeng 3). Three bays wide, six rafters deep, with a six-rafter beam spanning north-south, camel-hump blocks and large blocks above, and direct-block solid brackets supporting the four-rafter beam. The upper purlin joints retain Song-style timber blocks, ling brackets, large blocks, and inter-tie beams.

《硖石寺大隋远法师遗迹记》碑首阴刻佛殿图
Buddhist hall drawing on the stele head of 'Record of Master Yuanyuan of Xiashi Temple'
图版11
位置: 晋城县寺南庄 | Sinanzhuang, Jincheng County
历史背景
此幅佛殿图是唐宝历元年(825)硖石寺大隋远法师遗迹记的碑首纹样。原碑存古青莲寺南殿中。图中所刻是弥勒（一称慈氏）讲经的情况，因为当时弥勒尚未成佛，所以服装仍是印度在家人（菩萨）的装束，头戴花冠，结跏趺坐莲台上。身后背景是两层高阁，四周带有廊庑，与前面山门相连。因为所雕的内容虽为印度佛教故事，但出自我国远法师之手，所以是唐代寺院的写实作品。高阁即俗称的弥勒阁，为当时最流行的建筑式样。此碑雕刻年代约晚于西安大雁塔西门楣刻佛殿图百余年，在建筑史上是一份很有价值的资料。
This Buddhist hall drawing is the stele-head ornament of the 825 (Baoli 1) "Record of Master Yuanyuan of Xiashi Temple," preserved in the south hall of Ancient Qinglian Temple. It depicts Maitreya preaching — still in layman's (bodhisattva) attire with a flower crown, seated in lotus position. Behind him is a two-story pavilion surrounded by corridors connected to the front gate. Though depicting an Indian Buddhist story, carved by a Chinese master, it is a realistic depiction of a Tang monastery. The pavilion, commonly called Maitreya Pavilion, was the most popular architectural style of the time. Carved about a century after the Buddhist hall relief on the west lintel of the Giant Wild Goose Pagoda in Xi'an, it is a highly valuable architectural document.

北留石塔
Beiliu Stone Pagoda
图版14
位置: 阳城县北留 | Beiliu, Yangcheng County
历史背景
塔在北留公社路旁，寺已毁，只存孤塔。塔基为方形，四角有倚柱，上施塔座，上做叠涩，中雕半开莲。塔身为八角形，两层，在第一层塔身南向辟门，门两侧雕守门力士，拱券上雕飞天、坐佛、云彩，已风化。塔室中刻释迦佛、迦叶、阿难及二胁侍菩萨。自二层起塔身显著低矮。第二层塔身正面刻奉为开元圣文神武皇帝敬造浮图一座字样，乃知为唐代开元年间(713-741)作品。唯塔身中段前部已毁，现用砖补砌。
The pagoda stands by the roadside at Beiliu; the temple is destroyed, leaving only this solitary tower. Square base with corner pilasters, corbeled pedestal with a half-open lotus carving. Octagonal two-story body: first story has a south door with guardian figures, flying apsarasas, seated Buddha, and clouds above the arch (weathered); interior carved with Shakyamuni, Kashyapa, Ananda, and two attendant bodhisattvas. The second story bears an inscription dedicating the pagoda to "Emperor Kaiyuan Shengwen Shenwu," dating it to the Kaiyuan era (713-741). The middle front section is destroyed and now repaired with brick.

青莲寺慧峰石塔
Huifeng Stone Pagoda at Qinglian Temple
图版15-16
位置: 晋城县寺南庄 | Sinanzhuang, Jincheng County
历史背景
塔身平面为八角形，下刻宝装莲花。莲座上每角做束莲柱，普拍枋下雕垂幛纹。前面开方门，后面刻文：唐故先师和尚，汝州襄城县人也，俗姓贺阑氏，法号慧峰。于中和戊申岁八月二十八日迁化去。乾宁乙卯年建造灵塔，十月功毕后记耳。弟子及功德主玄藏、弟子玄恬……塔身宝盖上置山花蕉叶、半开莲层、仰覆莲层、宝瓶。乾宁乙卯年即乾宁二年(895)。此塔雕法极精，为唐末不可多得的石造建筑物。基座圆形，上下层各雕仰伏宝装莲花，中间石面雕人形鸟的半开莲层，图案别致。基座下为八角形须弥座，半埋土中。
Octagonal body carved with precious lotus below. Bundle-lotus columns at each corner, with hanging curtain patterns under the pulpit tie beam. Square door at front; rear inscription records Master Huifeng (surname Helan, from Xiangcheng, Ruzhou), who passed away on the 28th day of the 8th month of Zhonghou Wushen (888), and whose relic pagoda was completed in the 10th month of Qianning Yimao (895). Above the canopy are mountain-flower banana leaves, half-open lotus, up-turned lotus, and jewel vase. The carving is extremely refined — a rare late-Tang stone structure. Circular base with up-turned and down-turned precious lotus layers, and a distinctive half-open lotus with human-bird figures in between. Below is an octagonal Xumi pedestal, half-buried.

清化寺石塔（四座）
Qinghua Temple Stone Pagodas (Four Pagodas)
图版17-20
位置: 长治县师庄羊头山 | Yangtoushan, Shizhuang, Changzhi County
历史背景
石塔一为圆形，四层密檐式，层层叠涩出檐，且有优美的轮廓。过去的发现，唐塔为正圆形平面的很少见，上党地区已发现的有六座，其中三座已倒塌，极为罕见和珍贵。石塔二为圆形塔，七层密檐式。此塔出檐与前者同，但收分甚大，从下至上全部为大型石块构筑。位于山之最高峰，数十里外均可望见。石塔三平面为方形，两层单檐式，基座半埋土中。此塔出檐厚重，线条古朴，上置四坡水式的塔顶，可能是唐代的作品。石塔四平面为正方形，在方形台基上置塔座一层，座身方形，施以雕刻，座的上面为仰莲一层。塔身立面刻造像二龛，上为四坡水顶。檐角雄厚，起翘甚锐，在唐塔中很少见。
Pagoda 1: circular, four-story multi-eaved, with corbeled eaves and graceful silhouette. Circular-plan Tang pagodas are extremely rare — six have been found in Shangdang, three of which have collapsed. Pagoda 2: circular, seven-story multi-eaved, with strong entasis, built entirely of large stone blocks at the mountain peak, visible from dozens of li away. Pagoda 3: square, two-story single-eaved, with heavy eaves and archaic lines, topped by a four-slope hip roof — likely Tang. Pagoda 4: square, with a carved square pedestal and up-turned lotus layer, two sculptured niches on the body, and a four-slope hip roof with robust, sharply upturned eaves — rare among Tang pagodas.

玉溪石塔
Yuxi Stone Pagoda
图版21-22
位置: 沁水县玉溪 | Yuxi, Qinshui County
历史背景
塔平面为方形，高五层，檐做叠涩式。在石台之上有基座三层，在中层基座束腰部分四周刻建塔缘起和施财人姓名。塔初层特高，南向辟门，门两侧雕守门力士，拱券上雕飞天、坐佛、云彩，已风化。塔室中刻释迦佛、迦叶、阿难及二胁侍菩萨。自二层起塔身显著低矮。虽未见造塔年月铭文，但此种方塔与原存朔县崇福寺内北魏天安元年(466)小石塔形制极为相似。塔背刻文玉溪村东归古浮图多年损坏，已有常主张得善舍孳牛一只重修……正统十年八月吉日。可见此塔自唐创建后到明正统十年(1445)，曾于重修时改换底层塔身前二石。底层门周浮雕的佛画，大多风化，内容有仙鹤、仙马、力士、盘龙柱……题材很丰富。
Square plan, five stories, corbeled eaves. Three pedestal layers above the stone platform; the middle pedestal's waisted section is carved all around with the pagoda's founding story and donor names. The first story is exceptionally tall with a south door flanked by guardian figures, and flying apsarasas, seated Buddha, and clouds above the arch (weathered). Interior carved with Shakyamuni, Kashyapa, Ananda, and two attendants. Though no founding date inscription survives, this square pagoda closely resembles the Northern Wei small stone pagoda of 466 at Chongfu Temple in Shuozhou. A rear inscription records repairs in the 8th month of Zhengtong 10 (1445), when the front two stones of the base were replaced. The door surround reliefs include cranes, celestial horses, guardians, and coiled-dragon columns — rich subject matter.

海会院明惠大师塔
Minghui Master Pagoda at Haihui Monastery
图版23

From September to November 1962, Zhang Yuhuan conducted fieldwork on ancient architecture in southeastern Shanxi. It is published first in the hope of serving as a reference for architectural historians and archaeologists at home and abroad.

—— 张驭寰，1962年12月20日 | Zhang Yuhuan, December 20, 1962

碉楼
Watchtower (Diaolou)

中文释义：碉楼是四川西部藏族、羌族地区的独特高层建筑形式，以石块或土坯砌筑，高可达数十米，有四角、六角、八角等多种平面形式。碉楼具有防御、瞭望、居住等多种功能，是嘉绒藏族和羌族建筑文化的代表。丹巴古碉群、直波碉楼、日斯满巴碉房等是四川碉楼的杰出代表，其中丹巴碉楼被誉为'千碉之国'。碉楼的建造不用图纸，全凭工匠口传心授，是中国古代石构建筑的奇迹。

English: Watchtowers (diaolou) are unique high-rise architectural forms in Tibetan and Qiang areas of western Sichuan, built of stone blocks or adobe, reaching heights of tens of meters, with square, hexagonal, octagonal and other plan forms. Serving multiple functions including defense, observation, and residence, they represent the architectural culture of the Jiarong Tibetan and Qiang peoples. The Danba ancient watchtower cluster, Zhibo watchtowers, and Risimanba watchtower house are outstanding representatives; Danba is known as the 'Country of a Thousand Watchtowers.' Built without blueprints, relying entirely on craftsmen's oral transmission, watchtowers are miracles of ancient Chinese stone architecture.

实例 Examples: 丹巴古碉群、直波碉楼、日斯满巴碉房、乡城碉楼群 | 关联 Related: 羌寨, 土司官寨, 藏传佛教建筑, 石构建筑
穿斗式
Column-and-Tie Structure (Chuandoushi)

中文释义：穿斗式是四川地区传统建筑最主要的木结构形式，以柱子直接承檩，柱子之间用穿枋串联，形成整体框架。与北方抬梁式不同，穿斗式不用大梁，柱子较密，结构轻巧，适合四川多雨潮湿的气候和山地地形。四川民居、寺庙、会馆等普遍采用穿斗式结构，望江楼明确记载'采用穿斗式梁架，是川渝地区常见的梁架构造之一'。穿斗式结构是川派建筑的核心技术特征。

English: Column-and-tie structure (chuandoushi) is the main wooden structural form of traditional Sichuan architecture, with columns directly supporting purlins and columns connected by tie beams to form an integral frame. Unlike northern post-and-lintel style, column-and-tie structure uses no large beams, has denser columns, and is lightweight—suited to Sichuan's rainy humid climate and mountainous terrain. Sichuan residences, temples, and guildhalls universally use column-and-tie structure; Wangjiang Tower explicitly records 'using column-and-tie beam framing, one of the common beam structures in the Sichuan-Chongqing region.' Column-and-tie structure is the core technical feature of Sichuan-style architecture.

实例 Examples: 望江楼、成都民居、洛带会馆、四川各地民居 | 关联 Related: 川派建筑, 悬山顶, 小青瓦, 抬梁式
悬山顶
Overhanging Gable Roof (Xuanshanding)

中文释义：悬山顶是四川传统建筑最常见的屋顶形式，屋面有一条正脊和四条垂脊，两侧山面的檩木伸出山墙之外，形成悬挑。悬山顶有利于排水和保护山墙，适合四川多雨的气候。四川民居、寺庙、会馆普遍采用悬山顶，与北方的硬山顶形成对比。悬山顶的出檐深远，屋脊常有灰塑和剪粘装饰，是川派建筑的显著特征。

English: Overhanging gable roof (xuanshanding) is the most common roof form of traditional Sichuan architecture, with one main ridge and four vertical ridges, and the purlins on both gable sides extending beyond the gable wall to form an overhang. Overhanging gable roofs facilitate drainage and protect gable walls, suited to Sichuan's rainy climate. Sichuan residences, temples, and guildhalls universally use overhanging gable roofs, contrasting with northern flat-gable roofs. With deep eaves and ridge decorations of gray plaster and cut-and-paste porcelain, overhanging gable roofs are a distinctive feature of Sichuan-style architecture.

实例 Examples: 四川各地民居、洛带会馆、平武报恩寺 | 关联 Related: 穿斗式, 小青瓦, 川派建筑, 硬山顶
汉阙
Han Dynasty Gate (Han Que)

中文释义：汉阙是汉代宫殿、祠庙、陵墓前的标志性建筑，通常左右成对，中间为通道，具有标识等级和装饰功能。四川是中国汉阙保存最多的省份，有'中国汉阙之乡'之称，著名的有雅安高颐阙、芦山樊敏阙、渠县冯焕阙、沈府君阙、绵阳平阳府君阙、李业阙、乐山杨公阙等。汉阙以石材建造，仿木结构，雕刻精美，是研究汉代建筑和雕刻艺术的重要实物。

English: Han dynasty gates (han que) are landmark buildings in front of Han palaces, temples, and tombs, usually in left-right pairs with a passage between, serving grade-marking and decorative functions. Sichuan is the province with the most surviving Han que in China, known as the 'Hometown of Chinese Han Que,' with famous examples including Gao Yi Que in Ya'an, Fan Min Que in Lushan, Feng Huan Que and Shen Fujun Que in Qu County, Pingyang Fujun Que and Li Ye Que in Mianyang, and Yang Gong Que in Leshan. Built of stone imitating wooden structure with exquisite carvings, Han que are important artifacts for studying Han architecture and carving art.

实例 Examples: 高颐阙、樊敏阙、冯焕阙、沈府君阙、平阳府君阙 | 关联 Related: 汉代建筑, 石雕, 陵墓建筑, 牌坊
摩崖造像
Cliff Carvings (Moya Zaoxiang)

中文释义：摩崖造像是在山崖壁面上雕刻的佛教、道教造像，是四川石窟艺术的主要形式。四川摩崖造像数量众多，规模宏大，著名的有大足宝顶山和北山摩崖造像、广元千佛崖、皇泽寺、巴中南龛、水宁寺、资阳毗佛寺、安岳卧佛院、眉山牛角寨、能仁寺等。四川摩崖造像始于南北朝，盛于唐宋，是中国石窟艺术的重要组成部分，与敦煌、云冈、龙门石窟并称。

English: Cliff carvings (moya zaoxiang) are Buddhist and Taoist statues carved on cliff faces, the main form of Sichuan cave art. Sichuan has numerous and large-scale cliff carvings, with famous examples including the Baodingshan and Beishan carvings at Dazu, Qianfo Cliff and Huangze Temple in Guangyuan, Nankan and Shuining Temple in Bazhong, Pifo Temple in Ziyang, Wofo Temple in Anyue, Niujiaozhai and Nengren Temple in Meishan. Sichuan cliff carvings began in the Northern and Southern Dynasties and flourished in the Tang-Song, an important component of Chinese cave art, ranked with Dunhuang, Yungang, and Longmen grottoes.

实例 Examples: 大足宝顶山、北山摩崖造像、广元千佛崖、巴中南龛 | 关联 Related: 石窟寺, 佛教建筑, 唐代建筑, 宋代建筑
会馆
Guild Hall (Huiguan)

中文释义：会馆是明清时期同乡或同业在异地建立的社会组织建筑，具有聚会、议事、祭祀、商业等功能。四川会馆数量众多，是清代'湖广填四川'移民文化的产物，著名的有重庆湖广会馆、自贡西秦会馆、成都洛带会馆（广东会馆、江西会馆、湖广会馆、川北会馆）等。四川会馆建筑融合了原籍和四川本地建筑风格，多为院落式布局，有戏楼、正殿、厢房等，装饰精美，是研究清代移民文化和建筑艺术的重要实物。

English: Guild halls (huiguan) are social organization buildings established by same-hometown or same-trade groups in other places during the Ming-Qing, serving assembly, discussion, worship, and commercial functions. Sichuan has numerous guild halls, a product of the Qing 'Huguang fills Sichuan' immigration culture, with famous examples including Chongqing Huguang Guild Hall, Zigong Xiqin Guild Hall, and Chengdu Luodai Guild Halls (Guangdong, Jiangxi, Huguang, and North Sichuan guild halls). Sichuan guild hall architecture blends hometown and local Sichuan styles, mostly in courtyard layouts with theater stages, main halls, and wing rooms with exquisite decoration—important for studying Qing immigration culture and architectural art.

实例 Examples: 重庆湖广会馆、自贡西秦会馆、洛带会馆 | 关联 Related: 移民文化, 戏楼, 川派建筑, 院落式布局
土司官寨
Tusi Official Fortress (Tusi Guanzhai)

中文释义：土司官寨是元明清时期西南少数民族地区土司（世袭地方统治者）的官邸和衙署，具有政治、军事、居住等多种功能。四川土司官寨主要分布在阿坝、甘孜等藏羌地区，著名的有马尔康卓克基土司官寨、小金沃日土司官寨经楼与碉楼等。土司官寨融合了藏族、羌族和汉族建筑风格，多为石木结构，有碉楼、经堂、官署、住宅等，是研究中国土司制度和藏羌建筑的重要实物。

English: Tusi official fortresses (tusi guanzhai) are the residences and government offices of tusi (hereditary local rulers) in southwestern minority areas during the Yuan-Ming-Qing, serving political, military, and residential functions. Sichuan tusi fortresses are mainly distributed in Tibetan and Qiang areas such as Aba and Ganzi, with famous examples including Zhuokeji Tusi Fortress in Maerkang and the Woju Tusi Fortress scripture hall and watchtower in Xiaojin. Blending Tibetan, Qiang, and Han architectural styles, mostly in stone-wood structure with watchtowers, scripture halls, government offices, and residences, they are important for studying China's tusi system and Tibetan-Qiang architecture.

实例 Examples: 卓克基土司官寨、沃日土司官寨经楼与碉楼 | 关联 Related: 碉楼, 羌寨, 藏传佛教建筑, 土司制度
羌寨
Qiang Village (Qiangzhai)

中文释义：羌寨是羌族的传统村落，以石砌房屋和碉楼为主要建筑形式，依山而建，错落有致。羌族建筑以片石和黄泥砌筑，不用绘图和吊线，全凭经验和目测，房屋为平顶，多层，底层养牲畜，中层住人，顶层晒粮。著名的羌寨有理县桃坪羌寨、萝卜寨等。桃坪羌寨始建于汉代，有2000多年历史，保存有完整的地下水网和碉楼，被誉为'羌族建筑艺术活化石'。

English: Qiang villages (qiangzhai) are traditional villages of the Qiang people, with stone houses and watchtowers as main architectural forms, built along hillsides in a staggered arrangement. Qiang architecture is built of flagstones and yellow mud without drawings or plumb lines, relying entirely on experience and visual estimation; houses are flat-roofed and multi-story, with livestock on the ground floor, residents on the middle floor, and grain drying on the top floor. Famous Qiang villages include Taoping Qiang Village and Luobo Village in Li County. Taoping Qiang Village, founded in the Han dynasty with over 2,000 years of history, preserves a complete underground water network and watchtowers, known as the 'living fossil of Qiang architectural art.'

实例 Examples: 桃坪羌寨、萝卜寨 | 关联 Related: 碉楼, 碉房, 羌族, 石构建筑
藏传佛教建筑
Tibetan Buddhist Architecture

中文释义：藏传佛教建筑是四川甘孜、阿坝地区的主要建筑类型，包括寺院、经堂、佛塔、印经院等。四川藏传佛教建筑融合了西藏、青海和本地建筑风格，多为石木结构，墙体厚实，窗户较小，屋顶有金顶、法轮、祥麟等装饰。著名的有德格印经院、八邦寺、白玉噶陀寺、长青春科尔寺、达扎寺、草登寺、木里大寺等。德格印经院是中国最大的藏文印经院，被誉为'藏文化大百科全书'。

English: Tibetan Buddhist architecture is the main building type in Ganzi and Aba regions of Sichuan, including monasteries, scripture halls, pagodas, and printing houses. Sichuan Tibetan Buddhist architecture blends Tibetan, Qinghai, and local styles, mostly in stone-wood structure with thick walls, small windows, and roof decorations of golden roofs, Dharma wheels, and auspicious deer. Famous examples include Dege Printing House, Bangong Temple, Baiyu Gatuo Temple, Changqing Chunkeer Temple, Dazha Temple, Caodeng Temple, and Muli Temple. Dege Printing House is China's largest Tibetan scripture printing house, known as the 'Encyclopedia of Tibetan Culture.'

实例 Examples: 德格印经院、八邦寺、长青春科尔寺、达扎寺 | 关联 Related: 印经院, 经堂, 土司官寨, 碉楼
印经院
Scripture Printing House (Yinjingyuan)

中文释义：印经院是藏传佛教地区印刷藏文经典的专门建筑，以木刻雕版印刷为主要工艺。四川德格印经院是中国最大、最著名的藏文印经院，始建于清雍正七年（1729年），保存有书版20余万块，涵盖佛教经典、医学、历算、文学、艺术等内容。印经院建筑为藏式石木结构，有经版库、印刷室、裁纸室、装订室等，是研究藏族印刷文化和建筑的重要实物。

English: Scripture printing houses (yinjingyuan) are specialized buildings for printing Tibetan scriptures in Tibetan Buddhist areas, using woodblock printing as the main technique. Sichuan's Dege Printing House is China's largest and most famous Tibetan scripture printing house, founded in the seventh year of Yongzheng of the Qing (1729 CE), preserving over 200,000 woodblocks covering Buddhist scriptures, medicine, astronomy, literature, and art. In Tibetan stone-wood structure with woodblock storehouses, printing rooms, paper cutting rooms, and binding rooms, printing houses are important for studying Tibetan printing culture and architecture.

实例 Examples: 德格印经院 | 关联 Related: 藏传佛教建筑, 经堂, 藏族文化, 木刻雕版
戏楼
Theater Stage (Xilou)

中文释义：戏楼是四川会馆、祠堂、寺庙中常见的建筑，用于演出川剧等地方戏曲。四川戏楼多为木结构，悬山顶，台口面向正殿或院落，有上下场门、后台、化妆室等。戏楼的装饰精美，梁枋、雀替、斗拱常有木雕和彩画。四川著名的戏楼有重庆湖广会馆戏楼、自贡西秦会馆戏楼、洛带会馆戏楼等，是研究清代戏曲文化和建筑装饰的重要实物。

English: Theater stages (xilou) are common buildings in Sichuan guild halls, ancestral halls, and temples, used for performing Sichuan opera and other local dramas. Sichuan theater stages are mostly wooden structures with overhanging gable roofs, the stage opening facing the main hall or courtyard, with entrance/exit doors, backstage, and dressing rooms. Exquisitely decorated, the beams, spandrels, and bracket sets often have wood carvings and painted decorations. Famous Sichuan theater stages include those at Chongqing Huguang Guild Hall, Zigong Xiqin Guild Hall, and Luodai Guild Hall—important for studying Qing drama culture and architectural decoration.

实例 Examples: 重庆湖广会馆戏楼、自贡西秦会馆戏楼、洛带会馆戏楼 | 关联 Related: 会馆, 祠堂, 川派建筑, 木雕
天井
Courtyard/Light Well (Tianjing)

中文释义：天井是四川传统民居和会馆中常见的院落空间，面积较小，四面房屋相连，具有采光、通风、排水的功能。与北方四合院的宽敞不同，四川天井较为狭小，适应了四川多雨潮湿的气候和密集的城市布局。天井中常有水缸、花台、鱼缸等，既防火又装饰。天井是川派民居的核心空间，体现了四川人对自然和生活的理解。

English: Courtyards/light wells (tianjing) are common courtyard spaces in traditional Sichuan residences and guild halls, small in area with connected houses on all sides, serving lighting, ventilation, and drainage functions. Unlike the spacious northern courtyard houses, Sichuan courtyards are smaller, adapting to Sichuan's rainy humid climate and dense urban layout. Courtyards often contain water vats, flower beds, and fish tanks for both fire prevention and decoration. The courtyard is the core space of Sichuan-style residences, reflecting Sichuan people's understanding of nature and life.

实例 Examples: 四川各地民居、洛带会馆、自贡西秦会馆 | 关联 Related: 川派建筑, 院落式布局, 穿斗式, 悬山顶

川派建筑
Sichuan Style Architecture (Chuanpai Jianzhu)

中文释义：川派建筑是四川地区传统建筑的统称，融合了中原、江南、湖广、藏羌等多种建筑文化，形成了独特的地方风格。川派建筑以穿斗式木结构、悬山顶、小青瓦、深出檐为主要特征，布局灵活，适应山地地形。川派建筑包括民居、寺庙、会馆、祠堂、园林、桥梁等多种类型，是中国地域建筑的重要流派。四川盆地的气候、地形和移民文化共同塑造了川派建筑的独特面貌。

English: Sichuan style architecture (chuanpai jianzhu) is a general term for traditional architecture in Sichuan, blending Central Plains, Jiangnan, Huguang, Tibetan-Qiang and other architectural cultures to form a unique local style. Characterized by column-and-tie wooden structure, overhanging gable roofs, small grey tiles, and deep eaves, with flexible layouts adapting to mountain terrain. Sichuan style architecture includes residences, temples, guild halls, ancestral halls, gardens, and bridges—an important school of Chinese regional architecture. Sichuan Basin's climate, terrain, and immigration culture jointly shaped the unique face of Sichuan style architecture.

实例 Examples: 望江楼、洛带会馆、四川各地民居、平武报恩寺 | 关联 Related: 穿斗式, 悬山顶, 小青瓦, 会馆
地主庄园
Landlord Manor (Dizhu Zhuangyuan)

中文释义：地主庄园是四川近代大地主的住宅建筑群，规模宏大，融合了传统民居和近代建筑风格。四川著名的地主庄园有大邑刘氏庄园、泸县屈氏庄园、南充丁氏庄园等。大邑刘氏庄园是四川最大的地主庄园，占地7万余平方米，有房屋350余间，保存有完整的庄园建筑和大量文物，是研究中国近代地主经济和社会生活的重要实物。

English: Landlord manors (dizhu zhuangyuan) are residential building complexes of large modern landlords in Sichuan, grand in scale, blending traditional residence and modern architectural styles. Famous Sichuan landlord manors include the Liu Family Manor in Dayi, Qu Family Manor in Luxian, and Ding Family Manor in Nanchong. The Liu Family Manor in Dayi is Sichuan's largest landlord manor, covering over 70,000 sq m with more than 350 rooms, preserving complete manor architecture and numerous cultural relics—important for studying modern Chinese landlord economy and social life.

实例 Examples: 大邑刘氏庄园、泸县屈氏庄园、南充丁氏庄园 | 关联 Related: 民居, 近代建筑, 川派建筑, 院落式布局
教堂
Church (Jiaotang)

中文释义：教堂是近代西方传教士在四川建立的宗教建筑，融合了西方建筑风格和中国传统建筑技术。四川著名的教堂有成都平安桥天主教堂（拜占庭风格）、彭州领报修院（法式修道院）等。平安桥天主教堂建于1897年，由法国籍骆书雅主教主持修建，历时七年，是由大小经堂、圣母无染原罪堂、主教公署组成的拜占庭风格建筑群，108根楠木廊柱气势恢宏。教堂建筑是四川近代中西文化交流的重要见证。

English: Churches (jiaotang) are religious buildings built by Western missionaries in modern Sichuan, blending Western architectural styles with traditional Chinese building techniques. Famous Sichuan churches include Chengdu Ping'anqiao Catholic Church (Byzantine style) and Pengzhou Lingbao Seminary (French monastery). Ping'anqiao Catholic Church, built in 1897 under the direction of French Bishop Louis Marsot over seven years, is a Byzantine-style complex consisting of large and small chapels, the Immaculate Conception Church, and the bishop's office, with 108 nanmu colonnades of grand momentum. Church architecture is an important witness to modern Sichuan-Western cultural exchange.

实例 Examples: 成都平安桥天主教堂、彭州领报修院 | 关联 Related: 近代建筑, 中西合璧, 宗教建筑, 楠木
盐井建筑
Salt Well Architecture (Yanjing Jianzhu)

中文释义：盐井建筑是四川自贡地区独特的盐业生产建筑，包括天车（井架）、碓房、大车房、灶房、柜房等。自贡是中国井盐的主要产地，有2000多年的盐业历史，燊海井是世界上第一口超千米的深井，凿成于清道光十五年（1835年），深1001.42米。自贡盐井建筑以天车最为壮观，天车由数百根杉木绑扎而成，高达数十米，是中国古代钻井技术的奇迹。自贡西秦会馆、桓侯宫、王爷庙等也与盐业文化密切相关。

English: Salt well architecture is a unique salt production architecture in Zigong, Sichuan, including derricks (tianche), pounding rooms, winch rooms, boiling rooms, and offices. Zigong is China's main well-salt producer with over 2,000 years of salt history; Shenhai Well, drilled to 1,001.42m in the 15th year of Daoguang of the Qing (1835 CE), was the world's first well exceeding 1,000m. The most spectacular salt well architecture is the derrick, built by binding hundreds of fir logs, reaching tens of meters high—a miracle of ancient Chinese drilling technology. Zigong's Xiqin Guild Hall, Huanhou Palace, and Wangye Temple are also closely related to salt culture.

English: Ba-Shu culture is the regional culture created by the ancient Ba and Shu states in Sichuan, an important component of Chinese regional culture. Sichuan gardens, residences, guild halls, and temples all reflect the aesthetic taste of Ba-Shu culture. Ba-Shu culture had close exchanges with Central Plains, Chu, and Qin cultures, forming a unique cultural face.

近代建筑
Modern Architecture (Jindai Jianzhu)

中文释义：四川近代建筑融合了中西建筑风格，以教堂、领事馆、洋行、教会学校等为代表。成都平安桥天主教堂（拜占庭风格）、彭州领报修院（法式修道院）是四川近代宗教建筑的代表。重庆作为近代开埠城市，保存有大量近代建筑，包括湖广会馆、通远门、东水门及城墙等。四川近代建筑是研究中国近代化进程和中西文化交流的重要实物。

English: Modern Sichuan architecture blends Chinese and Western styles, represented by churches, consulates, foreign firms, and missionary schools. Chengdu Ping'anqiao Catholic Church (Byzantine style) and Pengzhou Lingbao Seminary (French monastery) are representatives of modern religious architecture in Sichuan. As a modern treaty port, Chongqing preserves numerous modern buildings, including the Huguang Guild Hall, Tongyuan Gate, and Dongshui Gate and City Wall. Modern Sichuan architecture is important for studying China's modernization process and Sichuan-Western cultural exchange.

成都市 (35 sites)

成都，四川省会，简称蓉城或锦城，地处四川盆地西部，有'天府之国'的美誉。成都有近3000年的建城史，文化遗产极为丰富，是国务院首批公布的24个历史文化名城之一。其中都江堰、青城山被列入世界文化与自然遗产名录。成都建筑融合了川派传统和近代西洋风格，武侯祠、杜甫草堂、望江楼展现了成都在历朝历代的不同历史角色与故事。

Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan,简称 Rongcheng or Jincheng, located in the western Sichuan Basin, has the reputation of 'Land of Abundance.' Chengdu has nearly 3,000 years of city history with extremely rich cultural heritage, one of the first 24 historical and cultural cities announced by the State Council. Dujiangyan and Qingcheng Mountain are listed as UNESCO mixed cultural and natural heritage sites. Chengdu architecture blends Sichuan tradition and modern Western styles; Wuhou Shrine, Du Fu Thatched Cottage, and Wangjiang Tower show Chengdu's different historical roles and stories through dynasties.

杜甫草堂详解 Detailed
成都

杜甫草堂是成都地区最著名的景点之一，唐代大诗人杜甫流寓成都时居住于此。唐乾元二年（759年），杜甫因安史之乱辗转来到成都。次年，在友人的帮助下杜甫在浣花溪修建了数间茅屋。杜甫寓居于此的四年间写下了多达247首诗，或忧国忧民，或怀古咏史，或歌咏自然风光。杜甫离去后，茅屋在风雨中受到了极大的损坏，以致踪迹难寻。为表达对诗人的追忆与缅怀，五代前蜀韦庄等人在其旧址重建了茅屋。后经宋、元、明、清等年代多达13次修葺扩建。现存建筑为嘉庆十六年（1811年）重建，经过新中国成立以来的良好修缮，形成了现今的规模。现在的杜甫草堂，实际是后人为怀念'诗圣'而修建的一座优美的园林。主要建筑有大廨、诗史堂、柴门、工部祠、碑亭等，恢宏古朴、布局紧凑、错落有致，建筑与园林相映成趣。1999年曾发掘出水井、房屋、窖坑等，现已建成遗址博物馆。

Du Fu Thatched Cottage is one of Chengdu's most famous scenic spots, where the great Tang poet Du Fu lived during his exile in Chengdu. In the second year of Qianyuan of the Tang (759 CE), Du Fu came to Chengdu due to the An Lushan Rebellion. The following year, with friends' help, he built several thatched cottages by Huanhua Stream. During his four-year stay, he wrote as many as 247 poems—concerned for the nation and people, recalling history, or singing of natural scenery. After Du Fu left, the cottage was severely damaged by wind and rain until its traces were hard to find. To express remembrance, Wei Zhuang of the Former Shu in the Five Dynasties rebuilt a cottage on the old site. Through Song, Yuan, Ming, and Qing dynasties, it underwent as many as 13 repairs and expansions. The existing buildings were rebuilt in the 16th year of Jiaqing of the Qing (1811 CE), and through good restoration since the founding of New China, they have reached their current scale. Today's Du Fu Thatched Cottage is actually a beautiful garden built by later generations to commemorate the 'Poet Sage.' Main buildings include the Great Hall, Poetry History Hall, Firewood Gate, Ministry of Works Hall, and stele pavilions—grand and simple, compact in layout, well-arranged, with architecture and garden complementing each other. In 1999, wells, houses, and storage pits were excavated, now forming a site museum.

平安桥天主教堂详解 Detailed
成都

1897年，法国籍骆书雅主教在成都近郊召集了一批工匠，历时七年，主持修建了平安桥天主教堂。百年来，教堂历经战乱、地震、风雨侵蚀，在保护与修缮下仍保留了原有风貌，造型别致，古朴典雅，很具宗教特色。教堂坐落于成都市天府广场的西北角，是由大小经堂、圣母无染原罪堂、主教公署组成的一片拜占庭风格建筑群。教堂前为近年新修的灯光广场，矗立着十余米高的圣母无染原罪堂牌楼，花木雕像交相辉映，气势非凡。圣母无染原罪堂能容千余人，小经堂专供主教、神父祭献。教堂四周走廊宽敞相通，三道花园将教堂分成格式廊亭。整个教堂的梁、柱、门窗、地板全部用料考究。108根楠木廊柱气势恢宏，烘托出肃穆神圣的宗教氛围。

In 1897, French Bishop Louis Marsot gathered a group of craftsmen on the outskirts of Chengdu and, over seven years, oversaw the construction of Ping'anqiao Catholic Church. Over the past century, the church has endured wars, earthquakes, and wind and rain erosion, yet under protection and restoration it retains its original appearance—unique in form, simple and elegant, with strong religious character. Located at the northwest corner of Tianfu Square in Chengdu, the church is a Byzantine-style complex consisting of large and small chapels, the Immaculate Conception Church, and the bishop's office. In front of the church is a newly built lighting square, with a 10-plus-meter-tall Immaculate Conception archway standing, where flowers, trees, and statues complement each other in imposing splendor. The Immaculate Conception Church can hold over a thousand people, while the small chapel is reserved for the bishop and priests. The surrounding corridors are spacious and interconnected, with three gardens dividing the church into patterned pavilions. The entire church's beams, columns, doors, windows, and floors all use carefully selected materials. Its 108 nanmu colonnades are of grand momentum, creating a solemn and sacred religious atmosphere.

望江楼详解 Detailed
成都

望江楼又名'崇丽阁'，取的是晋代文学家左思《蜀都赋》'既崇且丽，实号成都'之意。因濒临锦江，登楼远眺，水光天色，尽收眼底，故人们又称之为望江楼。望江楼建于清代，一是为了纪念唐代女诗人薛涛；二是为了弘扬文风。自清代以来逐步建造，形成了如今由薛涛井、濯锦楼、吟诗楼、崇丽阁、浣笺亭、五云仙馆、泉香榭、流杯池、清婉室等构成的望江楼公园。望江楼为木结构建筑，楼石砌基座，共四层，上面两层八角攒尖，下面二层四角飞檐。朱柱碧瓦，宝顶鎏金。采用穿斗式梁架，是川渝地区常见的梁架构造之一。每层的屋脊、雀替都饰有精美的禽兽泥塑和人物雕刻。阁廊十分宽敞，每方四柱。屋面盖以绿色琉璃瓦，金光耀目。翘角飞檐，雕梁画栋，稳健雄伟中透着江南楼宇的秀丽玲珑。

Wangjiang Tower, also known as 'Chongli Pavilion,' takes its name from the line 'both lofty and beautiful, truly called Chengdu' from the 'Shu Capital Rhapsody' by Jin dynasty writer Zuo Si. Because it borders the Jinjiang River, climbing the tower to gaze afar offers a panoramic view of water and sky, hence people also call it Wangjiang (River Viewing) Tower. Built in the Qing dynasty, it was constructed both to commemorate the Tang dynasty poetess Xue Tao and to promote literary culture. Since the Qing, it has been gradually built, forming today's Wangjiang Tower Park consisting of Xue Tao Well, Zhuojin Tower, Poetry Recitation Tower, Chongli Pavilion, Huanjian Pavilion, Wuyun Immortal Hall, Quanxiang Pavilion, Liubei Pond, and Qingwan Room. Wangjiang Tower is a wooden structure with a stone foundation, four stories total—the upper two stories are octagonal pyramidal, the lower two stories have four-corner flying eaves. Red columns and green tiles, gilded finial. Using column-and-tie beam framing, it is one of the common beam structures in the Sichuan-Chongqing region. Each layer's ridge and spandrel are decorated with exquisite animal clay sculptures and figure carvings. The pavilion corridors are very spacious, with four columns on each side. The roof is covered with green glazed tiles, dazzling gold. With flying eaves, painted beams and carved rafters, it exudes the elegance and delicacy of Jiangnan towers amid steadiness and grandeur.

武侯祠详解 Detailed
成都

唐代名胜武侯祠始建于5世纪，宋、元时加以修葺，明初与毗邻的刘备墓、刘备庙并为一处，形成了纪念三国蜀汉君臣的祠堂。明末不幸毁于战火，清康熙十一年（1672年）重建，形成现在君臣合庙的格局。在中轴线上依次分布着大门、二门、刘备殿、过厅、诸葛亮殿五重建筑，旁另有桂荷楼、船舫、桂荷池、听鹂苑等附属建筑。占地面积约3.7万平方米，总建筑面积约1.25万平方米。武侯祠坐南朝北，就连钟鼓楼的方位，也与传统的钟鼓楼不同。正所谓'晨钟暮鼓'，一般东为钟楼，西为鼓楼，但武侯祠却正好相反，取的是诸葛亮不忘北伐，统一汉室之意。公元223年，诸葛亮病逝于五丈原，因其生前被封为武乡侯，死后又被蜀汉后主刘禅追谥为'忠武侯'，因此历史上尊称其祠庙为武侯祠。

The Tang dynasty scenic spot Wuhou Shrine was founded in the 5th century, repaired in the Song and Yuan, and in the early Ming merged with the adjacent Liu Bei Tomb and Liu Bei Temple to form a shrine commemorating the ruler and ministers of Shu Han of the Three Kingdoms. Unfortunately destroyed by war in the late Ming, it was rebuilt in the 11th year of Kangxi of the Qing (1672 CE), forming the current combined ruler-minister temple layout. On the central axis, five layers of buildings are distributed in sequence: the main gate, second gate, Liu Bei Hall, passage hall, and Zhuge Liang Hall, with additional buildings such as Guihe Tower, boat pavilion, Guihe Pond, and Tingli Garden. Covering about 37,000 sq m with a total building area of about 12,500 sq m. Wuhou Shrine faces south; even the orientation of its bell and drum towers differs from tradition. As the saying goes 'morning bell, evening drum,' normally the east is the bell tower and west the drum tower, but Wuhou Shrine is exactly the opposite—taking the meaning of Zhuge Liang not forgetting the northern expedition to unify the Han. In 223 CE, Zhuge Liang died of illness at Wuzhangyuan; because he was enfeoffed as Marquis of Wuxiang during his life and posthumously honored as 'Marquis of Loyalty and Martial Might' by Liu Shan, the second emperor of Shu Han, his shrine is historically respectfully called Wuhou Shrine.

都江堰详解 Detailed
成都

都江堰位于都江堰市城西，始建于秦昭王末年（约公元前256-前251年），由蜀郡太守李冰父子主持修建，是全世界至今为止年代最久、唯一留存、以无坝引水为特征的宏大水利工程。都江堰由鱼嘴、飞沙堰、宝瓶口三大主体工程组成，科学地解决了江水自动分流、自动排沙、控制进水流量等问题，使成都平原成为'水旱从人，不知饥馑'的天府之国。都江堰历经2200多年仍在发挥作用，2000年与青城山一起被列入世界文化遗产名录。

Dujiangyan, located west of Dujiangyan City, was founded in the late reign of King Zhaoxiang of Qin (c. 256-251 BCE), built under the direction of Shu Prefecture Governor Li Bing and his son. It is the world's oldest surviving, only remaining large-scale water conservancy project characterized by damless water diversion. Dujiangyan consists of three main projects: Fish Mouth, Flying Sand Weir, and Bottle Neck, scientifically solving problems of automatic water diversion, automatic sand discharge, and inflow control, making the Chengdu Plain a 'Land of Abundance' where 'flood and drought follow human will, and famine is unknown.' After more than 2,200 years, Dujiangyan continues to function, and was listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site together with Qingcheng Mountain in 2000.

青城山古建筑群详解 Detailed
成都

青城山位于都江堰市西南，是中国道教发祥地之一，有'青城天下幽'之誉。青城山历史悠久，曾叫汶山、天谷山、丈人山、赤城山、清城都、天国山等名。这里林木葱翠、四季常青，丹梯千级，曲径通幽，以幽洁取胜，自古就有'青城天下幽'的美誉。青城山之幽与剑门之险、峨眉之秀、夔门之雄齐名。山门绮丽繁复，山内现存古建筑包括建福宫、天师洞（常道观）、朝阳洞、上清宫、真武宫（祖师殿）、圆明宫、玉清宫、天然图画、五洞天及接仙桥、凝翠桥、山荫亭等。青城山古建筑群依山取势，不拘不构；亭台轩榭、石桥古道多就地取材不加雕琢，与山水完美融合。

Qingcheng Mountain, located southwest of Dujiangyan City, is one of the birthplaces of Chinese Taoism, known as 'Qingcheng is the most secluded under heaven.' With a long history, it was once called Wenshan, Tiangu Mountain, Zhangren Mountain, Chicheng Mountain, Qingcheng Capital, and Tianguo Mountain. Here the forests are lush and evergreen through all seasons, with thousands of red stone steps and winding paths leading to seclusion, winning by quiet cleanliness—since ancient times it has enjoyed the reputation of 'Qingcheng is the most secluded under heaven.' Qingcheng's seclusion ranks with Jianmen's danger, Emei's beauty, and Kuimen's majesty. The mountain gate is gorgeous and complex; surviving ancient buildings in the mountain include Jianfu Palace, Tianshi Cave (Changdao Temple), Chaoyang Cave, Shangqing Palace, Zhenwu Palace (Zushi Hall), Yuanming Palace, Yuqing Palace, Natural Painting, Five Caves Heaven, Jiexian Bridge, Ningcui Bridge, and Shanyin Pavilion. The Qingcheng Mountain ancient building complex follows the mountain's contours without rigid form; pavilions, terraces, stone bridges, and ancient roads mostly use local materials without excessive carving, perfectly integrating with the landscape.

平武报恩寺详解 Detailed
绵阳

平武报恩寺位于绵阳市平武县，始建于明正统五年（1440年），完工于天顺四年（1460年），是中国保存最完整的明代宫殿式佛教建筑群。报恩寺占地约2.5万平方米，有天王殿、大雄宝殿、万佛阁、大悲殿、华严藏、碑亭等建筑，全部为楠木建造，不施一钉一铆。寺内有精美明代彩画、木雕、石雕，大悲殿的千手观音像高8米，华严藏的转轮经藏是中国现存最精美的转轮经藏之一。报恩寺被誉为'深山王宫'，是研究明代建筑和佛教艺术的重要实物。

Pingwu Bao'en Temple, located in Pingwu County, Mianyang, was founded in the fifth year of Zhengtong of the Ming (1440 CE) and completed in the fourth year of Tianshun (1460 CE). It is China's best-preserved Ming palace-style Buddhist building complex. Covering about 25,000 sq m, it includes the Heavenly Kings Hall, Mahavira Hall, Ten Thousand Buddha Pavilion, Great Compassion Hall, Huayan Canon Hall, and stele pavilions—all built of nanmu without a single nail or rivet. The temple contains exquisite Ming painted decorations, wood carvings, and stone carvings; the Thousand-Armed Guanyin statue in the Great Compassion Hall is 8m tall, and the revolving scripture cabinet in the Huayan Canon Hall is one of China's most exquisite surviving revolving scripture cabinets. Bao'en Temple is known as the 'Palace in the Deep Mountains,' an important artifact for studying Ming architecture and Buddhist art.

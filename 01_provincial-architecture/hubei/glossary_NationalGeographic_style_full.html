建筑的语言

探索湖北古建筑背后的术语世界——从斗拱到吊脚楼，从武当金顶到汉口租界
National Geographic Style · 90 Terms · 35 Categories
建筑是凝固的历史，而术语是解读这段历史的钥匙。本词典收录了湖北古建筑中最具代表性的90个专业术语，涵盖结构、屋顶、装饰、民居、宗教建筑等多个领域，每一条都配有中英文释义和实地例证。
公共建筑 · 1 terms
会馆
Guild Hall (Huiguan)
中国古代同乡或同业商人在异地建立的组织和建筑，集聚会、祭祀、住宿、商业活动于一体，建筑装饰往往极为精美。
Organizations and buildings established in other places by fellow townsmen or same-trade merchants in ancient China, integrating gatherings, sacrifice, accommodation, and commercial activities; the architectural decorations are often extremely exquisite.
📌 上津山陕会馆、黄州会馆、武昌会馆
历史事件 · 3 terms
永乐大修武当
Yongle Major Renovation of Wudang
明永乐十年（1412），明成祖朱棣为报答真武大帝庇佑，动用大量人力物力大修武当山，建成八宫九观等庞大建筑群，是武当山建筑史上最重要的事件。
In the 10th year of Yongle, Ming (1412), Emperor Chengzu Zhu Di, to repay the protection of the Great Emperor Zhenwu, used a large amount of manpower and material resources to carry out a major renovation of Wudang Mountain, building a庞大 building complex including eight palaces and nine temples; it is the most important event in the architectural history of Wudang Mountain.
📌 武当山八宫九观、永乐十年兴建
嘉靖重修武当
Jiajing Restoration of Wudang
明嘉靖三十一年（1552），嘉靖皇帝为崇信道教，再次大修武当山，建治世玄岳牌坊，维修八宫庙观，是武当山建筑史上第二次大规模建设。
In the 31st year of Jiajing, Ming (1552), Emperor Jiajing, due to his belief in Taoism, carried out another major renovation of Wudang Mountain, building the Zhishi Xuanyue Archway and repairing the eight palaces and temples; it is the second large-scale construction in the architectural history of Wudang Mountain.
📌 治世玄岳牌坊、嘉靖三十一年重修
汉口开埠
Hankou Opening as a Treaty Port
1861年汉口根据《天津条约》开埠，成为长江中游重要的通商口岸，随后英、俄、法、德、日等国设立租界，带来了西方近代建筑风格。
In 1861, Hankou was opened as a treaty port according to the Treaty of Tientsin, becoming an important trading port in the middle reaches of the Yangtze River; subsequently, Britain, Russia, France, Germany, Japan, and other countries established concessions, bringing Western modern architectural styles.
📌 汉口近代建筑群、江汉关大楼
历史人物 · 1 terms
鲁班
Lu Ban
春秋时期鲁国的能工巧匠，被后世尊为木匠和建筑业的祖师爷，民间有许多关于他显圣助建的传说。
A skilled craftsman of the State of Lu in the Spring and Autumn period, revered by later generations as the patriarch of carpenters and the construction industry; there are many folk legends about him manifesting his divine power to help construction.
📌 显圣殿鲁班显圣传说、湖北建筑传说
历史制度 · 1 terms
以工代赈
Work as Relief (Yigong Daizhen)
中国古代一种救灾制度，政府在灾年招募灾民从事工程建设，以工钱代替直接救济，既救济灾民又完成工程。
An ancient Chinese disaster relief system in which the government recruited disaster victims to engage in engineering construction in famine years, using wages instead of direct relief, both relieving victims and completing projects.
📌 治世玄岳牌坊以工代赈修建、明代武当山工程
古塔 · 2 terms
风水塔
Fengshui Pagoda
中国古代为调节风水、振兴文运而建的塔，多为楼阁式砖塔，常建于城镇东南方或山水交汇处，具有象征意义。
Pagodas built in ancient China to regulate fengshui and revitalize literary fortune, mostly pavilion-style brick pagodas, often built southeast of towns or at the confluence of mountains and rivers, with symbolic significance.
📌 龙山宝塔（文笔塔）、湖北文峰塔
楼阁式塔
Pavilion-Style Pagoda (Lougeshi Ta)
中国古塔中最常见的形式之一，仿木结构楼阁，分层设塔身、塔檐和平座，可登临眺望，有砖、木、石等多种结构。
One of the most common forms of ancient Chinese pagodas, imitating wooden-structure pavilions, with tower body, eaves, and balconies set in layers, climbable for overlooking, available in brick, wood, stone, and other structures.
📌 龙山宝塔、铁山寺宝塔
台基 · 1 terms
须弥座
Sumeru Pedestal (Xumizuo)
中国古建筑中一种高等级的台基形式，源于佛教须弥山的意象，由多层线脚组成，上下出涩、中间束腰，常用于宫殿、寺庙的主殿和重要建筑。
A high-grade pedestal form in traditional Chinese architecture, derived from the imagery of Mount Sumeru in Buddhism, composed of multiple moldings, with protruding upper and lower parts and a concave central waist, commonly used for main halls and important buildings of palaces and temples.
📌 榔梅祠后殿、武当山宫殿台基
墙体 · 2 terms
清水砖墙
Face Brick Wall
不抹灰、直接露出砖面的砖墙，砖缝整齐，质感朴素，是西方近代建筑和中国民国时期建筑的常见做法。
A brick wall without plastering, directly exposing the brick surface, with neat brick joints and a plain texture, a common practice in Western modern architecture and Chinese Republican-period architecture.
📌 汉口近代建筑清水砖墙、鄂西民居
抹灰墙面
Plastered Wall
表面抹灰的墙面，可涂刷各种颜色，是西方近代建筑最常见的墙面处理方式。
A wall surface with plastering, paintable in various colors, the most common wall treatment in Western modern architecture.
📌 汉口近代建筑抹灰墙面、租界建筑
宗教建筑 · 4 terms
摆手堂
Baishou Hall (Hand-Waving Hall)
土家族特有的祭祀和歌舞场所，用于祭祀祖先和跳摆手舞，是土家族文化的重要载体。
A sacrificial and song-and-dance venue unique to the Tujia people, used for ancestor worship and the hand-waving dance, serving as an important carrier of Tujia culture.
📌 恩施摆手堂、土家族村寨
金殿
Golden Hall (Jindian)
中国古代以铜铸造、表面鎏金的殿堂，是最高等级的金属建筑，武当山金殿为明代铜铸鎏金建筑的代表。
In ancient China, a hall cast in bronze and gilded on the surface, representing the highest grade of metal architecture; the Wudang Mountain Golden Hall is a representative of Ming Dynasty bronze-cast gilded architecture.
📌 武当山金顶金殿、太和宫金殿
铜殿
Bronze Hall (Tongdian)
中国古代以铜铸造的仿木构殿堂，构件在外地铸造后运至现场拼装，是金属建筑的特殊形式，武当山转运殿内的铜殿为元代铸造。
In ancient China, an imitation-wood hall cast in bronze, with components cast elsewhere and transported to the site for assembly, a special form of metal architecture; the bronze hall inside the Wudang Transport Hall was cast in the Yuan Dynasty.
📌 太和宫转运殿铜殿、武当山铜殿
天门
Heaven Gate (Tianmen)
武当山神道上的标志性建筑，象征从人间进入天界的入口，有一天门、二天门、三天门等，均为砖石拱券门。
A landmark building on the Wudang Mountain sacred path, symbolizing the entrance from the human world to the heavenly realm, including the First, Second, and Third Heaven Gates, all of which are brick-stone arched gates.
📌 一天门、二天门、三天门
宗教建筑群 · 2 terms
九观
Nine Taoist Temples (Jiu Guan)
武当山古建筑群中九座重要道观的合称，包括太玄观、元和观、复真观、回龙观、仁威观、威烈观、八仙观、龙泉观、太常观，是武当山道教建筑体系的重要组成部分。
The collective name for nine important Taoist temples in the Wudang Mountain ancient building complex, including Taixuan Temple, Yuanhe Temple, Fuzhen Temple, Huilong Temple, Renwei Temple, Weilie Temple, Baxian Temple, Longquan Temple, and Taichang Temple, serving as an important part of the Wudang Taoist architectural system.
📌 元和观、复真观
八宫
Eight Palaces (Ba Gong)
武当山古建筑群中八座重要宫殿的合称，包括净乐宫、迎恩宫、玉虚宫、遇真宫、紫霄宫、南岩宫、太和宫、五龙宫，是武当山道教建筑的核心。
The collective name for eight important palaces in the Wudang Mountain ancient building complex, including Jingle Palace, Ying'en Palace, Yuxu Palace, Yuzhen Palace, Zixiao Palace, Nanyan Palace, Taihe Palace, and Wulong Palace, serving as the core of Wudang Taoist architecture.
📌 紫霄宫、南岩宫、玉虚宫
宗教神祇 · 1 terms
真武大帝
Great Emperor Zhenwu
道教尊奉的北方之神，又称玄天上帝、玄武大帝，武当山为其道场，是武当山道教建筑的核心信仰对象。
The god of the north revered in Taoism, also called the Mysterious Heaven Emperor or the Great Emperor Xuanwu; Wudang Mountain is his sacred site, serving as the core object of belief in Wudang Taoist architecture.
📌 武当山真武大帝信仰、元和观玄帝殿
宗教艺术 · 1 terms
造像
Statue (Zaoxiang)
以石、木、铜等材料雕刻或铸造的宗教神像，是寺庙和石窟中的核心供奉对象。
Religious deity statues carved or cast in stone, wood, bronze, and other materials, serving as the core objects of worship in temples and grottoes.
📌 琼台中观石雕造像8尊、武当山神像
宫城 · 1 terms
紫禁城
Purple Forbidden City (Zijincheng)
本指北京皇宫，此处指武当山天柱峰上环绕金顶的石砌城墙，因象征天帝居所而得名，是武当山最高等级的建筑区域。
Originally referring to the Beijing imperial palace, here it refers to the stone-built city wall surrounding the Golden Summit on Tianzhu Peak of Wudang Mountain, named for symbolizing the residence of the Heavenly Emperor; it is the highest-grade architectural area of Wudang Mountain.
📌 武当山紫金城、太和宫紫金城
屋面材料 · 2 terms
琉璃瓦
Glazed Tile
中国古建筑中一种高级屋面材料，以陶土为胎，表面施铅釉烧制而成，色彩丰富（黄、绿、蓝、黑等），防水性好，光泽亮丽，用于宫殿、寺庙等高等级建筑。
A high-grade roofing material in traditional Chinese architecture, made of clay body with lead glaze fired on the surface, with rich colors (yellow, green, blue, black, etc.), good waterproofing, and bright luster, used for high-grade buildings such as palaces and temples.
📌 武当山宫殿、治世玄岳牌坊
红瓦顶
Red Tile Roof
西方近代建筑中常见的红色陶瓦屋顶，与中国传统灰瓦形成对比，汉口租界建筑中大量使用。
Red clay tile roofs common in Western modern architecture, contrasting with traditional Chinese gray tiles, extensively used in Hankou concession buildings.
📌 汉口租界红瓦顶、洞庭街建筑
屋顶形式 · 7 terms
歇山顶
Hip-and-Gable Roof (Xieshan)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，由一条正脊、四条垂脊和四条戗脊组成，上部为悬山式，下部为庑殿式，又称九脊顶，常用于宫殿、寺庙的次要建筑。
One of the traditional Chinese roof forms, consisting of one main ridge, four vertical ridges, and four diagonal ridges; the upper part is of the overhanging-gable type and the lower part of the hip type, also called the nine-ridge roof, commonly used for secondary buildings of palaces and temples.
📌 武当山太和殿、汉口近代建筑
硬山顶
Gable Roof (Yingshan)
中国古建筑最常见的屋顶形式之一，屋面仅有前后两坡，左右两侧山墙与屋面齐平或略高于屋面，结构简单，多用于民居和普通建筑。
One of the most common roof forms in traditional Chinese architecture, with only front and rear slopes; the left and right gable walls are flush with or slightly higher than the roof surface, with a simple structure, mostly used for residences and ordinary buildings.
📌 湖北民居、祠堂配殿
悬山顶
Overhanging Gable Roof (Xuanshan)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，屋面有前后两坡，且屋面伸出山墙之外，形成悬挑，有利于保护山墙免受雨水侵蚀，多用于民居。
One of the traditional Chinese roof forms, with front and rear slopes that extend beyond the gable walls, forming an overhang that helps protect the gable walls from rain erosion, mostly used for residences.
📌 太和宫铜殿、湖北乡土建筑
庑殿顶
Hip Roof (Wudian)
中国古建筑最高等级的屋顶形式，由一条正脊和四条垂脊组成，屋面有四坡，又称四阿顶，仅用于宫殿、寺庙的主殿等最高等级建筑。
The highest-grade roof form in traditional Chinese architecture, consisting of one main ridge and four vertical ridges, with four roof slopes, also called the four-eave roof, used only for the highest-grade buildings such as main halls of palaces and temples.
📌 武当山金顶
攒尖顶
Pyramidal Roof (Zanjian)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，屋面为锥形，顶部集中于一点（宝顶），无正脊，有三角、四角、六角、八角和圆形等多种形式，常用于亭、阁、塔。
One of the traditional Chinese roof forms, with a conical roof surface converging at a single point (finial) at the top, without a main ridge; available in triangular, quadrangular, hexagonal, octagonal, and circular forms, commonly used for pavilions, towers, and pagodas.
📌 饶氏庄园碉楼、武当山亭阁
卷棚顶
Curved Roof (Juanpengding)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，屋面双坡相交处不设正脊，呈弧形曲面，外观柔和，多用于园林建筑和配殿。
One of the traditional Chinese roof forms, with no main ridge at the intersection of the two roof slopes, presenting an arc-shaped curved surface with a soft appearance, mostly used for garden buildings and side halls.
📌 上津山陕会馆卷棚顶、湖北园林建筑
重檐
Double Eaves (Zhongyan)
中国古建筑中屋檐有上下两层的做法，增加建筑的高度和庄重感，用于高等级建筑的主殿。
In traditional Chinese architecture, the practice of having two layers of eaves (upper and lower), increasing the height and solemnity of the building, used for the main halls of high-grade buildings.
📌 文庙大成殿重檐歇山顶、显圣殿重檐硬山顶
建筑构件 · 19 terms
马头墙
Horse-Head Gable Wall (Matouqiang)
中国南方民居特有的山墙形式，山墙高出屋面，呈阶梯状或云形，因形似马头而得名，具有防火（封火墙）和装饰作用，常见于徽派和鄂东南民居。
A gable wall form unique to southern Chinese residences, with the gable wall rising above the roof in stepped or cloud shapes, named for its resemblance to a horse head; it has fire-prevention (fire-sealing wall) and decorative functions, commonly seen in Huizhou-style and southeastern Hubei residences.
📌 饶氏庄园云形马头墙、鄂东南民居
墀头
Chi Tou (Gable-End Ornament)
中国古建筑中硬山建筑山墙伸出檐柱之外的部分，通常有 elaborate 的砖雕装饰，是建筑立面的重要装饰部位。
In traditional Chinese architecture, the part of the gable wall of a gable building that extends beyond the eave columns, usually with elaborate brick carving decoration, serving as an important decorative element of the building facade.
📌 甘氏宗祠墀头、湖北祠堂
抱鼓石
Drum-Holding Stone (Baogushi)
中国古建筑中置于大门两侧的石雕构件，下部为须弥座，上部为圆鼓形，具有固定门枕和装饰作用，是门第等级的象征。
A stone-carved component placed on both sides of the main gate in traditional Chinese architecture, with a Sumeru pedestal at the bottom and a round drum shape at the top, serving to fix the door pillow and for decoration, symbolizing the rank of the household.
📌 甘氏宗祠抱鼓石、彭家祠堂抱鼓石
槅扇门
Lattice Door (Geshanmen)
中国古建筑中一种装饰性很强的门，由外框、格心和裙板组成，格心有各种棂花图案，常用于宫殿、寺庙、民居的正立面。
A highly decorative door in traditional Chinese architecture, composed of an outer frame, lattice core, and skirt board, with various lattice patterns on the lattice core, commonly used on the front facades of palaces, temples, and residences.
📌 文庙大成殿槅扇门窗、湖北祠堂
影壁
Screen Wall (Yingbi)
中国古建筑中位于大门内外的墙壁，用于遮挡视线和装饰，有砖雕、琉璃等多种形式，又称照壁。
A wall located inside or outside the main gate in traditional Chinese architecture, used to block the view and for decoration, available in brick carving, glazed, and other forms, also called zhaobi.
📌 元和观影壁、湖北民居影壁
八字墙
Splayed Wall (Baziqiang)
中国古建筑中大门两侧呈八字形展开的墙壁，具有扩大入口气势和引导人流的作用，常用于祠堂、庙宇和官署。
In traditional Chinese architecture, walls splayed in a figure-eight shape on both sides of the main gate, serving to enhance the imposing presence of the entrance and guide pedestrian flow, commonly used for ancestral halls, temples, and government offices.
📌 高家花屋八字墙、甘氏宗祠
鸱吻
Chiwen (Roof Ornament)
中国古建筑屋脊两端的装饰构件，形似龙首鱼身，传说能喷水灭火，具有镇火和装饰作用。
Decorative components at both ends of the roof ridge in traditional Chinese architecture, shaped like a dragon head with a fish body; legend says they can spray water to extinguish fires, serving fire-suppression and decorative functions.
📌 治世玄岳牌坊鸱吻、武当山宫殿
仙人走兽
Immortal and Beasts (Xianren Zoushou)
中国古建筑屋脊上的装饰构件，最前端为骑凤仙人，其后依次排列龙、凤、狮子、天马、海马等走兽，数量和种类反映建筑等级。
Decorative components on the roof ridges of traditional Chinese architecture, with an immortal riding a phoenix at the front, followed by beasts such as dragon, phoenix, lion, heavenly horse, and sea horse in sequence; the number and type reflect the building grade.
📌 显圣殿戗脊仙人走兽、三闾书院屋脊
拱券门
Arched Gate (Gongquanmen)
以砖石砌筑的弧形门洞，结构稳固，跨度较大，常用于城门、塔门和重要建筑入口。
An arc-shaped gate opening built of brick and stone, with a stable structure and large span, commonly used for city gates, pagoda gates, and important building entrances.
📌 武当山天门拱券门、上津古城门
线脚
Molding (Xianjiao)
建筑表面的凹凸线条装饰，用于台基、柱础、额枋等部位，增加层次感和装饰性。
Concave-convex line decorations on building surfaces, used for pedestals, column bases, architraves, and other parts, increasing layering and decorativeness.
📌 须弥座线脚、湖北古建筑
石柱础
Stone Column Base
柱子底部的石质基座，用于防潮和传递荷载，表面常有雕刻装饰，是中国古建筑的重要构件。
A stone base at the bottom of a column, used for moisture protection and load transfer, often with carved decorations on the surface, serving as an important component of traditional Chinese architecture.
📌 湖北古建筑石柱础、祠堂柱础
门槛
Threshold (Menkan)
大门下方的横木或石条，具有分隔空间、挡风和象征意义，中国传统建筑中门槛高低反映门第等级。
A horizontal wood or stone bar below the main gate, serving to separate spaces, block wind, and for symbolic meaning; in traditional Chinese architecture, the height of the threshold reflects the rank of the household.
📌 湖北民居门槛、祠堂大门
门枕石
Door Pillow Stone
大门两侧承托门轴的石质构件，外侧常做成抱鼓石或石狮形式，具有固定和装饰作用。
Stone components on both sides of the main gate supporting the door pivot, with the outer side often made into drum-holding stone or stone lion forms, serving fixing and decorative functions.
📌 甘氏宗祠门枕石、湖北祠堂
门钉
Door Nails (Mending)
大门上的金属帽钉，既有加固门板的作用，也有装饰和等级象征意义，皇宫和高等级建筑中门钉数量有严格规定。
Metal cap nails on the main gate, serving both to reinforce the door panel and for decoration and rank symbolism; in imperial palaces and high-grade buildings, the number of door nails is strictly regulated.
📌 汉口近代建筑门钉、湖北古建筑
铺首
Door Knocker (Pushou)
大门上的金属门环底座，常做成兽面或环形，用于叩门和装饰，中国传统建筑中常见。
A metal door-ring base on the main gate, often made into animal-face or ring shapes, used for knocking and decoration, commonly seen in traditional Chinese architecture.
📌 湖北民居铺首、祠堂大门
匾额

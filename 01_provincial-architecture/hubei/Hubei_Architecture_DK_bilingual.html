武汉市 (21 sites)

武汉，湖北省会，有'九省通衢'之称，是长江中游的中心城市。武汉建筑融合了荆楚传统和近代西洋风格，黄鹤楼是江南三大名楼之一，归元寺是湖北首刹，武昌起义军政府旧址是辛亥革命的见证。武汉还是近代建筑的博物馆，汉口近代建筑群保存了大量西洋风格建筑。

Wuhan, the capital of Hubei, known as the 'thoroughfare of nine provinces,' is the central city of the middle Yangtze. Wuhan architecture blends Jingchu tradition and modern Western styles; the Yellow Crane Tower is one of the three famous towers south of the Yangtze, Guiyuan Temple is Hubei's number one monastery, and the Wuchang Uprising Military Government site witnesses the 1911 Revolution. Wuhan is also a museum of modern architecture, with the Hankou modern building complex preserving numerous Western-style buildings.

黄鹤楼详解 Detailed
武昌区

黄鹤楼位于武汉市武昌蛇山，始建于三国吴黄武二年（223年），是中国江南三大名楼之一，有'天下江山第一楼'之誉。黄鹤楼历史上屡毁屡建，现存建筑为1985年重建，高51.4米，五层，攒尖顶，层层飞檐，造型雄伟。黄鹤楼是武汉的城市标志，历代文人墨客在此留下了大量诗篇，崔颢的《黄鹤楼》最为著名。

The Yellow Crane Tower, located on Snake Hill in Wuchang, Wuhan, was founded in the second year of Huangwu of Eastern Wu (223 CE). One of China's three famous towers south of the Yangtze, it is known as the 'Number One Tower Under Heaven.' Destroyed and rebuilt repeatedly throughout history, the current building was reconstructed in 1985, 51.4m high with five stories, a pyramidal roof, and layered flying eaves in a majestic form. The Yellow Crane Tower is Wuhan's city symbol, with numerous poems by literati throughout history, most famously Cui Hao's 'Yellow Crane Tower.'

归元寺详解 Detailed
汉阳区

归元寺位于武汉市汉阳区，始建于清顺治十五年（1658年），是武汉四大佛教丛林之一，有'湖北首刹'之誉。寺院占地约4.7万平方米，有藏经阁、大雄宝殿、罗汉堂、观音阁等建筑，以罗汉堂的500尊泥塑罗汉最为著名。归元寺建筑为典型的清代寺院格局，融合了湖北地方建筑特色，是武汉重要的佛教文化中心。

Guiyuan Temple, located in Hanyang District, Wuhan, was founded in the 15th year of Shunzhi of the Qing (1658 CE). One of Wuhan's four major Buddhist monasteries, it is known as the 'Number One Monastery of Hubei.' Covering about 47,000 sq m with a Sutra Library, Mahavira Hall, Arhat Hall, and Guanyin Pavilion, it is most famous for the 500 clay arhat statues in the Arhat Hall. Guiyuan Temple follows a typical Qing monastery layout, blending Hubei local architectural features—an important Buddhist cultural center in Wuhan.

古琴台详解 Detailed
汉阳区

古琴台位于武汉市汉阳区，始建于北宋，是为纪念春秋时期俞伯牙与钟子期'高山流水遇知音'的故事而建。古琴台有琴台碑廊、伯牙台、知音树等建筑，碑廊内有历代文人题写的碑刻。古琴台是武汉著名的文化古迹，'知音'文化的发源地，与黄鹤楼、晴川阁并称武汉三大名胜。

Guqin Terrace, located in Hanyang District, Wuhan, was founded in the Northern Song, built to commemorate the story of Yu Boya and Zhong Ziqi's 'high mountains and flowing water—meeting a kindred spirit' in the Spring and Autumn period. Guqin Terrace includes the stele corridor, Boya Terrace, and Kindred Spirit Tree, with stele inscriptions by literati throughout history in the corridor. Guqin Terrace is a famous cultural relic in Wuhan, the birthplace of 'kindred spirit' culture, ranked with the Yellow Crane Tower and Qingchuan Pavilion as Wuhan's three famous scenic spots.

晴川阁详解 Detailed
汉阳区

晴川阁位于武汉市汉阳区龟山东麓，始建于明嘉靖年间，取唐代崔颢《黄鹤楼》诗'晴川历历汉阳树'之意命名。晴川阁与黄鹤楼隔江相望，有'楚国晴川第一楼'之誉。现存建筑为1983年重建，有晴川阁、禹稷行宫、铁门关等建筑，是武汉长江沿岸的重要景观。

Qingchuan Pavilion, located at the eastern foot of Turtle Hill in Hanyang District, Wuhan, was founded in the Jiajing era of the Ming, named after the line 'Clear river, clearly the Hanyang trees' from Cui Hao's Tang poem 'Yellow Crane Tower.' Qingchuan Pavilion faces the Yellow Crane Tower across the river, known as the 'Number One Pavilion of Chu's Clear River.' The existing building was reconstructed in 1983, including Qingchuan Pavilion, Yuji Palace, and Iron Gate Pass—an important landscape along the Yangtze River in Wuhan.

盘龙城遗址详解 Detailed
黄陂区

盘龙城遗址位于武汉市黄陂区，是商代早期的城址，距今约3500年，是长江流域发现的第一座商代古城。遗址面积约1.1平方公里，发现有城垣、宫殿基址、墓葬、作坊等，出土有大量青铜器、玉器、陶器。盘龙城遗址是研究商代南方文化和中原文化交流的重要遗址，2001年被评为'中国20世纪100项考古大发现'之一。

The Panlongcheng Site, located in Huangpi District, Wuhan, is an early Shang dynasty city site dating back about 3,500 years, the first Shang dynasty ancient city discovered in the Yangtze River basin. Covering about 1.1 sq km, the site contains city walls, palace foundations, tombs, and workshops, yielding numerous bronze, jade, and pottery artifacts. The Panlongcheng Site is important for studying the exchange between Shang dynasty southern culture and Central Plains culture, selected as one of the '100 Major Archaeological Discoveries of 20th Century China' in 2001.

问津书院详解 Detailed
新洲区

问津书院位于武汉市新洲区，始建于西汉，是中国最古老的书院之一，因孔子'使子路问津'的典故而得名。问津书院有大成殿、讲堂、仲子祠、隐士祠等建筑，是宋明理学的重要传播地。书院历经沧桑，现存建筑为清代重建，是研究中国古代教育制度和儒家文化的重要实物。

Wenjin Academy, located in Xinzhou District, Wuhan, was founded in the Western Han, one of China's oldest academies, named after the allusion of Confucius 'sending Zilu to ask about the ford.' Wenjin Academy includes the Dacheng Hall, lecture hall, Zhongzi Shrine, and Hermit Shrine, an important dissemination center for Song-Ming Neo-Confucianism. Having undergone vicissitudes, the existing building was rebuilt in the Qing, important for studying ancient Chinese educational institutions and Confucian culture.

武当山金顶详解 Detailed
丹江口市

武当山金顶位于武当山天柱峰顶端，建于明永乐十四年（1416年），为铜铸鎏金重檐庑殿顶，高5.54米，宽5.8米，深4.2米，重达数十吨，是中国现存最大的铜铸建筑。金顶的铜铸构件在武汉铸造后运至武当山组装，殿内供奉真武大帝铜像，是武当山古建筑群的核心和最高点。金顶1994年随武当山古建筑群列入世界文化遗产。

The Golden Summit of Wudang Mountain, located at the top of Tianzhu Peak, was built in the 14th year of Yongle of the Ming (1416 CE). It is a bronze-gilded double-eave hip roof, 5.54m high, 5.8m wide, 4.2m deep, weighing dozens of tons—China's largest surviving bronze-cast building. The bronze components were cast in Wuhan and transported to Wudang Mountain for assembly. Inside the hall enshrines a bronze statue of the Perfect Warrior (Zhenwu), the core and highest point of the Wudang Mountain building complex. Listed as UNESCO World Heritage with the Wudang Mountain complex in 1994.

武当山紫霄宫详解 Detailed
丹江口市

紫霄宫位于武当山展旗峰下，建于明永乐十一年（1413年），是武当山保存最完整的宫殿之一。紫霄宫占地约8万平方米，有龙虎殿、碑亭、十方堂、紫霄殿、父母殿等建筑。紫霄殿为重檐歇山顶，面阔五间，进深五间，殿内有真武大帝塑像和明代御赐法器。紫霄宫是武当山道教活动的中心，1994年列入世界文化遗产。

Zixiao Palace, located under Zhanqi Peak of Wudang Mountain, was built in the 11th year of Yongle of the Ming (1413 CE). It is one of the best-preserved palaces on Wudang Mountain. Covering about 80,000 sq m, it includes the Dragon-Tiger Hall, stele pavilions, Shifang Hall, Zixiao Hall, and Parents Hall. The Zixiao Hall has a double-eave hip-and-gable roof, five bays wide and five deep, with a statue of the Perfect Warrior and Ming imperial-bestowed ritual instruments inside. Zixiao Palace is the center of Taoist activities on Wudang Mountain, listed as UNESCO World Heritage in 1994.

武当山南岩宫详解 Detailed
丹江口市

南岩宫位于武当山独阳岩下，始建于元至元二十二年（1285年），明永乐年间扩建，是武当山三十六岩中风景最美的一岩。南岩宫依山而建，有南岩石殿、天乙真庆宫石殿、两仪殿、皇经堂等建筑。天乙真庆宫为石砌仿木结构，殿内有'太子卧龙床'等精美石雕。南岩宫的'龙头香'悬挑于绝壁之上，是武当山最惊险的景观。

Nanyan Palace, located under Duyang Rock of Wudang Mountain, was founded in the 22nd year of Zhiyuan of the Yuan (1285 CE) and expanded during the Yongle era of the Ming. It is the most scenic of Wudang's 36 rocks. Built along the cliff, it includes the Nanyan Stone Hall, Tianyi Zhenqing Palace Stone Hall, Liangyi Hall, and Huangjing Hall. The Tianyi Zhenqing Palace is stone-built imitating wooden structure, with exquisite stone carvings such as the 'Crown Prince Sleeping on Dragon Bed.' The 'Dragon Head Incense' of Nanyan Palace cantilevers over the precipice, the most thrilling landscape on Wudang Mountain.

武当山太子坡详解 Detailed
丹江口市

太子坡又名复真观，位于武当山狮子峰下，建于明永乐十二年（1414年），相传是真武大帝入山修炼初期的居所。太子坡依山而建，有照壁、梵津桥、龙虎殿、大殿、藏经楼、五云楼等建筑。五云楼为五层木结构，是武当山最高的木构建筑，楼内有'一柱十二梁'的奇特结构。太子坡的建筑布局巧妙利用地形，是武当山建筑的典范。

Taizipo, also known as Fuzhen Temple, is located under Lion Peak of Wudang Mountain, built in the 12th year of Yongle of the Ming (1414 CE). According to legend, it was the residence of the Perfect Warrior in his early cultivation period. Built along the hillside, it includes a screen wall, Fanjin Bridge, Dragon-Tiger Hall, main hall, sutra library, and Wuyun Tower. The Wuyun Tower is a five-story wooden structure, the tallest wooden building on Wudang Mountain, with the peculiar 'one column, twelve beams' structure inside. Taizipo's layout cleverly uses the terrain, a model of Wudang Mountain architecture.

武当山玉虚宫

The Chibi Ancient Battlefield, located on the south bank of the Yangtze River northwest of Chibi City, is the site of the Battle of Chibi in the 13th year of Jian'an of the Eastern Han (208 CE). The Battle of Chibi is a famous outnumbered-victory battle in Chinese history, laying the foundation for the Three Kingdoms tripartite division. The site contains the Chibi Cliff Inscription, Yijiang Pavilion, Wangjiang Pavilion, Zhou Yu statue, and Fengchu Nunnery. The two characters 'Chibi' on the cliff inscription are traditionally attributed to Zhou Yu, an important relic of Three Kingdoms culture.

羊楼洞古镇详解 Detailed

Yangloudong Ancient Town, located in Chibi City, was a famous tea distribution center in the Ming-Qing, known as the 'Hometown of Brick Tea.' The ancient town preserves Ming-Qing streets, shops, residences, and temples, with winding stone-paved roads and ancient bridges. Yangloudong was one of the important starting points of the Jin merchants' Ten-Thousand-Li Tea Road, with tea transported to Russia and Europe. The town architecture blends southern Hubei residences and Shanxi merchant firm styles, important for studying the history of Chinese tea trade.

曾侯乙墓遗址详解 Detailed
曾都区

曾侯乙墓位于随州市，是战国早期曾国国君乙的墓葬，1978年发掘，出土文物15000余件，其中以编钟最为著名。曾侯乙编钟共65件，铸造于约公元前433年，音域宽广，音色优美，是迄今发现的最完整、最大的一套编钟，体现了中国古代青铜铸造和声学的高超水平。曾侯乙墓是楚文化的重要遗址，对研究战国时期的历史文化具有重要价值。

The Tomb of Marquis Yi of Zeng, located in Suizhou, is the tomb of Yi, ruler of the Zeng state in the early Warring States period, excavated in 1978, yielding over 15,000 artifacts, of which the bianzhong (bell chimes) are the most famous. The Zeng Hou Yi bianzhong consists of 65 bells, cast around 433 BCE, with a wide range and beautiful tone—the most complete and largest set ever discovered, reflecting the high level of ancient Chinese bronze casting and acoustics. The Tomb of Marquis Yi is an important Chu cultural site, of great value for studying Warring States history and culture.

彭家寨详解 Detailed

The site covers about 740,000 sq m, preserving city walls, gates, streets, government offices, memorial arches, tombs, and temples. Tangya Tusi City was listed as UNESCO World Heritage in 2015 as part of the 'Tusi Sites of China,' an important site for studying China's tusi system and Tujia history and culture.

江南：中国古典建筑的精华之地

江苏和上海位于长江下游，是江南文化的核心区域。这里保存了中国最精致的古典园林、最古老的水乡古镇、最完整的明清民居，以及融合中西的近代建筑。苏州9座古典园林被列入世界文化遗产，南京明孝陵开创了明清帝陵制度，大运河贯穿南北孕育了无数水乡古镇。

从南京的六朝烟水气到苏州的园林雅致，从扬州的盐商奢华到上海的海派风情，江苏上海古建筑展现了江南文化的丰富层次。本图集收录319处重要古建筑，覆盖14个城市，是了解江南建筑文化的全景导览。

Jiangsu and Shanghai, located in the lower Yangtze River, are the core of Jiangnan culture. They preserve China's most exquisite classical gardens, oldest water towns, most complete Ming-Qing residences, and East-West blended modern architecture. Nine Suzhou gardens are UNESCO World Heritage Sites, Nanjing's Ming Xiaoling pioneered the Ming-Qing imperial mausoleum system, and the Grand Canal nurtured countless water towns. From Nanjing's Six Dynasties atmosphere to Suzhou's garden elegance, from Yangzhou's salt merchant luxury to Shanghai's haipai style, the architecture of Jiangsu and Shanghai reveals the rich layers of Jiangnan culture. This atlas covers 319 important ancient buildings across 14 cities.

1明孝陵
玄武区
明孝陵位于南京市玄武区紫金山南麓，是明太祖朱元璋与马皇后的合葬陵墓，始建于明洪武十四年（1381年），次年马皇后入葬，洪武三十一年（1398年）朱元璋入葬。陵园占地面积170余万平方米，是中国现存规模最大的帝王陵寝之一。明孝陵开创了明清帝陵'前方后圆'的规制，神道蜿蜒曲折，两侧排列石象、石骆驼、石麒麟、石马、石武将、石文臣等石像生18对。2003年作为'明清皇家陵寝'扩展项目列入世界文化遗产名录。
Ming Xiaoling, located at the southern foot of Purple Mountain in Xuanwu District, Nanjing, is the joint mausoleum of the Hongwu Emperor (founder of the Ming dynasty) and Empress Ma. Construction began in 1381; Empress Ma was interred in 1382 and the emperor in 1398. Covering over 1.7 million square meters, it is one of China's largest imperial mausoleums. Ming Xiaoling pioneered the 'square front, round rear' layout of Ming-Qing imperial tombs, with a winding spirit way flanked by 18 pairs of stone statues including elephants, camels, qilin, horses, military officers, and civil officials. Listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2003 as an extension of the 'Imperial Tombs of the Ming and Qing Dynasties'.
2中山陵
玄武区
中山陵位于南京市玄武区紫金山南麓，是中国民主革命先行者孙中山先生的陵墓，1926年奠基，1929年建成。陵园由建筑师吕彦直设计，占地面积8万余平方米，主要建筑有牌坊、墓道、陵门、碑亭、祭堂、墓室等，沿中轴线排列，共392级台阶。祭堂为仿宫殿式建筑，融合中西建筑风格，堂内有孙中山先生汉白玉坐像。中山陵是中国近代建筑史上的杰作，被誉为'中国近代建筑史上的第一陵'。
Zhongshan Ling (Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum), located at the southern foot of Purple Mountain in Xuanwu District, Nanjing, is the mausoleum of Sun Yat-sen, the pioneer of China's democratic revolution. The foundation was laid in 1926 and completed in 1929. Designed by architect Lü Yanzhi, it covers over 80,000 square meters with main buildings including a memorial arch, tomb path, mausoleum gate, stele pavilion, sacrificial hall, and burial chamber arranged along a central axis with 392 steps. The sacrificial hall is a palace-style structure blending Chinese and Western architectural styles, containing a white marble seated statue of Sun Yat-sen. Zhongshan Ling is a masterpiece of modern Chinese architecture, known as the 'foremost mausoleum in modern Chinese architectural history'.
3总统府
玄武区
总统府位于南京市玄武区长江路，始建于明洪武年间，清代为两江总督署，太平天国时期为天王府，1912年孙中山在此就任中华民国临时大总统，1927年后为国民政府总统府。建筑群占地面积9万平方米，由中区、西区、东区组成，融合了中国古典园林、晚清官式建筑和西方近代建筑风格。主要建筑有大堂、二堂、子超楼、煦园、西花园等。总统府是中国近代史的重要见证，现为中国近代史遗址博物馆。
The Presidential Palace, located on Changjiang Road in Xuanwu District, Nanjing, was first built in the Hongwu era of the Ming dynasty. It served as the Viceroy of Liangjiang's yamen in the Qing dynasty, the Heavenly King's Palace during the Taiping Rebellion, the site where Sun Yat-sen took office as Provisional President of the Republic of China in 1912, and the Nationalist government's Presidential Palace after 1927. Covering 90,000 square meters, the complex comprises central, western, and eastern zones, blending classical Chinese gardens, late-Qing official architecture, and modern Western styles. Main buildings include the Great Hall, Second Hall, Zichao Tower, Xuyuan Garden, and West Garden. The Presidential Palace is an important witness of modern Chinese history, now the Museum of Modern Chinese History Sites.
4夫子庙
秦淮区
夫子庙位于南京市秦淮区秦淮河北岸，始建于北宋景祐元年（1034年），是祭祀孔子的文庙，也是江南地区的文化教育中心。夫子庙建筑群由孔庙、学宫、贡院三部分组成，现建筑为1984年后复建。主要建筑有照壁、泮池、牌坊、大成门、大成殿、明德堂等。夫子庙地区是南京著名的文化商业区，秦淮河畔的灯彩、小吃、画舫构成了独特的秦淮风光。
Fuzimiao (Confucius Temple), located on the northern bank of the Qinhuai River in Qinhuai District, Nanjing, was founded in 1034 (Northern Song Jingyou 1). It is a Confucian temple and was the cultural and educational center of the Jiangnan region. The complex comprises the Confucian Temple, Academy, and Examination Hall, with current buildings reconstructed after 1984. Main structures include the screen wall, pan pond, memorial arch, Dacheng Gate, Dacheng Hall, and Mingde Hall. The Fuzimiao area is Nanjing's famous cultural and commercial district, with lanterns, snacks, and painted boats along the Qinhuai River creating the unique Qinhuai scenery.
5栖霞寺
栖霞区
栖霞寺位于南京市栖霞区栖霞山中峰西麓，始建于南齐永明七年（489年），是中国四大名刹之一，佛教三论宗的祖庭。寺院占地面积40余亩，主要建筑有山门、弥勒殿、毗卢殿、藏经楼、鉴真纪念堂等。寺后有舍利塔，为五代南唐时期的石塔，八角五层，高18米，雕刻精美，是中国古代石塔艺术的精品。寺旁还有千佛岩石窟，开凿于南齐至明代，共有佛像500余尊。
Qixia Temple, located on the western slope of the middle peak of Qixia Mountain in Qixia District, Nanjing, was founded in 489 (Southern Qi Yongming 7). It is one of China's Four Great Buddhist Temples and the ancestral temple of the Sanlun Buddhist sect. Covering over 40 mu, main buildings include the gate, Maitreya Hall, Vairocana Hall, Scripture Library, and Jianzhen Memorial Hall. Behind the temple stands the Stupa, a five-story octagonal stone pagoda 18 meters tall from the Southern Tang period of the Five Dynasties, with exquisite carvings—a fine example of ancient Chinese stone pagoda art. Nearby are the Qianfo Rock Grottoes, carved from the Southern Qi to Ming dynasties, containing over 500 Buddhist statues.
6鸡鸣寺
玄武区
鸡鸣寺位于南京市玄武区鸡笼山东麓，始建于西晋永康元年（300年），是南京最古老的梵刹之一，有'南朝第一寺'之称。寺院依山而建，主要建筑有山门、天王殿、大雄宝殿、观音楼、豁蒙楼、景阳楼等。寺内有胭脂井，相传为陈后主与张丽华、孔贵妃藏身之处。鸡鸣寺的樱花道是南京著名的赏樱胜地，每年春季吸引大量游客。
Jiming Temple, located on the eastern slope of Jiming Mountain in Xuanwu District, Nanjing, was founded in 300 (Western Jin Yongkang 1). It is one of Nanjing's oldest Buddhist temples, known as the 'Foremost Temple of the Southern Dynasties'. Built on a hillside, main buildings include the gate, Heavenly Kings Hall, Mahavira Hall, Guanyin Tower, Huomeng Tower, and Jingyang Tower. The temple contains the Rouge Well, where according to legend, Chen Houzhu (last emperor of the Chen dynasty) hid with his concubines. The cherry blossom avenue at Jiming Temple is a famous cherry blossom viewing spot in Nanjing, attracting many visitors each spring.

8朝天宫
秦淮区
朝天宫位于南京市秦淮区冶山，始建于明洪武十七年（1384年），是明代百官演习朝贺礼仪的场所，清代改为江宁府学和文庙。建筑群占地面积约7万平方米，是江南地区现存规模最大、保存最完整的古建筑群之一。主要建筑有照壁、泮池、棂星门、大成门、大成殿、崇圣殿、敬一亭等。朝天宫现为南京市博物馆，馆藏文物丰富。
Chaotian Palace, located on Yeshan in Qinhuai District, Nanjing, was founded in 1384 (Ming Hongwu 17). It was where Ming dynasty officials practiced court ceremonies, and in the Qing dynasty became the Jiangning Prefecture School and Confucian Temple. Covering about 70,000 square meters, it is one of the largest and best-preserved ancient building complexes in the Jiangnan region. Main buildings include the screen wall, pan pond, Lingxing Gate, Dacheng Gate, Dacheng Hall, Chongsheng Hall, and Jingyi Pavilion. Chaotian Palace is now the Nanjing Museum with rich collections.

10南京明城墙
秦淮区
南京明城墙始建于元至正二十六年（1366年），明洪武二十六年（1393年）完工，全长35.2公里，是世界上现存最长的砖石结构城墙。城墙以花岗石为基，巨砖为墙，以糯米汁夹灰灌浆，极为坚固。原有城门13座，现存中华门、神策门、清凉门等。中华门瓮城是中国现存最大的瓮城，有三道瓮城、四道拱门，可藏兵3000人。南京明城墙是中国古代城市防御工程的杰出代表。
The Nanjing Ming City Wall was begun in 1366 (Yuan Zhizheng 26) and completed in 1393 (Ming Hongwu 26). With a total length of 35.2 km, it is the world's longest surviving brick-stone city wall. Built on granite foundations with large bricks and mortar made of glutinous rice juice and lime, it is extremely solid. Originally with 13 gates, surviving gates include Zhonghua Gate, Shence Gate, and Qingliang Gate. The Zhonghua Gate barbican is China's largest surviving barbican, with three enclosures, four arch gates, and capacity for 3,000 soldiers. The Nanjing Ming City Wall is an outstanding example of ancient Chinese urban defense engineering.
11中华门瓮城
秦淮区
中华门瓮城位于南京市秦淮区，是南京明城墙的正南门，始建于明洪武年间，原名聚宝门，1931年改今名。瓮城为长方形，东西宽118.5米，南北深129米，总面积15168平方米。有三道瓮城、四道拱门，每道拱门均有千斤闸。城墙上下设有27个藏兵洞，可藏兵3000人。中华门瓮城是中国现存规模最大、保存最完整的瓮城，是研究古代城防建筑的重要实物。
The Zhonghua Gate Barbican, located in Qinhuai District, Nanjing, is the south gate of the Nanjing Ming City Wall. Built in the Hongwu era of the Ming dynasty, originally named Jubao Gate, it was renamed in 1931. The rectangular barbican is 118.5 meters wide east-west and 129 meters deep north-south, covering 15,168 square meters. It has three enclosures, four arch gates each with a portcullis, and 27 soldier-hiding caves capable of sheltering 3,000 troops. The Zhonghua Gate Barbican is China's largest and best-preserved barbican, an important artifact for studying ancient urban defense architecture.
12瞻园
秦淮区
瞻园位于南京市秦淮区，始建于明嘉靖年间，为明代开国功臣徐达的府邸西花园，清代为江南行省左布政使署，太平天国时期为东王杨秀清王府和夏官副丞相赖汉英衙署。园林占地面积约2万平方米，以山池为主景，有静妙堂、翼然亭、花篮厅、观鱼亭等建筑。瞻园是南京现存历史最久的明代古典园林，与上海豫园、苏州拙政园、留园并称'江南四大名园'。
Zhan Garden, located in Qinhuai District, Nanjing, was first built in the Jiajing era of the Ming dynasty as the west garden of the residence of Xu Da, a founding general of the Ming. In the Qing dynasty it served as the Left Provincial Administration Commissioner's office, and during the Taiping Rebellion as the palace of East King Yang Xiuqing and the office of Lai Hanying. Covering about 20,000 square meters, the garden centers on mountains and ponds, with buildings including Jingmiao Hall, Yiran Pavilion, Hualan Hall, and Fish Viewing Pavilion. Zhan Garden is Nanjing's oldest surviving Ming classical garden, ranked with Shanghai's Yu Garden, Suzhou's Humble Administrator's Garden, and Lingering Garden as one of the 'Four Great Gardens of Jiangnan'.

1拙政园
姑苏区
拙政园位于苏州市姑苏区，始建于明正德四年（1509年），为明代御史王献臣弃官回乡后所建，取晋代潘岳《闲居赋》中'拙者之为政也'之意命名。园林占地面积约5.2万平方米，是苏州最大的古典园林，分为东、中、西三部分。中部为全园精华，以远香堂为主体，临水而建，周围有小飞虹、香洲、荷风四面亭、见山楼等建筑。拙政园是中国四大名园之一，1997年列入世界文化遗产名录。
Humble Administrator's Garden (Zhuozheng Yuan), located in Gusu District, Suzhou, was founded in 1509 (Ming Zhengde 4) by Wang Xianchen, a Ming censor who returned home after leaving office, named after a phrase from Pan Yue's 'Ode to Leisure' meaning 'the clumsy one governs'. Covering about 52,000 square meters, it is Suzhou's largest classical garden, divided into eastern, central, and western sections. The central section is the essence, with the Yuanxiang Hall as the main structure built by the water, surrounded by the Xiaofei Rainbow Bridge, Xiangzhou Boat, Lotus Wind Pavilion, and Jianshan Tower. One of China's Four Great Gardens, it was listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.
2留园
姑苏区
留园位于苏州市姑苏区，始建于明万历二十一年（1593年），为太仆寺少卿徐泰时的私家园林，清代嘉庆年间刘恕改建后名'寒碧山庄'，俗称'刘园'，光绪年间盛康购得后改名'留园'。园林占地面积约2.3万平方米，分为中、东、西、北四部分。中部以山水见长，东部以建筑取胜，冠云峰为江南园林中最高的太湖石，高6.5米。留园是中国四大名园之一，1997年列入世界文化遗产名录。
Lingering Garden (Liu Yuan), located in Gusu District, Suzhou, was founded in 1593 (Ming Wanli 21) as the private garden of Xu Taishi, a vice minister of the Court of the Imperial Stud. In the Qing Jiaqing era, Liu Shu rebuilt it and named it 'Hanbi Villa', commonly called 'Liu Garden'; after Sheng Kang purchased it in the Guangxu era, it was renamed 'Lingering Garden'. Covering about 23,000 square meters, it is divided into central, eastern, western, and northern sections. The central section excels in landscape, the eastern in architecture. The Guanyun Peak, at 6.5 meters tall, is the highest Taihu stone in Jiangnan gardens. One of China's Four Great Gardens, it was listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.
3网师园
姑苏区
网师园位于苏州市姑苏区，始建于南宋淳熙年间（1174-1189年），为侍郎史正志的'万卷堂'故址，清代乾隆年间宋宗元重建，取'渔隐'之意名'网师园'。园林占地面积约5400平方米，是苏州中型园林的代表，以精巧紧凑著称。主要建筑有万卷堂、撷秀楼、殿春簃、月到风来亭、看松读画轩等。网师园的殿春簃是美国纽约大都会博物馆'明轩'的蓝本。1997年列入世界文化遗产名录。
Master of Nets Garden (Wangshi Yuan), located in Gusu District, Suzhou, was founded in the Chunxi era (1174-1189) of the Southern Song as the site of Vice Minister Shi Zhengzhi's 'Wanjuan Hall'. Rebuilt by Song Zongyuan in the Qing Qianlong era, it was named 'Master of Nets Garden' meaning 'hermit fisherman'. Covering about 5,400 square meters, it is a representative medium-sized Suzhou garden, renowned for its exquisite compactness. Main buildings include Wanjuan Hall, Xiexiu Tower, Dianchun Yi Pavilion, Moon and Wind Pavilion, and Pine Viewing and Painting Reading Studio. The Dianchun Yi was the model for the 'Ming Hall' at the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York. Listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.
4环秀山庄
姑苏区
环秀山庄位于苏州市姑苏区，始建于五代，清代乾隆年间为刑部员外郎蒋楫宅园，后归大学士孙士毅，嘉庆年间戈裕良在此堆叠假山。园林占地面积约2180平方米，以假山闻名，戈裕良所叠假山占地仅半亩，却有60余米山径，峭壁、峰峦、洞壑、涧谷、悬崖、磴道俱全，被誉为'江南园林假山之冠'。1997年列入世界文化遗产名录。
Mountain Villa with Embracing Beauty (Huanxiu Shanzhuang), located in Gusu District, Suzhou, was founded in the Five Dynasties period. In the Qing Qianlong era it was the garden of Jiang Ji, a vice director of the Ministry of Justice, later belonging to Grand Secretary Sun Shiyi. In the Jiaqing era, rockery master Ge Yuliang stacked the garden's famous rockery. Covering about 2,180 square meters, the garden is renowned for its rockery—Ge Yuliang's rockery occupies only half a mu but features over 60 meters of mountain paths, cliffs, peaks, caves, ravines, and stone steps, hailed as the 'finest rockery in Jiangnan gardens'. Listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.
5沧浪亭
姑苏区
沧浪亭位于苏州市姑苏区，始建于北宋庆历五年（1045年），为诗人苏舜钦的私家园林，是苏州现存最古老的园林。园林占地面积约1.1万平方米，以水景见长，园外有河，园内有山，山水相映。主要建筑有沧浪亭、明道堂、看山楼、翠玲珑馆、御碑亭等。沧浪亭的108式漏窗最为著名，图案各异，是江南园林漏窗艺术的精华。1997年列入世界文化遗产名录。
Canglang Pavilion (Canglang Ting), located in Gusu District, Suzhou, was founded in 1045 (Northern Song Qingli 5) as the private garden of poet Su Shunqin. It is Suzhou's oldest surviving garden. Covering about 11,000 square meters, it excels in water scenery with a river outside and mountains inside, creating beautiful reflections. Main buildings include the Canglang Pavilion, Mingdao Hall, Mountain Viewing Tower, Cuilinglong Hall, and Imperial Stele Pavilion. The garden's 108 lattice window patterns are most famous, each different, representing the essence of Jiangnan lattice window art. Listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.
6狮子林
姑苏区
狮子林位于苏州市姑苏区，始建于元至正二年（1342年），为僧惟则为纪念其师中峰禅师而建，因园内太湖石形似狮子而得名。园林占地面积约1.1万平方米，以假山迷宫著称，假山占地约0.15公顷，有洞壑21个、曲径9条，人行其中如入迷宫。主要建筑有燕誉堂、指柏轩、问梅阁、真趣亭等。清乾隆帝六下江南，五次到访狮子林，并在圆明园、承德避暑山庄仿建。1997年列入世界文化遗产名录。
Lion Grove Garden (Shizi Lin), located in Gusu District, Suzhou, was founded in 1342 (Yuan Zhizheng 2) by monk Weize to honor his teacher Zhongfeng Chan, named for the lion-shaped Taihu stones in the garden. Covering about 11,000 square meters, it is renowned for its rockery maze—the rockery covers about 0.15 hectares with 21 caves and 9 winding paths, like entering a labyrinth. Main buildings include Yanyu Hall, Zhibai Xuan, Wenmei Pavilion, and Zhenqu Pavilion. Emperor Qianlong visited five times during his six southern tours and had replicas built in the Old Summer Palace and Chengde Mountain Resort. Listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.

10虎丘云岩寺塔
姑苏区
虎丘云岩寺塔位于苏州市姑苏区虎丘山，始建于五代后周显德六年（959年），北宋建隆二年（961年）建成，是一座八角七层楼阁式砖塔，高47.7米。塔为仿木结构，塔身有砖雕斗拱、门窗等装饰。由于地基原因，塔身向北倾斜，最大倾角2.48米，被誉为'中国的比萨斜塔'。虎丘塔是苏州的城市标志，塔内曾发现五代至北宋时期的珍贵文物，包括越窑青瓷莲花碗、檀木经箱等。
The Yunyan Temple Pagoda at Tiger Hill, located on Tiger Hill in Gusu District, Suzhou, was begun in 959 (Later Zhou Xiande 6) and completed in 961 (Northern Song Jianlong 2). It is an octagonal seven-story pavilion-style brick pagoda 47.7 meters tall. Imitating wooden structure, the pagoda features brick-carved bracket sets, doors, and windows. Due to foundation issues, the pagoda leans north with a maximum inclination of 2.48 meters, known as 'China's Leaning Tower of Pisa'. The Tiger Hill Pagoda is Suzhou's city symbol; precious artifacts from the Five Dynasties to Northern Song were discovered inside, including a Yue celadon lotus bowl and sandalwood scripture boxes.

Hanshan Temple, located in Gusu District, Suzhou, was founded in the Tianjian era (502-519) of the Liang dynasty, originally named 'Miaoli Puming Pagoda Courtyard'. It was renamed in the Tang dynasty after the poet-monk Hanshan lived there. Hanshan Temple is world-famous for Tang poet Zhang Ji's poem 'Night Mooring at Maple Bridge': 'Outside Suzhou, Hanshan Temple rings its midnight bell, reaching my guest boat.' Main buildings include the gate, Mahavira Hall, Scripture Library, Bell Tower, and Fengjiang Tower. The temple's bell tower hosts a New Year's Eve bell-listening event every year, attracting many Chinese and foreign visitors.

21周庄古镇
昆山市
周庄古镇位于苏州市昆山市，始建于北宋元祐元年（1086年），因周迪功郎舍宅为寺而得名，是江南六大古镇之一。古镇面积0.47平方公里，河道呈'井'字形，有古桥14座，其中双桥（世德桥、永安桥）最为著名，画家陈逸飞的《故乡的回忆》使周庄名扬世界。古镇保存有近百座明清民居，主要景点有沈厅、张厅、迷楼、叶楚伧故居等。周庄被誉为'中国第一水乡'。
Zhouzhuang Ancient Town, located in Kunshan, Suzhou, was founded in 1086 (Northern Song Yuanyou 1), named after Zhou Digong who donated his residence for a temple. It is one of the Six Great Water Towns of Jiangnan. Covering 0.47 square kilometers, its waterways form a 'well' shape with 14 ancient bridges, of which the Twin Bridges (Shide and Yong'an) are most famous—painter Chen Yifei's 'Memory of Hometown' made Zhouzhuang world-famous. The town preserves nearly 100 Ming-Qing residences, with main attractions including Shen Hall, Zhang Hall, Milou Tower, and Ye Chucang's Former Residence. Zhouzhuang is known as 'China's Foremost Water Town'.
22同里古镇
吴江区
同里古镇位于苏州市吴江区，始建于宋代，是江南六大古镇之一，因'富土'二字拆为'同里'而得名。古镇面积0.87平方公里，被15条河道分割为7个小岛，由49座古桥相连。主要景点有退思园、崇本堂、嘉荫堂、三桥（太平桥、吉利桥、长庆桥）等。退思园建于清光绪十一年（1885年），为任兰生的私家园林，2000年作为苏州园林扩展项目列入世界文化遗产名录。
Tongli Ancient Town, located in Wujiang District, Suzhou, was founded in the Song dynasty. It is one of the Six Great Water Towns of Jiangnan, its name derived from splitting the characters for 'rich land' into 'tongli'. Covering 0.87 square kilometers, the town is divided into 7 small islands by 15 waterways, connected by 49 ancient bridges. Main attractions include the Garden of Seclusion (Tuisi Yuan), Chongben Hall, Jiayin Hall, and the Three Bridges (Taiping, Jili, Changqing). The Garden of Seclusion, built in 1885 (Qing Guangxu 11) as Ren Lansheng's private garden, was listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2000 as an extension of the Suzhou gardens.

1寄畅园
梁溪区
寄畅园位于无锡市梁溪区惠山横街，始建于明正德年间（1506-1521年），为南京兵部尚书秦金的私家园林，原名'凤谷行窝'，清代秦德藻扩建后改名'寄畅园'。园林占地面积约1公顷，以借景惠山著称，园内有锦汇漪、知鱼槛、七星桥、含贞斋等建筑。清乾隆帝六下江南，每次都到访寄畅园，并在北京颐和园仿建'谐趣园'。寄畅园是江南山麓园林的代表。
Jichang Garden, located on Huishan Heng Street in Liangxi District, Wuxi, was founded in the Zhengde era (1506-1521) of the Ming as the private garden of Qin Jin, Minister of War of Nanjing, originally named 'Fenggu Xingwo'. After expansion by Qin Dezao in the Qing, it was renamed 'Jichang Garden'. Covering about 1 hectare, it is renowned for borrowing the scenery of Mount Hui, with features including the Jinhuiyi Pond, Fish-Knowing Railing, Seven-Star Bridge, and Hanzhen Studio. Emperor Qianlong visited on each of his six southern tours and had a replica 'Xiequ Garden' built in the Summer Palace in Beijing. Jichang Garden is representative of Jiangnan hillside gardens.
2薛福成故居
梁溪区
薛福成故居位于无锡市梁溪区，始建于清光绪十六年（1890年），为清末著名外交家、思想家薛福成的宅第，光绪帝御笔亲题'钦使第'。故居占地面积约2.1万平方米，有房屋160余间，分为中、东、西三条轴线。建筑融合了中国传统与西方近代风格，转盘楼是江南地区最大的转盘楼。薛福成故居是研究中国近代建筑和社会变迁的重要实物，2001年被列为全国重点文物保护单位。
Xue Fucheng's Former Residence, located in Liangxi District, Wuxi, was founded in 1890 (Qing Guangxu 16). It was the residence of Xue Fucheng, a famous late-Qing diplomat and thinker, with the emperor's inscription 'Qinshi Di' (Envoy's Residence). Covering about 21,000 square meters with over 160 rooms, it is divided into central, eastern, and western axes. Blending traditional Chinese with modern Western styles, its revolving staircase tower is the largest in Jiangnan. The residence is an important artifact for studying modern Chinese architecture and social change, listed as a National Key Cultural Relics Protection Unit in 2001.
3东林书院
梁溪区
东林书院位于无锡市梁溪区，始建于北宋政和元年（1111年），为理学家杨时讲学之所，明代万历三十二年（1604年）顾宪成、高攀龙等重建并在此讲学，形成著名的'东林党'。书院主要建筑有石牌坊、泮池、东林精舍、丽泽堂、依庸堂、燕居庙等。依庸堂内有顾宪成名联'风声雨声读书声声声入耳，家事国事天下事事事关心'。东林书院是中国古代书院建筑的重要实例。
Donglin Academy, located in Liangxi District, Wuxi, was founded in 1111 (Northern Song Zhenghe 1) as a lecture place for Neo-Confucian Yang Shi. In 1604 (Ming Wanli 32), Gu Xiancheng, Gao Panlong, and others rebuilt it and lectured here, forming the famous 'Donglin Party'. Main buildings include the stone arch, pan pond, Donglin Jingshe, Lize Hall, Yiyong Hall, and Yanju Temple. The Yiyong Hall contains Gu Xiancheng's famous couplet: 'The sounds of wind, rain, and reading—each enters my ears; the affairs of family, state, and world—each concerns my heart.' Donglin Academy is an important example of ancient Chinese academy architecture.

Guangjiao Temple on Wolf Mountain, located on Wolf Mountain in Chongchuan District, Nantong, was founded in 669 (Tang Zongzhang 2). It is the main temple on Wolf Mountain, one of China's Eight Lesser Famous Buddhist Mountains. The temple is divided into upper and lower sections: the upper has the Mahavira Hall, Scripture Library, and Dasheng Hall; the lower has the Vajra Hall and Wheel Repository Hall. Wolf Mountain is the bodhimandala of Mahasthamaprapta Bodhisattva, holding an important position in Buddhism. With beautiful scenery, Wolf Mountain is Nantong's famous scenic area.

崇川区

Changzhou Tianning Temple, located in Tianning District, Changzhou, was founded in the Yonghui era (650-655) of the Tang. It is one of the Four Great Zen Buddhist Monasteries in China, known as the 'Foremost Monastery in the Southeast'. Main buildings include the gate, Heavenly Kings Hall, Mahavira Hall, Vajra Hall, Manjushri Hall, Samantabhadra Hall, and Scripture Library. The Mahavira Hall, seven bays wide with a double-eave hip-and-gable roof, is one of the largest Buddhist halls in Jiangsu. Beside the temple stands the Tianning Pagoda, completed in 2007 at 153.79 meters tall—one of the world's tallest Buddhist pagodas.

青龙寺是江苏上海地区重要的古建筑之一，具有较高的历史、艺术和科学价值。建筑体现了江南地区的传统工艺和地方特色，是研究该地区建筑发展史的重要实物资料。
青龙寺 is one of the important ancient buildings in the Jiangsu-Shanghai region, with high historical, artistic, and scientific value. The architecture embodies traditional Jiangnan craftsmanship and local characteristics, serving as important material for studying the architectural development of the region.

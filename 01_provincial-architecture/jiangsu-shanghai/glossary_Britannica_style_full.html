交通建筑Transportation Architecture
[080]码头
Wharf (Matou)
中国古代和近代江河海边供船只停靠、装卸货物和旅客上下的设施，是水运交通的节点。
Facilities along rivers, lakes and seas in ancient and modern China for ships to dock, load and unload cargo, and passengers to embark and disembark, nodes of water transportation.
分类 Category: 交通建筑 / Transportation Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 龙江宝船厂遗址、京杭大运河
[081]桥梁
Bridge (Qiaoliang)
中国古代跨越河流沟壑的建筑，有梁桥、拱桥、索桥、浮桥等多种形式，是古代交通工程的杰出成就。
Buildings spanning rivers and gullies in ancient China, with various forms including beam bridges, arch bridges, suspension bridges and pontoon bridges, outstanding achievements of ancient transportation engineering.
分类 Category: 交通建筑 / Transportation Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 七桥瓮、赵州桥、宝带桥
公共建筑Public Architecture
[069]会馆
Guild Hall (Huiguan)
中国古代旅居异地的同乡或同行设立的机构建筑，供集会、议事、祭祀、住宿之用，明清时期商业发达地区尤为兴盛。
Institutional buildings established in ancient China by fellow townsmen or colleagues living away from home, for gatherings, discussions, worship and lodging, especially flourishing in commercially developed areas during the Ming and Qing.
分类 Category: 公共建筑 / Public Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 三山会馆、全晋会馆、安徽会馆
[070]戏台
Opera Stage (Xitai)
中国古建筑中供戏曲演出的建筑，常设于寺庙、会馆、园林中，有固定的形制，装饰华丽，是中国传统戏曲文化的物质载体。
Buildings in traditional Chinese architecture for opera performances, often set in temples, guild halls and gardens, with a fixed form and luxurious decoration, the material carrier of traditional Chinese opera culture.
分类 Category: 公共建筑 / Public Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 三山会馆戏台、全晋会馆戏台、常州古戏楼群
[071]驿站
Post Station (Yizhan)
中国古代供传递公文的人或往来官员中途休息、换马的处所，是古代交通和通信体系的重要组成部分。
Places in ancient China for people transmitting official documents or traveling officials to rest and change horses midway, an important part of the ancient transportation and communication system.
分类 Category: 公共建筑 / Public Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 盂城驿、高邮当铺
[072]当铺
Pawnshop (Dangpu)
中国古代收取衣物等动产作质押向放贷者放款的机构建筑，多为高墙深院，有坚固的库房。
Institutional buildings in ancient China that lent money to lenders by taking movable property such as clothing as collateral, mostly high-walled deep courtyards with sturdy warehouses.
分类 Category: 公共建筑 / Public Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 高邮当铺
园林艺术Garden Art
[030]借景
Borrowed Scenery (Jiejing)
中国古典园林的重要造园手法，将园外的景色引入园内，扩大园林空间感，《园冶》将其列为造园诸法之首。
An important garden-making technique in classical Chinese gardens, introducing scenery outside the garden into the garden to expand the sense of space, listed by Yuanye (The Craft of Gardens) as the foremost of all garden-making methods.
分类 Category: 园林艺术 / Garden Art
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 拙政园、留园、寄畅园
[031]对景
Opposite Scenery (Duijing)
中国古典园林造园手法，在园林轴线或视线的两端设置景物，互相呼应，形成视觉焦点。
A garden-making technique in classical Chinese gardens, where scenery is set at both ends of a garden axis or sightline, echoing each other to form a visual focus.
分类 Category: 园林艺术 / Garden Art
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 个园、何园、秋霞圃
[032]框景
Framed Scenery (Kuangjing)
中国古典园林造园手法，利用门框、窗框、洞门等将园景框入其中，形成一幅立体的画。
A garden-making technique in classical Chinese gardens, using door frames, window frames and cave doors to frame garden scenery, forming a three-dimensional painting.
分类 Category: 园林艺术 / Garden Art
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 拙政园、留园、网师园
[033]障景
Obstructed Scenery (Zhangjing)
中国古典园林造园手法，在园林入口或视线前方设置假山、屏风、植物等遮挡景物，达到先抑后扬的效果。
A garden-making technique in classical Chinese gardens, where rockeries, screens and plants are set at the garden entrance or in front of the sightline to obstruct scenery, achieving the effect of first suppressing then releasing.
分类 Category: 园林艺术 / Garden Art
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 拙政园、留园、个园
[034]假山
Artificial Rockery (Jiashan)
中国古典园林中用天然石块堆叠而成的山，是园林的骨架，有土山、石山、土石山之分，追求透、漏、瘦、皱的审美标准。
Mountains piled from natural stones in classical Chinese gardens, the skeleton of the garden, divided into earth mountains, stone mountains and earth-stone mountains, pursuing the aesthetic standards of transparency, perforation, slenderness and wrinkling.
分类 Category: 园林艺术 / Garden Art
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 个园四季假山、留园冠云峰、环秀山庄、秋霞圃
[035]太湖石
Taihu Stone
中国古典园林中最常用的观赏石，产于太湖流域，经湖水长期侵蚀形成玲珑剔透的形态，以瘦、皱、漏、透为审美标准。
The most commonly used ornamental stone in classical Chinese gardens, produced in the Taihu Lake basin, formed into exquisite and perforated shapes by long-term lake water erosion, with slenderness, wrinkling, perforation and transparency as aesthetic standards.
分类 Category: 园林艺术 / Garden Art
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 留园冠云峰、豫园玉玲珑、苏州园林
[036]花街铺地
Patterned Paving (Huajie Pudi)
中国古典园林中用碎石、瓦片、瓷片等材料铺设成各种图案的地面，是江南园林的特色之一。
Ground paving in classical Chinese gardens using crushed stone, tiles and porcelain pieces to form various patterns, one of the features of Jiangnan gardens.
分类 Category: 园林艺术 / Garden Art
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 苏州园林、扬州园林、豫园
[037]旱院水做
Dry Courtyard with Water Illusion
中国古典园林造园手法，在无水的庭院中用铺地、石矶等手法模拟水景，创造出水的意境。
A garden-making technique in classical Chinese gardens, using paving and stone islets in a waterless courtyard to simulate water scenery, creating the artistic conception of water.
分类 Category: 园林艺术 / Garden Art
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 秋霞圃凝霞阁前小院
地方流派Regional Schools
[056]苏州园林
Suzhou Gardens
中国古典园林的代表流派，以私家园林为主，始于春秋，发展于唐宋，鼎盛于明清，以精致典雅、小中见大著称，拙政园、留园、网师园等为代表。
The representative school of classical Chinese gardens, dominated by private gardens, beginning in the Spring and Autumn period, developing in the Tang and Song, and reaching its peak in the Ming and Qing, known for refinement and elegance and achieving grandeur within small spaces, represented by the Humble Administrator's Garden, Lingering Garden and Master-of-Nets Garden.
分类 Category: 地方流派 / Regional Schools
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 拙政园、留园、网师园、环秀山庄
[057]扬州园林
Yangzhou Gardens
中国古典园林的重要流派，以盐商园林为特色，风格豪放大度，叠石艺术高超，个园、何园、小盘谷等为代表。
An important school of classical Chinese gardens, characterized by salt merchant gardens, with a bold and generous style and superb rock-piling art, represented by Ge Garden, He Garden and Xiaopangu.
分类 Category: 地方流派 / Regional Schools
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 个园、何园、小盘谷、卢氏盐商住宅
[058]金陵四十八景
Forty-Eight Scenes of Jinling
南京（金陵）地区的传统风景名胜集，始于明代，清代定型，涵盖南京地区的山水、古迹、园林等景观。
The collection of traditional scenic spots in Nanjing (Jinling), beginning in the Ming and finalized in the Qing, covering the mountains, waters, historical sites and gardens of the Nanjing area.
分类 Category: 地方流派 / Regional Schools
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 栖霞寺、明孝陵、夫子庙
[059]沪城八景
Eight Scenes of Shanghai City
上海地区的传统风景名胜集，明清时期形成，包括黄浦秋涛、龙华晚钟、江皋霁雪、吴淞烟雨等景观。
The collection of traditional scenic spots in the Shanghai area, formed during the Ming and Qing, including scenes such as Huangpu Autumn Waves, Longhua Evening Bell, River Bank Snow Clearing and Wusong Misty Rain.
分类 Category: 地方流派 / Regional Schools
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 大境阁江皋霁雪、龙华寺龙华晚钟
[060]吴门画派
Wu School of Painting
明代中期活跃于苏州（吴门）地区的绘画流派，以沈周、文徵明、唐寅、仇英为代表，主张文人画传统，对江南园林艺术影响深远。
A painting school active in the Suzhou (Wu) area in the mid-Ming, represented by Shen Zhou, Wen Zhengming, Tang Yin and Qiu Ying, advocating the literati painting tradition, with profound influence on Jiangnan garden art.
分类 Category: 地方流派 / Regional Schools
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 苏州园林、文徵明手植紫藤
[061]松江画派
Songjiang School of Painting
明代后期活跃于松江（今上海松江）地区的绘画流派，以董其昌为代表，主张南北宗论，对中国绘画史影响深远。
A painting school active in the Songjiang (now Songjiang, Shanghai) area in the late Ming, represented by Dong Qichang, advocating the Northern and Southern Schools theory, with profound influence on the history of Chinese painting.
分类 Category: 地方流派 / Regional Schools
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 醉白池董其昌雕像、松江方塔园
城市规划Urban Planning
[090]大上海计划
Greater Shanghai Plan
1929年国民政府上海市政府提出的城市建设计划，旨在绕开租界，在上海东北部建设新的市中心，包括市政府大楼、图书馆、博物馆、体育场等建筑。
An urban construction plan proposed by the Nationalist government's Shanghai Municipal Government in 1929, aiming to bypass the concessions and build a new city center in northeastern Shanghai, including the municipal government building, library, museum and stadium.
分类 Category: 城市规划 / Urban Planning
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 旧上海特别市政府、旧上海市图书馆、旧上海市博物馆、江湾体育场
塔幢建筑Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
[019]楼阁式塔
Pavilion-Style Pagoda
中国佛塔的主要类型之一，仿照中国传统楼阁建筑形式，每层有塔身、塔檐、平座，可登临，是中国古塔中数量最多的类型。
One of the main types of Chinese Buddhist pagodas, imitating the form of traditional Chinese pavilion buildings, with each story having a pagoda body, eave and flat seat, climbable, and the most numerous type of ancient Chinese pagodas.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 龙华塔、法华塔、兴圣教寺塔、海清寺塔、文通塔
[020]密檐式塔
Multi-Eaved Pagoda
中国佛塔的主要类型之一，第一层塔身较高，以上各层塔檐紧密重叠，多为实心，不可登临，常见于北方地区。
One of the main types of Chinese Buddhist pagodas, with a tall first story and closely overlapping eaves above, mostly solid and not climbable, commonly found in northern China.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 南京南朝陵墓石刻附近
[021]塔刹
Pagoda Finial (Tasha)
塔顶部的装饰性构件，由基座、覆钵、相轮、宝盖、宝珠等组成，是佛塔的标志性元素，象征佛的涅槃。
The decorative component at the top of a pagoda, consisting of a base, alms bowl, wheel discs, canopy and jewel, the iconic element of Buddhist pagodas, symbolizing the Buddha's nirvana.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 龙华塔、法华塔、兴圣教寺塔
[022]相轮
Wheel Disc (Xianglun)
塔刹的组成部分，为多重圆环，套在刹杆上，是佛塔的重要装饰，相轮的数量代表塔的等级。
A component of the pagoda finial, consisting of multiple circular rings fitted on the finial pole, an important decoration of Buddhist pagodas, with the number of wheel discs representing the pagoda's rank.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 龙华塔、兴圣教寺塔
[023]壶门
Pot Door (Humen)
塔、石窟、碑刻等建筑中常见的门形装饰，轮廓为尖拱形，形似壶（古代盛器），常用于塔身壁面。
A door-shaped decoration commonly found in pagodas, grottoes and steles, with a pointed arch outline resembling an ancient pot (hu), commonly used on pagoda body walls.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 龙华塔、兴圣教寺塔、南翔寺砖塔
[024]腰檐
Waist Eave (Yaoyan)
楼阁式塔各层之间的短檐，形似腰带，是楼阁式塔的标志性特征，使塔身呈现明显的分层。
The short eaves between stories of pavilion-style pagodas, resembling a waist belt, the iconic feature of pavilion-style pagodas, giving the pagoda body distinct layering.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 龙华塔、法华塔、兴圣教寺塔
[025]平座
Flat Seat (Pingzuo)
楼阁式塔各层塔檐上方的平台，由斗拱承托，外围有栏杆，可供登临眺望。
The platform above each eave of pavilion-style pagodas, supported by bracket sets, with railings around the perimeter, for climbing and viewing.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 龙华塔、兴圣教寺塔
[026]地宫
Underground Palace (Digong)
佛塔塔基下的地下空间，用于埋藏舍利、佛经、宝物等，是佛塔的重要组成部分，考古价值极高。
The underground space beneath the foundation of a Buddhist pagoda, used to bury relics, sutras and treasures, an important component of Buddhist pagodas with high archaeological value.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 法华塔、兴圣教寺塔、阿育王寺
[027]经幢
Sutra Pillar (Jingchuang)
中国古代佛教石刻建筑，由基座、幢身、幢顶三部分组成，幢身镌刻佛经（多为《佛顶尊胜陀罗尼经》），盛行于唐代，是佛教建筑的独特形式。
Ancient Chinese Buddhist stone-carved architecture, consisting of a base, pillar body and pillar top, with the pillar body engraved with Buddhist sutras (mostly the Buddha's Top Victorious Dharani Sutra), prevalent in the Tang dynasty, a unique form of Buddhist architecture.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 松江唐经幢、南翔古猗园唐经幢、太平兴国教寺大殿
[028]须弥座
Sumeru Base (Xumizuo)
中国古建筑中高级建筑的台基形式，由多层线脚组成，中间束腰，形似佛教传说中的须弥山，常用于宫殿、寺庙、塔幢等建筑。
A high-grade platform form in traditional Chinese architecture, consisting of multiple layers of moldings with a waist in the middle, resembling Mount Sumeru in Buddhist legend, commonly used for palaces, temples, pagodas and pillars.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 松江唐经幢、南京明故宫遗址
[029]叠涩
Corbel (Dieshe)
砖石结构中常见的砌筑方式，将砖石逐层向外挑出，形成支撑或装饰，常用于塔檐、经幢等建筑。
A common masonry method in brick-stone structures, where bricks or stones are projected outward layer by layer to form support or decoration, commonly used for pagoda eaves and sutra pillars.
分类 Category: 塔幢建筑 / Pagoda & Pillar Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 龙华塔、南翔寺砖塔、松江唐经幢
宗教建筑Religious Architecture
[083]清真寺
Mosque (Qingzhensi)
伊斯兰教的宗教建筑，中国清真寺多采用中国传统建筑形式，有礼拜殿、邦克楼、水房等组成部分。
Religious buildings of Islam; Chinese mosques mostly adopt traditional Chinese architectural forms, consisting of prayer halls, minarets and ablution rooms.
分类 Category: 宗教建筑 / Religious Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 净觉寺、仙鹤寺、山巷清真寺
[084]天主教堂
Catholic Church
天主教的宗教建筑，近代传入中国后多采用西方哥特式或罗马式建筑风格，有钟楼、玫瑰花窗、彩色玻璃等元素。
Religious buildings of Catholicism; after being introduced to China in modern times, they mostly adopt Western Gothic or Romanesque architectural styles, with elements such as bell towers, rose windows and stained glass.
分类 Category: 宗教建筑 / Religious Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 徐家汇天主教堂、耶稣圣心堂
[085]基督教堂
Protestant Church
基督教新教的宗教建筑，近代传入中国，风格多样，有哥特式、罗马式、现代式等。
Religious buildings of Protestantism, introduced to China in modern times, with diverse styles including Gothic, Romanesque and modern.
分类 Category: 宗教建筑 / Religious Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 沐恩堂、国际礼拜堂
[086]道观
Taoist Temple (Daoguan)
道教的宗教建筑，多采用中国传统宫殿式建筑布局，有山门、灵官殿、三清殿、玉皇殿等组成部分。
Religious buildings of Taoism, mostly adopting traditional Chinese palace-style layout, consisting of mountain gate, Lingguan Hall, Sanqing Hall and Jade Emperor Hall.
分类 Category: 宗教建筑 / Religious Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 大境道观、玄妙观
[087]石窟
Grotto (Shiku)
在山崖上开凿的佛教建筑，内有佛像、壁画，是佛教艺术的重要形式，始于魏晋，盛于北朝至隋唐。
Buddhist buildings carved into cliffs, with Buddha statues and murals inside, an important form of Buddhist art, beginning in the Wei-Jin period and flourishing from the Northern Dynasties to the Sui-Tang.
分类 Category: 宗教建筑 / Religious Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 千佛崖石窟、孔望山摩崖造像
屋顶形式Roof Forms
[001]歇山顶
Hip-and-Gable Roof (Xieshan)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，又称九脊顶，由一条正脊、四条垂脊和四条戗脊组成，上半部为悬山式，下半部为庑殿式，常用于宫殿、寺庙、园林建筑中等级较高的建筑。
One of the traditional Chinese architectural roof forms, also called the nine-ridge roof, consisting of one main ridge, four vertical ridges and four diagonal ridges; the upper half is of the overhanging gable type and the lower half of the hip type, commonly used for higher-ranking buildings in palaces, temples and gardens.
分类 Category: 屋顶形式 / Roof Forms
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 朝天宫、灵谷寺无梁殿、真如寺大殿、龙华寺大雄宝殿
[002]庑殿顶
Hip Roof (Wudian)
中国古建筑最高等级的屋顶形式，又称四阿顶，由一条正脊和四条垂脊组成，四面坡，常用于宫殿最高等级建筑，如北京故宫太和殿。
The highest-ranking traditional Chinese roof form, also called the four-slope roof, consisting of one main ridge and four vertical ridges with four sloping sides, commonly used for the highest-ranking palace buildings such as the Hall of Supreme Harmony in the Forbidden City.
分类 Category: 屋顶形式 / Roof Forms

Art Deco
20世纪20-30年代流行的建筑风格，强调几何图案、竖向线条、阶梯状收分和金属装饰，上海是世界上Art Deco建筑最集中的城市之一。
An architectural style popular in the 1920s-30s, emphasizing geometric patterns, vertical lines, stepped setbacks and metal decoration; Shanghai is one of the cities with the highest concentration of Art Deco buildings in the world.
分类 Category: 近代建筑 / Modern Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 沙逊大厦、国际饭店、百老汇大厦、中国银行大楼
[047]折衷主义
Eclecticism
19世纪西方建筑风格，任意模仿历史上各种风格并自由组合，不追求固定的法式，上海跑马总会等建筑采用此风格。
A 19th-century Western architectural style that freely imitates and combines various historical styles without pursuing a fixed formula; buildings such as the Shanghai Race Club adopt this style.
分类 Category: 近代建筑 / Modern Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 跑马总会、江海关大楼
[048]外廊式
Veranda Style
近代殖民建筑风格，建筑外围设连续外廊，适应热带亚热带气候，是上海早期租界建筑的常见形式。
A modern colonial architectural style with continuous verandas around the building, adapted to tropical and subtropical climates, a common form of early concession buildings in Shanghai.
分类 Category: 近代建筑 / Modern Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 英国驻沪领事馆、南洋公学中院
[049]哥特复兴
Gothic Revival
19世纪复兴中世纪哥特式建筑的风格，采用尖拱、花窗玻璃、尖塔等元素，常用于教堂建筑。
A 19th-century style reviving medieval Gothic architecture, using pointed arches, stained glass windows and spires, commonly used for church buildings.
分类 Category: 近代建筑 / Modern Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 徐家汇天主教堂、沐恩堂
[050]钢框架结构
Steel Frame Structure
近代高层建筑的结构形式，用钢材制作梁柱框架，墙体只起围护作用，使建筑可以向高空发展，上海20世纪30年代高层建筑多采用此结构。
A structural form of modern high-rise buildings, using steel for beam-column frames, with walls only serving as enclosure, allowing buildings to develop upward; many Shanghai high-rises of the 1930s adopt this structure.
分类 Category: 近代建筑 / Modern Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 沙逊大厦、国际饭店、中国银行大楼
防御建筑Defensive Architecture
[076]城墙
City Wall (Chengqiang)
中国古代城市的防御设施，由墙体、城门、城楼、角楼、瓮城、护城河等组成，是城市的重要标志。
Defensive facilities of ancient Chinese cities, consisting of walls, gates, gate towers, corner towers, barbicans and moats, an important symbol of the city.
分类 Category: 防御建筑 / Defensive Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 南京城墙、上海古城墙、高邮城墙
[077]瓮城
Barbican (Wengcheng)
中国古代城门外的防御设施，为半圆形或方形的小城，与主城墙相连，可对攻入之敌形成瓮中捉鳖之势。
Defensive facilities outside ancient Chinese city gates, small semicircular or square towns connected to the main city wall, allowing the enemy who breaks in to be caught like a turtle in a jar.
分类 Category: 防御建筑 / Defensive Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 南京城墙、中华门
[078]护城河
Moat (Huchenghe)
中国古代城市城墙外的人工河道，起防御和运输作用，是城市防御体系的重要组成部分。
Artificial waterways outside ancient Chinese city walls, serving defense and transportation functions, an important part of the urban defense system.
分类 Category: 防御建筑 / Defensive Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 南京护城河、上海古城墙城壕
[079]炮台
Fort (Paotai)
中国古代和近代在江海要隘设置的火炮防御工事，是海防和江防的重要设施。
Artillery defensive works set at strategic river and sea passes in ancient and modern China, important facilities for coastal and river defense.
分类 Category: 防御建筑 / Defensive Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 黄山炮台旧址、江阴要塞
陵墓建筑Tomb Architecture
[066]陵墓石刻
Tomb Stone Carvings
古代陵墓前的石人、石兽等石刻，起象征墓主身份和守护陵墓的作用，南朝陵墓石刻是中国石刻艺术的高峰。
Stone figures and animals in front of ancient tombs, serving to symbolize the tomb owner's status and guard the tomb; the Southern Dynasties tomb stone carvings represent a peak of Chinese stone carving art.
分类 Category: 陵墓建筑 / Tomb Architecture
代表建筑 Representative Examples: 南京南朝陵墓石刻、丹阳南朝陵墓石刻、明祖陵

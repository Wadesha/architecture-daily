福州市

福州市 (30 sites)

福州，福建省会，有'榕城'之称，建城2200余年。福州建筑以马鞍墙、马鞍脊为标志，三坊七巷是中国现存最完整的历史文化街区之一。华林寺大殿为中国南方最早木构建筑之一。福州还是脱胎漆器、寿山石雕的发源地。

Fuzhou, the capital of Fujian, known as the 'Banyan City,' has over 2,200 years of city history. Fuzhou architecture is marked by saddle walls and saddle ridges; Three Lanes and Seven Alleys is one of China's most complete historical districts. Hualin Temple Main Hall is one of the earliest wooden structures in southern China. Fuzhou is also the birthplace of bodiless lacquerware and Shoushan stone carving.

三坊七巷详解 Detailed
鼓楼区

三坊七巷位于福州市中心，是中国现存规模最大、保存最完整的历史文化街区之一，始建于西晋，唐代形成格局，明清时期达到鼎盛。街区由三个坊（衣锦坊、文儒坊、光禄坊）和七条巷（杨桥巷、郎官巷、塔巷、黄巷、安民巷、宫巷、吉庇巷）组成，占地面积约38公顷，保存有明清古建筑200余座。建筑以马鞍墙、马鞍脊为标志，白墙青瓦，曲线优美，是福州民居的典型代表。三坊七巷走出了林则徐、严复、沈葆桢、林觉民、冰心等众多历史名人，被誉为'中国城市里坊制度的活化石'和'近代中国名人的摇篮'。

Three Lanes and Seven Alleys (Sanfang Qixiang), located in central Fuzhou, is one of China's largest and best-preserved historical and cultural districts. Founded in the Western Jin, its layout took shape in the Tang and flourished in the Ming-Qing. Comprising three lanes (Yijin, Wenru, Guanglu) and seven alleys (Yangqiao, Langguan, Ta, Huang, Anmin, Gong, Jibi), it covers about 38 hectares with over 200 Ming-Qing ancient buildings. Marked by saddle walls and saddle ridges with white walls and dark tiles in graceful curves, it represents the quintessential Fuzhou residence. The district produced Lin Zexu, Yan Fu, Shen Baozhen, Lin Juemin, Bing Xin and many other historical figures, known as the 'living fossil of China's urban ward system' and 'cradle of modern Chinese celebrities.'

华林寺详解 Detailed
鼓楼区

华林寺位于福州市鼓楼区，始建于北宋乾德二年（964年），是中国南方现存最早的木构建筑之一。大殿面阔三间，进深四间，单檐歇山顶，斗拱硕大，出檐深远，保留了宋代建筑的典型特征。华林寺大殿的斗拱采用'单抄三下昂'做法，梁架结构简洁，是研究中国古代建筑从唐到宋演变的重要实物。1982年被列为全国重点文物保护单位。

Hualin Temple, located in Gulou District, Fuzhou, was founded in the second year of Qiande of the Northern Song (964 CE), making it one of the earliest surviving wooden structures in southern China. The main hall is three bays wide and four bays deep, with a single-eave hip-and-gable roof, massive bracket sets, and deep overhanging eaves, preserving typical Song dynasty features. Its bracket sets use the 'single bracket arm, three descending ang' technique, with a simple beam structure—an important artifact for studying the evolution of ancient Chinese architecture from Tang to Song. Listed as a National Key Cultural Relic in 1982.

宏琳厝详解 Detailed
闽清县

宏琳厝位于福州市闽清县坂东镇，建于清乾隆六十年（1795年），是福州地区规模最大的古民居，有'民间故宫'之誉。建筑占地约1.7万平方米，有大小厅堂35间、住房666间，为三进一花园布局。宏琳厝以马鞍墙为标志，白墙青瓦，布局严谨，是福州民居的典范。建筑内保存有大量木雕、石雕和楹联，体现了福州传统工艺的水平。

Honglin Cuo, located in Bandong Town, Minqing County, Fuzhou, was built in the 60th year of Qianlong of the Qing (1795 CE). It is the largest ancient residence in the Fuzhou region, known as the 'folk Forbidden City.' Covering about 17,000 sq m with 35 halls of various sizes and 666 rooms, in a three-court-one-garden layout. Marked by saddle walls with white walls and dark tiles in a rigorous layout, it is a model of Fuzhou residences. The building preserves numerous wood carvings, stone carvings, and couplets, reflecting the level of traditional Fuzhou craftsmanship.

厦门市 (14 sites)

厦门，闽南经济中心，有'鹭岛'之称。厦门建筑融合了闽南传统与近代西洋风格，鼓浪屿是近代万国建筑博览会，2017年列入世界文化遗产。南普陀寺是闽南佛教圣地，集美学村的嘉庚建筑是中西合璧的典范。

鼓浪屿详解 Detailed
思明区

鼓浪屿位于厦门岛西南，面积1.87平方公里，是近代万国建筑博览会。岛上保存有1000多栋近代建筑，包括英式、法式、德式、日式、闽南式等多种风格，融合了中西建筑文化。鼓浪屿原为渔村，鸦片战争后成为公共租界，各国领事馆、洋行、教会、华侨别墅纷纷兴建。2017年被列入世界文化遗产名录。著名建筑包括八卦楼、黄家花园、菽庄花园、海天堂构等。

Gulangyu Island, located southwest of Xiamen Island with an area of 1.87 sq km, is a modern international architecture expo. The island preserves over 1,000 modern buildings in British, French, German, Japanese, and Minnan styles, blending Chinese and Western architectural culture. Originally a fishing village, Gulangyu became a public concession after the Opium War, with consulates, foreign firms, churches, and overseas Chinese villas built in succession. Listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2017. Famous buildings include the Bagua Tower, Huang Family Garden, Shuzhuang Garden, and Haitian Tangou.

南普陀寺详解 Detailed
思明区

南普陀寺位于厦门五老峰下，始建于唐代，是闽南佛教圣地。寺庙依山而建，中轴线依次为天王殿、大雄宝殿、大悲殿、藏经阁，两侧有钟鼓楼、禅堂、客堂等。建筑为典型闽南风格，燕尾脊高耸，剪粘装饰绚丽，大悲殿为八角三重檐攒尖顶，造型独特。南普陀寺与闽南佛学院同址，是中国佛教教育的重要基地。

Nanputuo Temple, located at the foot of Wulao Peak in Xiamen, was founded in the Tang dynasty and is a sacred site of Minnan Buddhism. Built along the hillside, its central axis includes the Heavenly Kings Hall, Mahavira Hall, Great Compassion Hall, and Sutra Library, with bell and drum towers, meditation halls, and guest halls on both sides. In typical Minnan style with towering swallow-tail ridges and brilliant cut-and-paste porcelain decoration, the Great Compassion Hall features an octagonal triple-eave pyramidal roof with a unique form. Nanputuo Temple shares its site with the Minnan Buddhist College, an important base for Chinese Buddhist education.

二宜楼详解 Detailed
华安县

二宜楼位于漳州市华安县仙都镇，建于清乾隆三十五年（1770年），是福建土楼中保存最完整的圆形土楼之一。楼外径73.4米，高16米，四层，每层52个房间，共208个房间。二宜楼的独特之处在于每个单元都有独立的天井和楼梯，单元之间有隔墙，既保持了聚族而居的传统，又兼顾了小家庭的私密性。楼内保存有大量壁画、彩绘和楹联，是土楼中的艺术宝库。2008年随福建土楼列入世界文化遗产。

The building preserves numerous murals, paintings, and couplets, an artistic treasure house among tulou. Listed as UNESCO World Heritage with Fujian Tulou in 2008.

鲤城区

开元寺位于泉州市鲤城区，始建于唐垂拱二年（686年），是闽南地区最大的佛教寺院。寺院占地约7.8万平方米，中轴线有天王殿、拜亭、大雄宝殿、甘露戒坛、藏经阁，东西两侧有镇国塔（东塔）和仁寿塔（西塔）。东西塔为南宋石构楼阁式塔，高48.24米和45.06米，是中国现存最高的石塔，雕刻精美，是宋代石构建筑的杰作。开元寺是泉州作为宋元世界海洋商贸中心的重要遗产点，2021年随泉州列入世界文化遗产。

Kaiyuan Temple, located in Licheng District, Quanzhou, was founded in the second year of Chuigong of the Tang (686 CE) and is the largest Buddhist temple in the Minnan region. 清净寺详解 Detailed
鲤城区

清净寺位于泉州市鲤城区，始建于北宋大中祥符二年（1009年），是中国现存最早的伊斯兰教寺院之一。寺院为阿拉伯建筑风格，大门为尖拱形，高20米，以青绿色花岗石砌筑，门楼后为礼拜堂。清净寺是泉州海外贸易和伊斯兰文化传播的重要见证，2021年随泉州列入世界文化遗产。寺内保存有古阿拉伯文碑刻和明代永乐敕谕碑，是研究中国伊斯兰教史的重要实物。

Qingjing Mosque, located in Licheng District, Quanzhou, was founded in the second year of Dazhong Xiangfu of the Northern Song (1009 CE), one of the earliest surviving Islamic mosques in China. In Arabic architectural style, its gate is a pointed arch 20m high, built of blue-green granite, with a prayer hall behind the gatehouse. Qingjing Mosque is an important witness to Quanzhou's overseas trade and the spread of Islamic culture, listed as UNESCO World Heritage with Quanzhou in 2021. The mosque preserves ancient Arabic inscriptions and a Ming dynasty Yongle imperial edict stele, important artifacts for studying the history of Islam in China.

Tianhou Palace is an important site where Mazu worship evolved from folk belief to state sacrifice, listed as UNESCO World Heritage with Quanzhou in 2021.

安平桥位于晋江市安海镇，始建于南宋绍兴八年（1138年），是中国现存最长的古代石桥，全长2255米，有'天下无桥长此桥'之誉。桥为梁式石桥，有桥墩361座，桥面以巨型石板铺成，宽3-3.8米。安平桥是宋代泉州海外贸易繁荣的见证，2021年随泉州列入世界文化遗产。桥上建有五座憩亭，桥头有白塔，是古代桥梁工程的杰作。

Anping Bridge, located in Anhai Town, Jinjiang, was founded in the eighth year of Shaoxing of the Southern Song (1138 CE). It is the longest surviving ancient stone bridge in China, with a total length of 2,255m, praised as 'no bridge under heaven is longer.' A beam-style stone bridge with 361 piers, its deck is paved with giant stone slabs 3-3.8m wide. Anping Bridge witnesses the prosperity of Song dynasty Quanzhou overseas trade, listed as UNESCO World Heritage with Quanzhou in 2021. Five rest pavilions stand on the bridge, with a white pagoda at the bridgehead—a masterpiece of ancient bridge engineering.

Cao'an is an important heritage site of Quanzhou as the Song-Yuan world maritime trade center, witnessing the spread of Manichaeism in the East, listed as UNESCO World Heritage with Quanzhou in 2021.

万安桥位于宁德市屏南县长桥镇，始建于北宋元祐五年（1090年），是中国现存最长的木拱廊桥，全长98.2米，宽4.7米，五墩六孔。桥屋有37开间，供奉神像，桥中设神龛。万安桥不用一钉一铆，以木材搭接成拱形结构，是中国古代桥梁技术的杰出代表。2022年8月万安桥遭遇火灾，大部分桥体被毁，目前正在复建中。

Wan'an Bridge, located in Changqiao Town, Pingnan County, Ningde, was founded in the fifth year of Yuanyou of the Northern Song (1090 CE). It is the longest surviving timber arch lounge bridge in China, with a total length of 98.2m, width of 4.7m, five piers and six spans. The bridge house has 37 bays with deity statues and a shrine in the middle. Wan'an Bridge was built without a single nail or rivet, using timber joinery to form an arch structure—an outstanding representative of ancient Chinese bridge technology. In August 2022, Wan'an Bridge suffered a fire that destroyed most of the bridge body; reconstruction is currently underway.

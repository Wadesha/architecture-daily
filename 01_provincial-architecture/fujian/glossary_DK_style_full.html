塔与桥梁 Pagodas and Bridges
石塔
Stone Pagoda (Shita)
以石材建造的佛塔或风水塔，福建地区保存了大量唐宋时期的石塔，多为仿木结构楼阁式塔，雕刻精美，是研究古代建筑和佛教艺术的重要实物。
Buddhist or feng shui pagodas built of stone; the Fujian region preserves a large number of stone pagodas from the Tang and Song dynasties, mostly timber-structure-imitating pavilion-style pagodas with exquisite carvings, important physical evidence for studying ancient architecture and Buddhist art.
典型实例 Examples
泉州东西塔、莆田无尘塔、云霄石矾塔、厦门婆罗门教塔
楼阁式塔
Pavilion-Style Pagoda (Lougeshi Ta)
中国佛塔的主要类型之一，模仿中国传统楼阁建筑形式，分层设塔身、塔檐、平座，可登临眺望，泉州开元寺东西塔是中国现存最高的一对石构楼阁式塔。
One of the main types of Chinese Buddhist pagodas, imitating the form of traditional Chinese pavilion architecture, with layered tower bodies, eaves and flat seats, accessible for viewing; the East and West Pagodas of Quanzhou Kaiyuan Temple are the tallest existing pair of stone pavilion-style pagodas in China.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺东西塔、莆田东岩山报恩寺塔、厦门婆罗门教塔、云霄石矾塔
宝箧印经塔
Sutra-Printing Stupa (Baoqie Yinjing Ta)
佛塔的一种形式，模仿古印度宝箧（藏经匣）形状，塔身方形，四角立柱，塔顶为塔刹，莆田天中万寿塔是中国现存最大的宝箧印经塔实例。
A type of Buddhist pagoda, imitating the shape of ancient Indian treasure boxes (sutra storage boxes), with a square tower body, columns at the four corners, and a spire at the top; the Tianzhong Wanshou Pagoda in Putian is the largest existing example of a sutra-printing stupa in China.
典型实例 Examples
莆田天中万寿塔、泉州开元寺阿育王塔、厦门婆罗门教塔
石梁桥
Stone Beam Bridge (Shiliangqiao)
以巨大石梁横跨桥墩的桥梁形式，福建沿海地区保存了多座宋代石梁桥，泉州洛阳桥、安平桥是中国古代石梁桥的杰出代表。
A bridge form with huge stone beams spanning bridge piers; the coastal region of Fujian preserves multiple Song dynasty stone beam bridges, and Quanzhou Luoyang Bridge and Anping Bridge are outstanding representatives of ancient Chinese stone beam bridges.
典型实例 Examples
泉州洛阳桥、晋江安平桥、莆田宁海桥、龙海江东桥
筏形基础
Raft Foundation (Faxing Jichu)
泉州洛阳桥首创的桥梁基础形式，在江底沿桥梁中线抛置大量石块，形成一条横跨江底的矮石堤，再在其上建造桥墩，是世界上最早的筏形基础工程。
A bridge foundation form pioneered by Quanzhou Luoyang Bridge, where a large number of stones are thrown along the bridge centerline at the river bottom, forming a low stone embankment across the river bottom, on which bridge piers are then built; it is the world's earliest raft foundation engineering.
典型实例 Examples
泉州洛阳桥、晋江安平桥
种蛎固基
Oyster-Cultivation Foundation (Zhongli Guji)
泉州洛阳桥首创的桥梁加固技术，在桥基和桥墩上养殖牡蛎，利用牡蛎外壳的附着力将桥基石块胶结成整体，是世界上最早将生物学应用于桥梁工程的创举。
A bridge reinforcement technology pioneered by Quanzhou Luoyang Bridge, where oysters are cultivated on the bridge foundation and piers, using the adhesion of oyster shells to cement the foundation stones into a whole; it is the world's earliest initiative to apply biology to bridge engineering.
典型实例 Examples
泉州洛阳桥
2
墙体与基础 Walls and Foundations
夯土墙
Rammed Earth Wall (Hangtuqiang)
以生土为主要材料，通过模板分层夯实而成的墙体，是福建土楼、土堡、庄寨等防御性建筑的主要外墙形式，具有保温隔热、防火抗震等优良性能。
Walls made mainly of raw earth, compacted layer by layer through formwork; they are the main outer wall form of defensive buildings such as Fujian tulou, earthen forts and fortified manors, with excellent properties including thermal insulation, fire resistance and earthquake resistance.
典型实例 Examples
永定土楼、南靖土楼、大田土堡、永泰庄寨
三合土
Three-in-One Soil (Sanhetu)
由石灰、黏土、沙子（或糯米浆、红糖等）按比例混合而成的建筑材料，强度高、耐久性好，常用于福建土楼的墙基、地面和屋面防水层。
A building material made by mixing lime, clay and sand (or glutinous rice paste, brown sugar, etc.) in proportion, with high strength and good durability; commonly used for the foundations, floors and roof waterproofing layers of Fujian tulou.
典型实例 Examples
赵家堡完璧楼、南靖怀远楼、华安二宜楼、平和庄上大楼
鹅卵石墙基
Cobblestone Foundation (Eluan Shi Qiangji)
福建土楼和民居中常见的基础形式，以河流中天然鹅卵石砌筑墙基，高出地面1-2米，既防潮又防洪水，上部再接夯土墙或砖墙。
A common foundation form in Fujian tulou and residences, using natural cobblestones from rivers to build the wall foundation, 1-2 meters above ground, both moisture-proof and flood-proof, with rammed earth or brick walls connected above.
典型实例 Examples
南靖河坑土楼群、平和绳武楼、华安大地土楼群、永泰庄寨
马鞍墙
Saddle Wall (Ma'anqiang)
福州及闽东地区民居中特有的山墙形式，墙顶呈马鞍形曲线，高出屋面，具有防火、防风功能，是福州三坊七巷等传统街区的标志性景观。
A distinctive gable wall form in residences of Fuzhou and eastern Fujian, with the wall top forming a saddle-shaped curve, rising above the roof, with fire-prevention and wind-protection functions; it is the iconic landscape of traditional districts such as Fuzhou's Three Lanes and Seven Alleys.
典型实例 Examples
福州三坊七巷、闽清宏琳厝、宁德民居、永泰庄寨
3
宗教与礼制建筑 Religious and Ritual Architecture
妈祖庙
Mazu Temple (Mazumiao)
供奉海神妈祖（林默娘）的庙宇，起源于福建莆田湄洲岛，是中国沿海地区最重要的民间信仰建筑之一，湄洲妈祖庙是全世界妈祖庙的祖庙。
Temples enshrining the sea goddess Mazu (Lin Moniang), originating from Meizhou Island in Putian, Fujian; one of the most important folk belief buildings in China's coastal regions, and Meizhou Mazu Temple is the ancestral temple of all Mazu temples worldwide.
典型实例 Examples
莆田湄洲妈祖庙、莆田平海天后宫、泉州天后宫、厦门顺济宫
慈济宫
Ciji Palace (Cijigong)
供奉保生大帝（吴夲）的庙宇，保生大帝是闽南地区最重要的医神信仰，厦门青礁慈济宫和漳州白礁慈济宫是慈济信仰的两大祖庙。
Temples enshrining the Great Emperor of Life Preservation (Wu Tao), the most important medical deity belief in the Minnan region; Xiamen Qingjiao Ciji Palace and Zhangzhou Baijiao Ciji Palace are the two ancestral temples of the Ciji belief.
典型实例 Examples
厦门青礁慈济祖宫、漳州白礁慈济宫、泉州花桥慈济宫
关帝庙
Guandi Temple (Guandimiao)
供奉三国蜀汉名将关羽的庙宇，关羽被尊为武圣、武财神，是中国民间信仰中最广泛的神祇之一，福建东山关帝庙是台湾关帝庙的祖庙。
Temples enshrining Guan Yu, the famous general of Shu Han during the Three Kingdoms period, revered as the Martial Sage and Martial God of Wealth, one of the most widely worshipped deities in Chinese folk belief; Dongshan Guandi Temple in Fujian is the ancestral temple of Taiwan's Guandi temples.
典型实例 Examples
东山关帝庙、泉州通淮关岳庙、诏安悬钟所关帝庙、平和城隍庙配殿
文庙
Confucian Temple (Wenmiao)
供奉孔子的礼制建筑，又称孔庙，是古代地方官学和祭祀孔子的场所，通常包括大成殿、明伦堂、泮池、棂星门等建筑，福建各地保存了多座明清时期的文庙。
Ritual buildings enshrining Confucius, also called Confucian temples, serving as ancient local government schools and places for sacrificing to Confucius, usually including the Dacheng Hall, Minglun Hall, Pan Pond and Lingxing Gate; multiple Ming and Qing dynasty Confucian temples are preserved throughout Fujian.
典型实例 Examples
泉州府文庙、漳州府文庙、福州文庙、莆田文庙
城隍庙
City God Temple (Chenghuangmiao)
供奉城隍神的庙宇，城隍是古代城市的保护神，城隍庙通常与县衙并设，是中国古代城市的重要标志建筑，福建平和城隍庙因王阳明奏建而具有特殊历史价值。
Temples enshrining the City God, the protective deity of ancient cities; City God Temples were usually established alongside county government offices, serving as important landmark buildings of ancient Chinese cities; Pinghe City God Temple in Fujian has special historical value due to being built by Wang Yangming's memorial request.
典型实例 Examples
平和城隍庙、诏安城隍庙、福州城隍庙、泉州城隍庙
石牌坊
Stone Archway (Shipaifang)
以石材建造的纪念性牌坊，用于表彰功德、科举、节孝等，福建地区保存了大量明清石牌坊，多为仿木结构，雕刻精美，长泰武安牌坊群、诏安南诏牌坊群是代表性实例。
Memorial archways built of stone, used to commend merits, imperial examinations, chastity and filial piety, etc.; the Fujian region preserves a large number of Ming and Qing stone archways, mostly imitating timber structures with exquisite carvings; Changtai Wu'an Archway Cluster and Zhao'an Nanzhao Archway Cluster are representative examples.
典型实例 Examples
长泰武安牌坊群、诏安南诏牌坊群、漳州石牌坊、仙游东门石坊
宗祠
Ancestral Hall (Zongci)
供奉祖先牌位、举行宗族祭祀活动的建筑，是中国宗法制度的物质载体，福建地区聚族而居的传统使得宗祠建筑特别发达，闽南宗祠装饰华丽，客家宗祠简朴庄重。
Buildings enshrining ancestral tablets and holding clan sacrificial activities, the material carrier of the Chinese patriarchal system; the tradition of clan-based living in the Fujian region makes ancestral hall architecture particularly developed, with Minnan ancestral halls gorgeously decorated and Hakka ancestral halls simple and solemn.
典型实例 Examples
泉州陈埭丁氏宗祠、晋江衙口施氏大宗祠、长泰杨氏宗祠、南靖塔下张氏家庙
书院
Academy (Shuyuan)
中国古代的教育机构，兼具教学、祭祀、藏书功能，福建是书院发达地区，漳州松州书院是中国最早的书院之一，创办于唐景龙二年（708年）。
Ancient Chinese educational institutions, combining teaching, sacrifice and book-collection functions; Fujian is a region with developed academies, and Zhangzhou Songzhou Academy is one of the earliest academies in China, founded in the second year of Jinglong reign of the Tang (708).
典型实例 Examples
漳州松州书院、漳州霞东书院、尤溪豫章书院、福州鳌峰书院
4
屋顶形式 Roof Forms
歇山顶
Hip-and-Gable Roof (Xieshan)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，又称九脊顶，由一条正脊、四条垂脊和四条戗脊组成，上半部为悬山式，下半部为庑殿式，常用于宫殿、寺庙、园林建筑中等级较高的建筑。福建地区寺庙大殿多采用此形式。
One of the traditional Chinese architectural roof forms, also called the nine-ridge roof, consisting of one main ridge, four vertical ridges and four diagonal ridges; the upper half is of the overhanging gable type and the lower half of the hip type, commonly used for higher-ranking buildings in palaces, temples and gardens. Temple main halls in Fujian often adopt this form.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺大雄宝殿、福州华林寺大殿、漳州南山寺、平和城隍庙大殿
燕尾脊
Swallowtail Ridge (Yanweiji)
闽南地区特有的屋脊形式，正脊两端向上翘起，形似燕尾分叉，曲线优美，是闽南红砖建筑的标志性特征。燕尾脊有单燕、双燕之分，常见于寺庙、祠堂和大型民居。
A distinctive ridge form unique to southern Fujian, where both ends of the main ridge curve upward and fork like a swallow's tail, with elegant curves; it is the iconic feature of Minnan red-brick architecture. Swallowtail ridges are divided into single-swallowtail and double-swallowtail types, commonly seen in temples, ancestral halls and large residences.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、厦门青礁慈济祖宫、漳州白礁慈济宫、南安中宪第
马鞍脊
Saddle Ridge (Ma'anji)
福建民居中常见的屋脊形式，山墙顶部呈马鞍形曲线，两端高中间低，形似马鞍。福州、闽东地区民居多用马鞍墙，具有防火、防风功能。
A common ridge form in Fujian vernacular architecture, where the top of the gable wall forms a saddle-shaped curve, high at both ends and low in the middle, resembling a saddle. Residences in Fuzhou and eastern Fujian often use saddle walls, with fire-prevention and wind-protection functions.
典型实例 Examples
福州三坊七巷民居、宁德廊桥、闽清宏琳厝、永泰庄寨
悬山顶
Overhanging Gable Roof (Xuanshan)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，又称挑山顶，两山屋顶伸出山墙之外，檩木暴露在外，是民间建筑中最常见的屋顶形式。福建土楼内环建筑多采用此形式。
A traditional Chinese roof form, also called the overhanging mountain roof, where the two gable roofs extend beyond the gable walls with exposed purlins, the most common roof form in vernacular architecture. The inner-ring buildings of Fujian tulou often adopt this form.
典型实例 Examples
永定土楼内环建筑、南靖云水谣民居、平和绳武楼、华安大地土楼群
硬山顶
Flush Gable Roof (Yingshan)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，山墙与屋顶齐平或高出屋顶，檩木不外露，防火性能较好。福建闽南红砖厝多采用硬山顶配合马鞍墙或出砖入石墙面。
A traditional Chinese roof form where the gable walls are flush with or rise above the roof, with no exposed purlins, with relatively good fire resistance. Minnan red-brick houses in Fujian often adopt flush gable roofs配合 saddle walls or brick-and-stone walls.
典型实例 Examples
泉州红砖厝、漳州古民居、厦门鼓浪屿老建筑、莆田大宗伯第
攒尖顶
Pyramidal Roof (Zanjian)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，屋顶为锥形，无正脊，顶部集中于一点（宝顶），有三角、四角、六角、八角、圆形等多种平面形式，常用于亭、阁、塔等建筑。福建廊桥桥亭多采用此形式。
A traditional Chinese roof form with a conical shape, no main ridge, and the top converging at a single point (finial), with various plan forms including triangular, quadrangular, hexagonal, octagonal and circular, commonly used for pavilions, towers and pagodas. The bridge pavilions of Fujian covered bridges often adopt this form.
典型实例 Examples
宁德木拱廊桥桥亭、泉州东西塔、莆田无尘塔、云霄石矾塔
重檐
Double Eaves (Zhongyan)
中国古建筑屋顶形式之一，在单檐屋顶下方再加一层屋檐，形成上下两层屋檐，显得建筑更加高大雄伟，常用于等级较高的宫殿、寺庙大殿。福建地区寺庙大殿和文庙大成殿常用重檐歇山顶。
A traditional Chinese roof form where an additional layer of eaves is added below a single-eave roof, forming upper and lower layers of eaves, making the building appear taller and more majestic; commonly used for higher-ranking palaces and temple main halls. Temple main halls and Confucian temple Dacheng Halls in Fujian often use double-eave hip-and-gable roofs.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺大雄宝殿、平和文庙大成殿、漳州府文庙、福州西禅寺
5
建筑典籍 Architectural Classics
营造法式
Yingzao Fashi (State Building Standards)
北宋李诫编修的建筑技术专著，是中国古代最完整的建筑技术书籍，确立了材分制、斗拱等级等建筑规范，对后世建筑影响深远，福建宋代建筑多遵循其规制。
An architectural technical monograph compiled by Li Jie in the Northern Song Dynasty, the most complete architectural technical book in ancient China, establishing architectural standards such as the cai-fen system and bracket set grades, with profound influence on later architecture; Song dynasty buildings in Fujian mostly follow its regulations.
典型实例 Examples
福州华林寺大殿、莆田元妙观三清殿、泉州开元寺
6
建筑布局 Architectural Layout
中轴对称
Central-Axis Symmetry
中国古建筑的基本布局原则，建筑沿中轴线对称排列，体现礼制秩序和等级观念，福建文庙、寺庙、大型民居多遵循中轴对称布局。
The basic layout principle of traditional Chinese architecture, where buildings are arranged symmetrically along a central axis, reflecting ritual order and hierarchical concepts; Fujian Confucian temples, temples and large residences mostly follow a central-axis symmetric layout.
典型实例 Examples
泉州府文庙、漳州府文庙、福州华林寺、莆田大宗伯第
前堂后寝
Front Hall Rear Quarters
中国传统民居的基本布局模式，前部为厅堂用于会客和祭祀，后部为卧室用于居住，福建大型民居和土楼内部多遵循此布局。
The basic layout pattern of traditional Chinese residences, with the front part being halls for receiving guests and sacrifice, and the rear part being bedrooms for living; large residences and tulou interiors in Fujian mostly follow this layout.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、南安中宪第、闽清宏琳厝、南靖怀远楼
7
建筑文化 Architectural Culture
风水
Feng Shui
中国古代关于建筑选址和环境规划的理论，强调人与自然的和谐，福建古建筑在选址、朝向、布局等方面深受风水理论影响，土楼、寺庙的选址尤其讲究。
An ancient Chinese theory about architectural site selection and environmental planning, emphasizing harmony between humans and nature; ancient Fujian architecture is deeply influenced by feng shui theory in site selection, orientation and layout, with the site selection of tulou and temples being particularly particular.
典型实例 Examples
南靖南薰楼、永定土楼群、泉州寺庙、福州三坊七巷
8
建筑等级 Architectural Grading
全国重点文物保护单位
National Key Cultural Heritage Site
中国文物保护的最高等级，由国务院核定公布，具有重大历史、艺术、科学价值，福建古建筑地图中收录的大量建筑被列为全国重点文物保护单位。
The highest level of cultural heritage protection in China, approved and announced by the State Council, with significant historical, artistic and scientific value; a large number of buildings included in the Historical Architectural Map of Fujian are listed as National Key Cultural Heritage Sites.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺、福州华林寺、漳州南山寺、永定土楼
省级文物保护单位
Provincial Cultural Heritage Site
由省级人民政府核定公布的文物保护等级，具有较高的历史、艺术、科学价值，福建各地保存了大量省级文物保护单位。
A cultural heritage protection level approved and announced by provincial-level people's governments, with relatively high historical, artistic and scientific value; a large number of Provincial Cultural Heritage Sites are preserved throughout Fujian.
典型实例 Examples
莆田大宗伯第、漳州威惠庙、南平宝峰寺、宁德廊桥
面阔
Building Width (Miankuo)
中国古建筑中建筑正面的宽度，以开间数计算，面阔间数越多等级越高，福建寺庙大殿多为五开间或七开间，民居多为三开间。
The width of the front face of traditional Chinese architecture, calculated by the number of bays; the more bays in width, the higher the rank; temple main halls in Fujian are mostly five or seven bays wide, while residences are mostly three bays wide.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺大雄宝殿、福州华林寺大殿、平和城隍庙大殿、泉州蔡氏古民居
进深
Building Depth (Jinshen)
中国古建筑中建筑前后的深度，以檩数或开间数计算，进深越大建筑规模越大，福建寺庙大殿进深多为十檩以上。
The front-to-back depth of traditional Chinese architecture, calculated by the number of purlins or bays; the greater the depth, the larger the building scale; temple main halls in Fujian mostly have a depth of over ten purlins.
典型实例 Examples
福州华林寺大殿、泉州开元寺大雄宝殿、华安南山宫、平和城隍庙
世界文化遗产
World Cultural Heritage
由联合国教科文组织认定的具有突出普遍价值的文化遗产，福建土楼于2008年列入世界文化遗产名录，泉州宋元中国的世界海洋商贸中心于2021年列入。
Cultural heritage with outstanding universal value recognized by UNESCO; Fujian Tulou was inscribed on the World Cultural Heritage List in 2008, and Quanzhou: Emporium of the World in Song-Yuan China was inscribed in 2021.
典型实例 Examples
福建土楼、泉州开元寺、泉州清净寺、泉州六胜塔
9
建筑部件 Architectural Components
柱础
Column Base (Zhuchu)
柱子底部的承重构件，多为石质，具有防潮、承重和装饰作用，福建古建筑中柱础形式多样，有鼓形、覆盆形、莲花形等。
Load-bearing components at the bottom of columns, mostly stone, with moisture-proof, load-bearing and decorative functions; column bases in Fujian ancient architecture have diverse forms, including drum-shaped, inverted-basin-shaped and lotus-shaped.
典型实例 Examples
东山关帝庙、泉州开元寺、福州华林寺、漳州南山寺
额枋
Architrave (Efang)
中国古建筑中柱子之间的横向联系构件，位于柱头之上，用于拉结柱子和承托斗拱，福建闽南建筑中额枋常施精美木雕和彩绘。
Horizontal connecting components between columns in traditional Chinese architecture, located above column tops, used to tie columns and support bracket sets; in Minnan architecture, architraves are often decorated with exquisite wood carvings and colored paintings.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺、东山关帝庙、漳州南山寺、平和绳武楼
雀替
Spandrel Bracket (Queti)
中国古建筑中位于梁枋与柱交接处的装饰构件，形似双翼，具有加固和装饰作用，福建建筑中雀替常雕刻成花草、鸟兽等形状。
Decorative components located at the junction of beams and columns in traditional Chinese architecture, shaped like two wings, with reinforcing and decorative functions; in Fujian architecture, spandrel brackets are often carved into flowers, plants, birds and beasts.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、东山关帝庙、长泰杨氏宗祠、平和绳武楼
照壁
Screen Wall (Zhaobi)
中国古建筑中位于大门内外的屏蔽墙，用于遮挡视线和装饰，福建寺庙、祠堂、大型民居中常见照壁，多以砖石砌筑并施装饰。
Screen walls located inside or outside the main gate in traditional Chinese architecture, used to block the view and decorate; screen walls are common in Fujian temples, ancestral halls and large residences, mostly built of brick and stone with decoration.
典型实例 Examples
云霄威惠庙、泉州开元寺、漳州南山寺、福州三坊七巷
门簪
Door Pin (Men Zan)
中国古建筑中固定大门上槛的构件，外露于门额上方，形似发簪，具有装饰和象征等级的作用，福建民居中门簪常雕刻成花卉形状。
Components fixing the upper door threshold in traditional Chinese architecture, exposed above the door lintel, shaped like hairpins, with decorative and rank-symbolizing functions; in Fujian residences, door pins are often carved into flower shapes.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、南安中宪第、福州三坊七巷、闽清宏琳厝
门枕石
Door Bearing Stone (Men Zhenshi)
中国古建筑中承托大门转轴的石质构件，位于门框两侧下方，常雕刻成狮子、鼓等形状，福建闽南建筑中门枕石雕刻精美。
Stone components supporting the door pivot in traditional Chinese architecture, located below both sides of the door frame, often carved into lions, drums and other shapes; in Minnan architecture, door bearing stones are exquisitely carved.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、厦门鼓浪屿老建筑、漳州古民居、南安中宪第
10
木构体系 Timber Frame System
斗拱
Bracket Set (Dougong)
中国古建筑特有的构件，由方形的斗、升和弓形的拱、斜向的昂组成，位于柱顶、额枋与屋顶之间，起承托屋檐、传递荷载和装饰作用，是中国古建筑的标志性元素。福建华林寺大殿保留了宋代斗拱的典型做法。
A unique component of traditional Chinese architecture, consisting of square blocks (dou/sheng), bow-shaped arms (gong) and inclined ang (lever arms), located between column tops, architraves and the roof, serving to support eaves, transfer loads and decorate, and is the iconic element of traditional Chinese architecture. The main hall of Fuzhou Hualin Temple preserves the typical Song dynasty bracket set method.
典型实例 Examples
福州华林寺大殿、泉州开元寺、浦城云峰寺、华安南山宫
丁头拱
Corbel Bracket (Dingtougong)
福建古建筑中常见的斗拱形式，从柱身或梁架侧面伸出的短拱，形似丁字头，用于承托挑檐枋或梁架，是闽南建筑中简化斗拱的典型做法，装饰性强。
A common bracket set form in Fujian ancient architecture, a short bracket protruding from the column body or side of the beam frame, shaped like the character 'ding' (T), used to support the eave lintel or beam frame; it is a typical simplified bracket set method in Minnan architecture, with strong decorative qualities.
典型实例 Examples
漳州威惠庙、东山关帝庙、长泰杨氏宗祠、诏安城隍庙
连珠斗
Connected-Bead Dou (Lianzhudou)
福建古建筑中斗拱的一种做法，多个斗上下叠置如连珠，用于襻间或梁架之间，具有装饰和结构双重作用，常见于闽南和闽东地区的寺庙、祠堂建筑。
A bracket set method in Fujian ancient architecture, where multiple dou (blocks) are stacked vertically like connected beads, used between inter-columnar spaces or beam frames, with both decorative and structural functions; commonly seen in temples and ancestral halls in southern and eastern Fujian.
典型实例 Examples
诏安南诏牌坊群、长泰杨氏宗祠、东山关帝庙、漳州南山寺
抬梁式
Beam-Lifting Frame (Tailiangshi)
中国古建筑木构架体系之一，又称叠梁式，柱上搁置梁头，梁头上搁置檩条，梁上再用矮柱支起较短的梁，如此层叠而上，形成屋架。福建寺庙大殿多采用抬梁式构架。
One of the traditional Chinese timber frame systems, also called the stacked-beam system, where beam heads rest on columns, purlins rest on beam heads, and shorter beams are supported by short columns on the beams, stacking upward to form the roof frame. Temple main halls in Fujian mostly adopt the beam-lifting frame.
典型实例 Examples
福州华林寺大殿、泉州开元寺大雄宝殿、华安南山宫、平和城隍庙
穿斗式
Column-and-Tie Frame (Chuandoushi)
中国古建筑木构架体系之一，又称立帖式，用穿枋把柱子串联起来形成房架，檩条直接搁置在柱头上，沿檩条方向再用斗枋把柱子串联起来。福建民居和土楼内部多采用穿斗式构架。
One of the traditional Chinese timber frame systems, also called the post-and-lintel system, where columns are connected by tie beams to form the frame, purlins rest directly on column tops, and columns are further connected by bracket beams along the purlin direction. The interiors of Fujian residences and tulou mostly adopt the column-and-tie frame.
典型实例 Examples
永定土楼、南靖土楼、福州三坊七巷民居、闽清宏琳厝
插梁式
Inserted-Beam Frame (Chaliangshi)
福建地区特有的木构架形式，梁头直接插入柱身而非搁置在柱顶，结合了抬梁式和穿斗式的特点，是闽地建筑木作的重要特色，华林寺大殿即采用此做法。
A timber frame form unique to the Fujian region, where beam heads are directly inserted into the column body rather than resting on column tops, combining features of both beam-lifting and column-and-tie systems; it is an important feature of Fujian architectural timber work, and the main hall of Hualin Temple adopts this method.
典型实例 Examples
福州华林寺大殿、宁德廊桥、莆田元妙观三清殿、泉州开元寺
梭柱
Shuttle-Shaped Column (Suozhu)
中国古建筑中柱子的一种形式，柱子上下两端收窄、中部饱满，形似织布梭子，具有古朴的美感，常见于宋代及以前的建筑。福建东山关帝庙、漳州威惠庙保留了梭形石柱。
A column form in traditional Chinese architecture, where the column narrows at both upper and lower ends and is full in the middle, resembling a weaving shuttle, with an ancient aesthetic; commonly seen in buildings from the Song dynasty and earlier. Dongshan Guandi Temple and Zhangzhou Weihui Temple in Fujian preserve shuttle-shaped stone columns.
典型实例 Examples
东山关帝庙、漳州威惠庙、福州华林寺、莆田元妙观
月梁
Crescent Beam (Yueliang)
中国古建筑中梁的一种形式，梁身中部微微拱起，两端收窄，形似新月，具有结构和装饰双重作用，常见于江南和福建地区的寺庙、祠堂建筑。
A beam form in traditional Chinese architecture, where the beam body slightly arches in the middle and narrows at both ends, resembling a crescent moon, with both structural and decorative functions; commonly seen in temples and ancestral halls in the Jiangnan and Fujian regions.
典型实例 Examples
诏安南诏牌坊群、泉州开元寺、漳州南山寺、福州西禅寺
藻井
Caisson Ceiling (Zaojing)
中国古建筑中天花板上的装饰性凹面结构，多位于室内明间顶部，呈方形、多边形或圆形，层层叠涩收进，中心常饰以莲花、龙凤等图案，具有神圣象征意义。华安南山宫保留了螺旋式鸡笼藻井。
A decorative concave structure on the ceiling of traditional Chinese architecture, mostly located at the top of the central bay indoors, in square, polygonal or circular forms, with layered corbelling receding inward, the center often decorated with lotus, dragon and phoenix patterns, with sacred symbolic meaning. Nanshan Palace in Hua'an preserves a spiral 'chicken coop' caisson ceiling.
典型实例 Examples
华安南山宫、泉州开元寺、福州华林寺、漳州南山寺
11
材料与工艺 Materials and Crafts
花岗岩
Granite (Huagangyan)
福建地区最丰富的建筑石材，质地坚硬，色泽灰白，广泛用于建筑的墙基、柱、梁、牌坊、桥梁等部位，惠安石雕即以花岗岩为主要材料。
The most abundant building stone in the Fujian region, with hard texture and grayish-white color, widely used for wall foundations, columns, beams, archways and bridges; Hui'an stone carving uses granite as the main material.
典型实例 Examples
泉州东西塔、厦门胡里山炮台、漳州石牌坊、惠安崇武古城
红砖
Red Brick (Hongzhuan)
闽南地区特有的建筑材料，以当地黏土烧制而成，色泽鲜红，规格统一，是闽南红砖厝的主要建筑材料，形成了闽南建筑独特的红色基调。
A distinctive building material in the Minnan region, fired from local clay, with bright red color and uniform specifications; it is the main building material of Minnan red-brick houses, forming the unique red tone of Minnan architecture.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、南安中宪第、漳州杨阿苗民居、厦门鼓浪屿老建筑
琉璃瓦
Glazed Tile (Liuliwa)
表面施釉的陶质瓦件，色彩鲜艳，防水性好，常用于宫殿、寺庙等高等级建筑的屋面，福建寺庙建筑中常见黄、绿、蓝等色琉璃瓦。
Ceramic tile pieces with glazed surfaces, with bright colors and good waterproofing, commonly used for the roofs of high-ranking buildings such as palaces and temples; yellow, green, blue and other colored glazed tiles are common in Fujian temple architecture.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺、福州西禅寺、厦门南普陀寺、莆田湄洲妈祖庙
灰塑
Lime Sculpture (Huisu)
以石灰为主要材料的建筑装饰工艺，在屋脊、檐口、山墙等部位塑造各种图案，福建闽南建筑中灰塑常与剪瓷雕配合使用。
An architectural decorative craft using lime as the main material, shaping various patterns on ridges, eaves, gable walls and other parts; in Minnan architecture, lime sculpture is often used in combination with porcelain-cut sculpture.
典型实例 Examples
东山关帝庙、泉州开元寺、漳州南山寺、厦门青礁慈济宫
木雕
Wood Carving (Mudiao)
以木材为材料的雕刻工艺，福建木雕工艺发达，闽南木雕华丽精细，闽东木雕古朴典雅，广泛应用于建筑的梁、枋、门窗、神龛等部位。
A carving craft using wood as material; Fujian has a developed wood carving craft, with Minnan wood carving gorgeous and refined, and eastern Fujian wood carving simple and elegant, widely applied to building beams, lintels, doors and windows, shrines and other parts.
典型实例 Examples
平和绳武楼、泉州蔡氏古民居、长泰杨氏宗祠、南靖怀远楼
彩绘
Colored Painting (Caihui)
在建筑木构件表面绘制的装饰图案，福建寺庙建筑中彩绘题材丰富，色彩绚丽，华安南山宫保留了明末清初的彩绘做法。
Decorative patterns painted on the surface of building timber components; in Fujian temple architecture, colored paintings have rich themes and gorgeous colors, and Nanshan Palace in Hua'an preserves the colored painting methods of the late Ming and early Qing periods.
典型实例 Examples
华安南山宫、泉州开元寺、漳州南山寺、福州华林寺
12
民居与聚落 Residences and Settlements
大夫第
Grand Official's Residence (Dafudi)
对有功名或官宦人家宅第的尊称，福建地区保存了多座大夫第，多为大型合院式民居，建筑规模宏大，装饰精美，体现了主人的社会地位和经济实力。
An honorific title for the residences of families with academic degrees or official positions; the Fujian region preserves multiple 'Dafudi', mostly large courtyard-style residences with grand scale and exquisite decoration, reflecting the owner's social status and economic strength.
典型实例 Examples
莆田大宗伯第、南安中宪第、龙海石码杨氏大夫第、漳浦蓝廷珍府第
百廿间大厝
120-Room Mansion (Bainian Jian Da Cuo)
闽南地区对大型民居的俗称，因房屋数量多达一百二十间而得名，莆田大宗伯第是典型的百廿间大厝，为明万历年间礼部尚书陈经邦的府第。
A colloquial term for large residences in the Minnan region, named for having as many as 120 rooms; Putian Dazongbo Di is a typical 120-room mansion, the residence of Chen Jingbang, Minister of Rites during the Wanli reign of the Ming.
典型实例 Examples
莆田大宗伯第、南安中宪第、漳浦蓝廷珍府第
护厝
Side Wing (Hucuo)
闽南民居中主体建筑两侧的纵向附属建筑，与主体建筑平行排列，之间以天井相隔，用于居住、厨房、储藏等功能，是闽南大厝的重要组成部分。
Longitudinal auxiliary buildings on both sides of the main building in Minnan residences, arranged parallel to the main building and separated by courtyards, used for residence, kitchens, storage and other functions; an important component of Minnan large houses.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、南安中宪第、华安东阳楼、南靖河坑绳庆楼
天井
Courtyard Skylight (Tianjing)
中国传统民居中建筑围合的露天空间，用于采光、通风、排水和晾晒，福建民居中天井尺度较小，深度较大，适应南方炎热多雨的气候。
Open-air spaces enclosed by buildings in traditional Chinese residences, used for lighting, ventilation, drainage and drying; the courtyards in Fujian residences are relatively small in scale and deep in depth, adapting to the hot and rainy climate of southern China.
典型实例 Examples
福州三坊七巷民居、泉州红砖厝、闽清宏琳厝、土楼内院
古城墙
Ancient City Wall (Guchengqiang)
古代城市的防御设施，福建地区保存了多座明清时期的所城、卫城城墙，多以花岗岩条石砌筑，是研究古代军事防御体系的重要实物。
Defensive facilities of ancient cities; the Fujian region preserves multiple Ming and Qing dynasty fortress and guard city walls, mostly built of granite strip stones, important physical evidence for studying ancient military defense systems.
典型实例 Examples
惠安崇武古城、漳浦六鳌城、龙海镇海卫、诏安悬钟千户所城墙
千户所城
Thousand-Household Fortress City (Qianhusuo Cheng)
明代卫所制度下的军事驻防城市，每所驻军约1120人，福建沿海地区为防御倭寇设置了大量千户所城，诏安悬钟千户所城是保存较好的实例。
Military garrison cities under the Ming dynasty guard-battalion system, with each battalion stationing about 1,120 soldiers; the coastal region of Fujian established numerous thousand-household fortress cities to defend against Japanese pirates, and Zhao'an Xuanzhong Thousand-Household Fortress City is a well-preserved example.
典型实例 Examples
诏安悬钟千户所城、惠安崇武所城、龙海镇海卫城、漳浦六鳌所城
瓮城
Barbican (Wengcheng)
古代城门外的防御设施，为半圆形或方形的护门小城，与城墙连为一体，可增强城门的防御能力，福建所城、土堡中常见瓮城设置。
Defensive facilities outside ancient city gates, a semicircular or square gate-protecting small city integrated with the city wall, enhancing the defensive capability of the city gate; barbican settings are common in Fujian fortress cities and earthen forts.
典型实例 Examples
漳浦赵家堡北门、诏安悬钟所东门、惠安崇武古城、大田安良堡
13
福建历史文化 Fujian History and Culture
开漳圣王
Sacred King Who Opened Zhangzhou (Kaizhang Shengwang)
对唐代陈元光的尊称，陈元光于唐垂拱二年（686年）奏请设立漳州，被尊为'开漳圣王'，是闽南地区最重要的民间信仰之一，威惠庙是供奉开漳圣王的主要庙宇。
An honorific title for Chen Yuanguang of the Tang Dynasty, who memorialized to establish Zhangzhou in the second year of Chuigong reign of the Tang (686), revered as the 'Sacred King Who Opened Zhangzhou'; one of the most important folk beliefs in the Minnan region, and Weihui Temple is the main temple enshrining the Sacred King Who Opened Zhangzhou.
典型实例 Examples
云霄威惠庙、漳州路边威惠庙、漳浦威惠庙、华安威惠庙
朱熹
Zhu Xi
南宋著名理学家，曾任漳州知州、泉州同安县主簿，在福建多地讲学授徒，对福建文化教育影响深远，建阳考亭书院是其晚年讲学之所。
A famous Neo-Confucian philosopher of the Southern Song Dynasty, who served as Prefect of Zhangzhou and Registrar of Tong'an County in Quanzhou, teaching and taking disciples in many places in Fujian, with profound influence on Fujian's culture and education; Kouting Academy in Jianyang was where he taught in his later years.
典型实例 Examples
建阳考亭书院、漳州芝山书院、泉州小山丛竹书院、尤溪南溪书院
王阳明
Wang Yangming
明代著名思想家、军事家，于正德十二年（1517年）巡抚南赣，奏请设立平和县，平和城隍庙即为其奏准以府级规格建造，对福建南部地区的开发和治理影响深远。
A famous thinker and military strategist of the Ming Dynasty, who served as Grand Coordinator of Southern Jiangxi in the 12th year of Zhengde reign (1517), memorialized to establish Pinghe County, and Pinghe City God Temple was built at prefectural specifications by his approved memorial; he had profound influence on the development and governance of southern Fujian.
典型实例 Examples
平和城隍庙、平和文庙、平和王阳明纪念馆
闽南文化
Minnan Culture
以泉州、漳州、厦门为核心的地域文化，以闽南语为载体，融合了中原文化、海洋文化和外来文化，具有开放包容、重商崇教的特征，红砖厝、燕尾脊、妈祖信仰是其文化标志。
A regional culture centered on Quanzhou, Zhangzhou and Xiamen, using the Minnan language as its carrier, integrating Central Plains culture, maritime culture and foreign cultures, with characteristics of openness, inclusiveness, commerce emphasis and education reverence; red-brick houses, swallowtail ridges and Mazu belief are its cultural symbols.
典型实例 Examples
泉州红砖厝、漳州燕尾脊建筑、厦门鼓浪屿、莆田妈祖信仰
客家文化
Hakka Culture
客家人创造的地域文化，客家人是历史上从中原南迁的汉族民系，福建西部永定、南靖、上杭等地是客家聚居区，土楼是客家文化最具代表性的建筑形式。
A regional culture created by the Hakka people, who are a Han ethnic group that migrated south from the Central Plains in history; Yongding, Nanjing and Shanghang in western Fujian are Hakka settlement areas, and tulou is the most representative architectural form of Hakka culture.
典型实例 Examples
永定土楼、南靖土楼、上杭客家民居、宁化石壁客家祖地
海上丝绸之路
Maritime Silk Road
古代中国与世界进行贸易和文化交流的海上通道，泉州是宋元时期海上丝绸之路的起点，被誉为'东方第一大港'，保存了大量相关史迹。
The maritime route for trade and cultural exchange between ancient China and the world; Quanzhou was the starting point of the Maritime Silk Road during the Song and Yuan dynasties, known as the 'No. 1 Eastern Port', preserving a large number of related historical sites.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺、泉州清净寺、泉州伊斯兰教圣墓、泉州六胜塔
倭寇
Japanese Pirates (Wokou)
明代骚扰中国沿海的日本海盗集团，福建沿海是倭患重灾区，明代在福建沿海修建了大量卫所、城堡、烽火台等防御设施，俞大猷、戚继光等名将曾在此抗倭。
Japanese pirate groups that harassed China's coast during the Ming Dynasty; the Fujian coast was a heavily affected area of Japanese pirate raids, and the Ming Dynasty built numerous guard posts, castles and beacon towers along the Fujian coast, with famous generals Yu Dayou and Qi Jiguang once fighting Japanese pirates here.
典型实例 Examples
惠安崇武古城、诏安悬钟千户所、漳浦六鳌城、龙海镇海卫
14
福建地理区域 Fujian Geographical Regions
闽中
Central Fujian (Minzhong)
福建中部地区，以福州为中心，包括福州、莆田、宁德等地，是福建政治文化中心，建筑风格以马鞍墙、燕尾脊为特色，庄寨建筑发达。
The central region of Fujian, centered on Fuzhou, including Fuzhou, Putian and Ningde; it is the political and cultural center of Fujian, with architectural styles featuring saddle walls and swallowtail ridges, and developed fortified manor architecture.
典型实例 Examples
福州三坊七巷、莆田湄洲妈祖庙、宁德木拱廊桥、永泰庄寨
闽南
Southern Fujian (Minnan)
福建南部地区，以泉州、漳州、厦门为核心，是福建经济最发达的地区，建筑风格以红砖厝、燕尾脊、剪瓷雕为特色，海洋文化浓厚。
The southern region of Fujian, centered on Quanzhou, Zhangzhou and Xiamen; it is the most economically developed region of Fujian, with architectural styles featuring red-brick houses, swallowtail ridges and porcelain-cut sculpture, with a strong maritime culture.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺、漳州南山寺、厦门鼓浪屿、南安蔡氏古民居
闽西
Western Fujian (Minxi)
福建西部地区，以龙岩为中心，包括龙岩、三明部分地区，是客家聚居区，土楼、土堡建筑发达，客家文化浓厚。
The western region of Fujian, centered on Longyan, including Longyan and parts of Sanming; it is a Hakka settlement area with developed tulou and earthen fort architecture and a strong Hakka culture.
典型实例 Examples
永定土楼、南靖土楼、大田土堡、连城培田古民居
闽北
Northern Fujian (Minbei)
福建北部地区，以南平为中心，包括南平、三明部分地区，是福建文化发源地之一，朱熹理学发源地，保存了大量宋元时期的古建筑。
The northern region of Fujian, centered on Nanping, including Nanping and parts of Sanming; it is one of the birthplaces of Fujian culture and the birthplace of Zhu Xi's Neo-Confucianism, preserving a large number of ancient buildings from the Song and Yuan dynasties.
典型实例 Examples
南平宝峰寺、浦城云峰寺、建阳考亭书院、尤溪朱熹墓
闽东
Eastern Fujian (Mindong)
福建东部地区，以宁德为中心，是中国木拱廊桥最集中的分布区，被誉为'世界木拱廊桥之乡'，廊桥文化是其最显著的文化标志。
The eastern region of Fujian, centered on Ningde; it is the most concentrated distribution area of Chinese timber-arch covered bridges, known as the 'Hometown of World Timber-Arch Covered Bridges', and covered bridge culture is its most prominent cultural symbol.
典型实例 Examples
屏南万安桥、寿宁鸾峰桥、周宁登龙桥、古田临水宫
15
福建特色建筑 Fujian Distinctive Architecture
土楼
Earthen Building (Tulou)
福建西南部客家和闽南地区特有的大型集体民居建筑，以生土夯筑外墙，内部为木结构，平面有圆形、方形、五边形等多种形式，具有防御、居住、祭祀等多重功能，2008年列入世界文化遗产名录。
Large-scale collective residential buildings unique to the Hakka and Minnan regions of southwestern Fujian, with rammed earth outer walls and timber structure interiors, in various plan forms including circular, square and pentagonal, with multiple functions including defense, residence and sacrifice; inscribed on the World Cultural Heritage List in 2008.
典型实例 Examples
永定土楼群、南靖田螺坑土楼群、华安大地土楼群、平和庄上大楼
圆楼
Round Tulou (Yuanlou)
土楼中最具代表性的形式，平面呈圆形，外墙环形夯筑，内部房间沿圆周均匀分布，中心为祖堂或天井，具有向心凝聚的空间特征，被誉为'东方古城堡'。
The most representative form of tulou, with a circular plan, ring-shaped rammed earth outer wall, interior rooms evenly distributed along the circumference, and an ancestral hall or courtyard in the center, with a centripetal and cohesive spatial character, known as the 'Eastern Ancient Castle'.
典型实例 Examples
华安二宜楼、南靖怀远楼、平和绳武楼、永定承启楼
方楼
Square Tulou (Fanglou)
土楼的主要形式之一，平面呈方形或长方形，四角设瞭望台，内部沿四壁布置居住单元，中心为祖堂和天井，是土楼中数量最多的形式。
One of the main forms of tulou, with a square or rectangular plan, observation platforms at the four corners, residential units arranged along the four walls inside, and an ancestral hall and courtyard in the center; it is the most numerous form of tulou.
典型实例 Examples
南靖和贵楼、平和裕昌楼、永定奎聚楼、南靖龙潭楼
五凤楼
Five-Phoenix Building (Wufenglou)
土楼的特殊形式之一，由前低后高的三堂或五堂建筑组成，屋顶层层叠落如凤凰展翅，强调中轴对称和等级秩序，常见于永定地区，是客家建筑中最具礼制特征的类型。
One of the special forms of tulou, composed of three or five hall buildings arranged from low in front to high in back, with roofs layered like a phoenix spreading its wings, emphasizing central-axis symmetry and hierarchical order; commonly seen in the Yongding region, it is the type with the most ritual characteristics in Hakka architecture.
典型实例 Examples
永定大夫第、永定福裕楼、永定奎聚楼
红砖厝
Red-Brick House (Hongzhuan Cuo)
闽南地区特有的传统民居形式，以红砖为主要建筑材料，配合白石基座、燕尾脊、出砖入石墙面，色彩鲜艳，装饰华丽，是泉州、漳州、厦门地区最具代表性的民居类型。
A traditional residential form unique to the Minnan region, using red bricks as the main building material,配合 white stone foundations, swallowtail ridges, and brick-and-stone walls, with bright colors and gorgeous decoration; it is the most representative residential type in the Quanzhou, Zhangzhou and Xiamen regions.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、南安中宪第、漳州杨阿苗民居、厦门鼓浪屿老建筑
骑楼
Arcade Building (Qilou)
闽南地区近代商住建筑形式，建筑底层沿街面后退留出公共人行空间，形成连续的廊道，可遮阳避雨，是东南亚建筑风格与中国传统建筑结合的产物，常见于泉州、漳州、厦门的老街。
A modern commercial-residential architectural form in the Minnan region, where the ground floor of buildings recedes along the street to leave a public pedestrian space, forming a continuous corridor that provides shade and shelter from rain; it is a product of the combination of Southeast Asian architectural styles and traditional Chinese architecture, commonly seen in the old streets of Quanzhou, Zhangzhou and Xiamen.
典型实例 Examples
泉州中山路骑楼、漳州台湾路骑楼、厦门中山路骑楼
廊桥
Covered Bridge (Langqiao)
在桥面上加盖廊屋、亭阁的桥梁，兼具交通、休憩、祭祀、交易等功能。福建宁德地区是中国木拱廊桥最集中的分布区，被誉为'世界木拱廊桥之乡'。
Bridges with covered corridors, pavilions and towers built on the bridge deck, serving multiple functions including transportation, rest, sacrifice and trade. The Ningde region of Fujian is the most concentrated distribution area of Chinese timber-arch covered bridges, known as the 'Hometown of World Timber-Arch Covered Bridges'.
典型实例 Examples
宁德万安桥、宁德鸾峰桥、宁德千乘桥、宁德龙江桥
木拱廊桥
Timber-Arch Covered Bridge (Mugong Langqiao)
廊桥中技术含量最高的类型，以短木料通过榫卯搭接形成拱形结构，不用一根铆钉，被誉为'世界桥梁史上的奇迹'。宁德屏南、寿宁、周宁三县保存最为集中。
The most technically advanced type of covered bridge, where short timber pieces are joined through mortise and tenon to form an arch structure without a single rivet, known as a 'miracle in the history of world bridges'. They are most concentrated in Pingnan, Shouning and Zhouning counties of Ningde.
典型实例 Examples
屏南万安桥、寿宁鸾峰桥、周宁登龙桥、古田沉字桥
庄寨
Fortified Manor (Zhuangzhai)
福建闽中地区特有的防御性民居建筑，结合了土楼的防御功能和民居的居住功能，多建于山坡之上，平面不规则，外墙高大厚实，内部院落层层递进，永泰县保存最为集中。
Defensive residential architecture unique to the central Fujian region, combining the defensive function of tulou with the residential function of houses, mostly built on hillsides with irregular plans, tall and thick outer walls, and progressively layered interior courtyards; they are most concentrated in Yongtai County.
典型实例 Examples
永泰爱荆庄、永泰嘉禄庄、永泰仁和庄、闽清宏琳厝
土堡
Earthen Fort (Tubao)
福建三明地区特有的防御性建筑，以生土夯筑外墙，内部为木结构，平面多为前方后圆或不规则形，具有强烈的军事防御色彩，与土楼相比更侧重防御功能。
Defensive architecture unique to the Sanming region of Fujian, with rammed earth outer walls and timber structure interiors, mostly with front-square-back-round or irregular plans, with strong military defensive characteristics; compared to tulou, they emphasize defensive functions more.
典型实例 Examples
大田安良堡、大田芳联堡、尤溪聚奎堡、永安安贞堡
16
装饰工艺 Decorative Crafts
剪瓷雕
Porcelain-Cut Sculpture (Jiancidiao)
闽南地区特有的建筑装饰工艺，将彩色瓷碗、瓷盘剪切成碎片，粘贴在屋脊、檐角等部位，塑造出人物、花鸟、龙凤等形象，色彩鲜艳，经久不褪，是闽南寺庙建筑的标志性装饰。
A distinctive architectural decorative craft in the Minnan region, where colored porcelain bowls and plates are cut into fragments and pasted on ridges, eaves corners and other parts, creating figures of people, flowers, birds, dragons and phoenixes, with bright colors that do not fade over time; it is the iconic decoration of Minnan temple architecture.
典型实例 Examples
东山关帝庙、泉州开元寺、厦门青礁慈济宫、漳州白礁慈济宫
惠安石雕
Hui'an Stone Carving
福建惠安地区传承千年的石雕工艺，以花岗岩为主要材料，技法包括圆雕、浮雕、透雕、线刻等，题材广泛，工艺精湛，被誉为'中国石雕之都'，广泛应用于闽南建筑的柱、础、栏、碑等部位。
A stone carving craft with a thousand-year heritage in the Hui'an region of Fujian, using granite as the main material, with techniques including round carving, relief, openwork carving and line carving, with a wide range of subjects and exquisite craftsmanship, known as the 'Capital of Chinese Stone Carving'; widely applied to columns, bases, railings and steles in Minnan architecture.
典型实例 Examples
泉州东西塔、厦门胡里山炮台、漳州石牌坊、惠安崇武古城
出砖入石
Brick-and-Stone Wall (Chuzhuan Rushi)
闽南地区特有的墙体砌筑工艺，将红砖和白色花岗岩块交错砌筑，形成红白相间的肌理效果，既美观又坚固，是闽南红砖建筑的标志性特征之一。
A distinctive wall masonry craft in the Minnan region, where red bricks and white granite blocks are alternately laid, forming a red-and-white textured effect, both beautiful and sturdy; it is one of the iconic features of Minnan red-brick architecture.
典型实例 Examples
泉州蔡氏古民居、厦门鼓浪屿老建筑、漳州古民居、南安中宪第
螭首
Chi-Dragon Head (Chishou)
中国古建筑中常见的石雕装饰，为传说中龙之九子之一的螭首形象，常用于台基、栏杆、柱础等部位，具有排水和装饰双重功能。福建文庙、寺庙中常见螭首排水口。
A common stone carving decoration in traditional Chinese architecture, depicting the head of Chi, one of the nine sons of the dragon in legend, commonly used on platforms, railings and column bases, with both drainage and decorative functions. Chi-dragon head drainage outlets are common in Fujian Confucian temples and temples.
典型实例 Examples
泉州府文庙、漳州府文庙、福州孔庙、莆田文庙
盘龙石柱
Dragon-Coiled Stone Column (Panlong Shizhu)
闽南寺庙建筑中常见的石柱装饰，以整根花岗岩雕刻而成，龙身缠绕柱身，龙头探出，气势雄伟，是惠安石雕工艺的杰出代表，常见于寺庙大殿前廊。
A common stone column decoration in Minnan temple architecture, carved from a single piece of granite, with the dragon body coiling around the column and the dragon head protruding, with majestic momentum; it is an outstanding representative of Hui'an stone carving craft, commonly seen in the front corridors of temple main halls.
典型实例 Examples
泉州开元寺、厦门青礁慈济宫、东山关帝庙、平和城隍庙
垂花柱
Pendant Column Capital (Chuihuazhu)
中国古建筑中檐下的装饰构件，从梁枋下悬垂的短柱，柱头雕刻成莲花、花篮等形状，具有装饰和象征意义，福建闽南建筑中常用木雕垂花柱。
A decorative component under the eaves in traditional Chinese architecture, a short column hanging from below the beam lintel, with the column capital carved into lotus, flower basket and other shapes, with decorative and symbolic meanings; wood-carved pendant column capitals are commonly used in Minnan architecture.
典型实例 Examples
长泰杨氏宗祠、东山关帝庙、漳州威惠庙、泉州蔡氏古民居

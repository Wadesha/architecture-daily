绪论 Introduction

甘肃地处黄土高原、青藏高原和内蒙古高原三大高原的交汇地带，地形呈狭长状，东西蜿蜒1600多公里。独特的地理环境孕育了丰富多样的传统村落形态。本书按照地理区域将甘肃传统村落分为陇中地区、陇东南地区、甘南地区和河西地区四大片区，共收录57个传统村落。

Gansu lies at the intersection of the Loess Plateau, Tibetan Plateau, and Inner Mongolian Plateau, stretching 1,600km east-west. Its unique geography has nurtured diverse traditional village forms. This book divides Gansu's traditional villages into four regions — Central Gansu, Southeastern Gansu, Southern Gansu, and Hexi Corridor — covering 57 villages.

每个村落从基本信息、选址格局、传统建筑、非物质文化遗产、人居环境现状五个维度进行详细记述，全面展现甘肃传统村落的历史文化价值和地域特色。

Each village is documented across five dimensions: basic information, site and layout, traditional architecture, intangible cultural heritage, and current living environment, comprehensively presenting the historical, cultural, and regional characteristics of Gansu's traditional villages.

河口村

Hekou Village · 兰州市西固区
始建年代 Founded明清时期 Ming-Qing
村落形态 Form庄河堡 Fortified village
核心建筑 Key Buildings四大城楼、钟鼓楼、张公祠、昌荣商店
非遗 ICH铁芯子

选址与格局 Site & Layout

河口村位于兰州市西固区，地处庄浪河与黄河交汇处，是古丝绸之路的重要渡口和商埠。村落以庄河堡为核心，设四大城楼，钟鼓楼位于堡内中心，形成典型的堡寨式格局。

Located in Xigu District, Lanzhou, at the confluence of the Zhuanglang River and Yellow River, Hekou was an important ferry and trading post on the ancient Silk Road. The village centers on Zhuanghe Fort with four gate towers and a central drum tower, forming a typical fortified village layout.

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

庄河堡四大城楼保存较为完整，钟鼓楼为堡内标志性建筑。张公祠纪念清代地方官员，昌荣商店为近代商号建筑，体现了河口作为黄河上游商埠的商业繁荣。

The four gate towers of Zhuanghe Fort are well-preserved, with the drum tower as the landmark. Zhang Gong Shrine commemorates a Qing official, and Changrong Store is a modern commercial building reflecting Hekou's prosperity as an upper Yellow River trading port.

非物质文化遗产 Intangible Cultural Heritage

铁芯子铁芯子是河口村传统社火表演形式，将孩童固定在铁制支架上，装扮成戏曲人物，由人抬着游行，造型惊险奇特。

Tiexinzi (iron-frame floats) is a traditional festival parade where children are fixed on iron frames dressed as opera characters, carried in procession with dramatic, gravity-defying poses.

连城村

Liancheng Village · 兰州市永登县
核心建筑 Key Building鲁土司衙门 33,000㎡ / 226间 / 8,848㎡建筑面积
始建年代 Founded明洪武十一年（1378年）
文物级别 Heritage全国重点文物保护单位
非遗 ICH四月初八浴佛节、妙音寺跳禅、永登舞狮

选址与格局 Site & Layout

连城村位于永登县连城镇，地处大通河畔，是鲁土司衙门所在地。鲁土司是明清时期甘肃最大的土司之一，统治连城地区长达560余年（1378-1932年），共历19世22代土司。

Liancheng Village in Yongdeng County sits on the Datong River, home to the Lu Tusi (chieftain) Yamen. The Lu Tusi was one of Gansu's largest chieftaincies, ruling for 560+ years (1378-1932) across 19 generations and 22 chieftains.

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

鲁土司衙门占地33,000平方米，共有房屋226间，建筑面积8,848平方米，是西北地区保存最完整的土司衙门建筑群。衙门沿中轴线布置，依次为大照壁、牌坊、仪门、大堂、燕喜堂、祖先堂等，气势恢宏。妙音寺位于衙门西侧，为藏传佛教寺院，是鲁土司家族的家庙。

The Lu Tusi Yamen covers 33,000 sqm with 226 rooms (8,848 sqm floor area), the best-preserved chieftain yamen complex in Northwest China. Arranged along a central axis: screen wall, paifang, ceremonial gate, main hall, Yanxi Hall, and ancestral hall. Miaoyin Monastery to the west is a Tibetan Buddhist temple and the Lu family's ancestral temple.

非物质文化遗产 Intangible Cultural Heritage

四月初八浴佛节每年农历四月初八，妙音寺举行盛大的浴佛节法会。跳禅僧人戴面具跳法舞。永登舞狮永登县传统民间舞蹈。

On the 8th day of the 4th lunar month, Miaoyin Monastery holds the grand Buddha Bathing Festival. Cham dance (masked religious dance) is performed. Yongdeng lion dance is a traditional folk dance.

青城村

Qingcheng Village · 兰州市榆中县
核心建筑 Key Buildings城隍庙(1038年)、青城书院(1831年)、高氏祠堂(1785年)、罗家大院
文物级别 Heritage中国历史文化名村
非遗 ICH青城小调(35曲调)、道台狮子(1873年)、水烟制作工艺

选址与格局 Site & Layout

青城村位于榆中县北部黄河南岸，古称条城，是古丝绸之路上的水烟集散地。青城因宋仁宗年间秦州刺史狄青巡边时所筑而得名。村落沿黄河分布，明清一条街保存完整。

Qingcheng Village on the south bank of the Yellow River in Yuzhong County, anciently called Tiaocheng, was a water-tobacco distribution center on the Silk Road. Named after Di Qing, a Song dynasty general. The village stretches along the Yellow River with a well-preserved Ming-Qing main street.

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

城隍庙始建于1038年（北宋仁宗宝元元年），是青城最古老的建筑。青城书院建于1831年（清道光十一年），是兰州六大书院之一。高氏祠堂建于1785年（清乾隆五十年），砖木结构，雕梁画栋。罗家大院为典型的西北四合院，是青城水烟商的宅邸。

Chenghuang Temple (1038, Northern Song) is Qingcheng's oldest building. Qingcheng Academy (1831) was one of Lanzhou's six major academies. Gao Clan Shrine (1785) is a brick-wood structure with ornate carvings. Luo Family Compound is a typical northwest courtyard house of a water-tobacco merchant.

非物质文化遗产 Intangible Cultural Heritage

青城小调青城小调有35个曲调，是当地民间说唱艺术。道台狮子始于1873年，由青城籍道台（省一级官员）引入。水烟制作工艺青城是西北水烟的主要产地，水烟制作工艺世代相传。

Qingcheng xiaodiao has 35 tunes as a local narrative-singing art. Daotai lion dance (1873) was introduced by a Qingcheng-born provincial official. Water-tobacco craftsmanship: Qingcheng was the northwest's main water-tobacco producer, with craft passed down generations.

永丰村

Yongfeng Village · 兰州市榆中县
核心建筑 Key Buildings永丰堡(1820年)、弥陀寺(641年)、金造家祠(1893年)、金家宅院(509㎡)
非遗 ICH七月官神、水烟手工技艺

选址与格局 Site & Layout

永丰村位于榆中县，永丰堡建于1820年（清嘉庆二十五年），为堡寨式村落。弥陀寺始建于641年（唐贞观十五年），历史悠久。金氏家族为村中望族，金造家祠建于1893年（清光绪十九年）。

Yongfeng Village in Yuzhong County features Yongfeng Fort (1820), a fortified village. Mituo Temple dates to 641 (Tang Zhenguan 15). The Jin clan is prominent; Jin Zao Shrine was built in 1893 (Guangxu 19).

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

金家宅院占地509平方米，为典型的陇中四合院，坐北朝南，双坡屋顶。弥陀寺历经多次重修，现存建筑为清代风格。永丰堡堡墙保存部分残段。

Jin Family Compound (509 sqm) is a typical Central Gansu siheyuan, south-facing with double-slope roofs. Mituo Temple, rebuilt multiple times, survives in Qing style. Parts of Yongfeng Fort's walls remain.

非物质文化遗产 Intangible Cultural Heritage

七月官神每年农历七月举行的迎神赛会活动。水烟手工技艺与青城村一脉相承。

Qiyue Guanshen: a deity-welcoming festival held every 7th lunar month. Handmade water-tobacco craft shares lineage with Qingcheng.

凤山村

Fengshan Village · 天水市秦安县
核心建筑 Key Buildings泰山庙(21,527.4㎡, 31座殿宇)、兴国寺(1330年)、文庙大成殿(1297年)
传统民居5处传统民居

选址与格局 Site & Layout

凤山村位于秦安县城东南的凤山脚下，因山形如凤展翅而得名。村落依山而建，泰山庙古建筑群位于凤山之上，是秦安县城的标志性景观。

Fengshan Village at the foot of Feng (Phoenix) Mountain southeast of Qin'an County town, named for the mountain's phoenix-wing shape. The village climbs the hillside; the Taishan Temple complex atop the mountain is Qin'an's landmark.

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

泰山庙古建筑群占地21,527.4平方米，共有31座殿宇，是甘肃保存较完整的明清道教建筑群。兴国寺始建于1330年（元至顺元年），现存般若殿为元代建筑，是甘肃最早的木构建筑之一。文庙大成殿始建于1297年（元大德元年），历代重修。村中保存5处传统民居。

Taishan Temple complex covers 21,527.4 sqm with 31 halls, one of Gansu's best-preserved Ming-Qing Daoist complexes. Xingguo Temple (1330, Yuan) retains its Yuan-era Bore Hall, among Gansu's earliest timber structures. Confucian Temple Dacheng Hall (1297, Yuan) has been rebuilt through dynasties. Five traditional dwellings survive in the village.

村落形态 Form古略阳城，八卦形布局
核心建筑 Key Buildings明清一条街(603米,114间)、女娲祠、关帝庙、西番寺(1385年)

娲皇村位于秦安县陇城镇，是古略阳城所在地。村落呈八卦形布局，明清一条街长603米，有114间传统商铺。这里是传说中女娲的诞生地，被誉为"娲皇故里"。

Wahuang Village in Longcheng Town, Qin'an County, is the site of ancient Lüeyang City. The village follows a bagua (eight-trigram) layout, with a 603m Ming-Qing main street lined by 114 traditional shops. Legend holds this as Nüwa's birthplace, called 'Home of the Empress Nüwa.'

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

女娲祠历经四迁五建，现存建筑为近年复建。关帝庙位于街中。西番寺始建于1385年（明洪武十八年），为藏传佛教风格寺院。9处传统民居分布在街巷两侧。

Nüwa Shrine has been relocated and rebuilt four/five times; current buildings are recent reconstructions. Guandi Temple stands on the main street. Xifan Temple (1385, Hongwu 18) is in Tibetan Buddhist style. Nine traditional dwellings line the streets.

非物质文化遗产 Intangible Cultural Heritage

女娲祭祀典礼国家级非物质文化遗产，每年农历三月十五日举行，是全国规模最大的女娲祭祀活动。

Nüwa Sacrifice Ceremony, a national-level ICH, is held annually on the 15th day of the 3rd lunar month — China's largest Nüwa worship event.

Xingquan Village · 天水市秦安县

邢泉村位于秦安县，因村中有邢泉而得名。可泉寺始建于宋代，明代学者胡缵宗曾在此读书并写下《可泉诗》。村落以圣母宫为中心，戏台正对宫庙。

Xingquan Village in Qin'an County is named for its Xing Spring. Kequan Temple, founded in the Song dynasty, was where Ming scholar Hu Zanzong studied and wrote 'Kequan Poems.' The village centers on the Shengmu (Holy Mother) Palace with an opera stage facing it.

非物质文化遗产 Intangible Cultural Heritage

圣母庙会每年春秋两季各演一台戏，1758年（清乾隆二十三年）定规，延续至今。庙会期间唱秦腔，是当地重要的民俗活动。

Shengmu Temple Fair: two opera performances annually (spring and autumn), regulated in 1758 (Qianlong 23) and continuing today. Qinqiang opera is performed, a key local folk event.

蔡家寺村位于甘谷县，蔡家寺始建于元至正年间（1341-1370年），明天启七年（1627年）木刻经板220片保存至今。村落因寺得名，寺院位于村中心。

Caijiasi Village in Gangu County; Caijia Temple was founded in the Yuan Zhizheng era (1341-1370). 220 wood-printed scripture blocks from 1627 (Tianqi 7) survive. The village is named after the temple, which stands at its center.

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

蔡家寺共有30余处建筑，全部为悬山顶，是甘肃保存较完整的元明清寺院建筑群。木刻经板刻于明天启七年，是珍贵的佛教印刷文物。村中保存11处传统民居。

Caijia Temple has 30+ buildings, all with overhanging gable roofs — a well-preserved Yuan-Ming-Qing temple complex. The 1627 woodblock scriptures are rare Buddhist printing artifacts. Eleven traditional dwellings survive.

Zhangjiaping Village in Gangu County has the legend of female zhuangyuan (top scholar) Zhang Yujing, who in 1405 was conferred the title by the Yongle Emperor for her literary and martial prowess. Wafang Village (1387, Hongwu 20) and Linggui Mountain are nearby.

Juehuangsi Village in Gangu County features an ancient scholar tree (Sophora japonica), legendarily planted by Tang Emperor Taizong Li Shimin, ~1,000 years old, 7m trunk diameter, 30m tall — a famous heritage tree. Juehuang Temple was founded in the Hongwu era and rebuilt in 1890 (Guangxu 16).

觉皇寺大殿进深4.07米，高5.75米，保存明代建筑风格。村中保存14处传统院落。

Juehuang Temple's main hall has a 4.07m depth and 5.75m height, retaining Ming style. Fourteen traditional courtyards survive.

正月十五社火三月二十迎春会七月十七李广诞辰——纪念西汉名将李广（甘谷是李广故里）。

Shehuo on the 15th of the 1st lunar month; Spring Welcoming Festival on the 20th of the 3rd; Li Guang Birthday on the 17th of the 7th — commemorating Western Han general Li Guang, a native of Gangu.

上下街村位于武山县滩歌镇，北宋真宗大中祥符七年（1014年）设威远寨，熙宁八年（1075年）废为镇。明清一条街长620米，有280间传统商铺，是武山县保存最完整的古街区。

Shangxiajie Village in Tange Town, Wushan County: Weiyuan Fort was established in 1014 (Song Zhenzong Dazhong Xiangfu 7) and demoted to town in 1075 (Xining 8). The 620m Ming-Qing main street with 280 traditional shops is Wushan's best-preserved historic district.

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

沿街建筑多为双檐深屋，砖雕饰头，体现了陇南商贸集镇的建筑特色。11处传统民居保存较好。1930年王占林回军追杀的历史事件曾发生于此。

Street buildings feature double-eave deep structures with brick-carved ornamentation, reflecting the commercial town architecture of southern Gansu. Eleven traditional dwellings survive. The 1930 Wang Zhanlin Muslim army pursuit event occurred here.

Tange gaojiaozi (stilt walking). Jiaban dance: folk dance with clapper boards.

永泰村

Yongtai Village · 白银市景泰县
始建年代 Founded明万历年间（1573-1620年）
村落形态 Form永泰城（龟城），墙周长1710米，面积22.59公顷
文物级别 Heritage全国重点文物保护单位
防御设施护城河6米宽2.5米深，五脏五井
非遗 ICH社火、背鼓子舞、景泰滚灯、景泰拉花、寿鹿山道教音乐（5项）

选址与格局 Site & Layout

永泰村位于景泰县，永泰城因形似金龟而俗称"龟城"，建于明万历年间，是明代军事防御要塞。城墙周长1710米，面积22.59公顷，城外有护城河宽6米、深2.5米。城内有"五脏五井"的独特布局，五眼井对应龟的五脏，是古代军事城市规划的杰作。

Yongtai Village in Jingtai County: Yongtai City, commonly called 'Turtle City' for its golden-turtle shape, was built in the Wanli era (1573-1620) as a Ming military fortress. Walls measure 1,710m around, enclosing 22.59 hectares, with a 6m-wide 2.5m-deep moat. The unique 'five organs, five wells' layout — five wells corresponding to a turtle's internal organs — is a masterpiece of ancient military urban planning.

传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

城内保存10处历史建筑，包括城门、炮台、民居等。城墙为夯土筑成，是甘肃保存最完整的明代堡寨之一。

Ten historic buildings survive within, including gates, gun emplacements, and dwellings. The rammed-earth walls are among Gansu's best-preserved Ming forts.

非物质文化遗产 Intangible Cultural Heritage

Huoshaozhai Village in Kuanchuan Town, Li County: named for a great fire, legendarily where Zhuge Liang burned Sima Yi (historically in Qishan, Shaanxi). Destroyed in 1958; only Taishan Temple (rebuilt 1997), ancient soapberry tree, rammed-earth wall fragments, and a 1904 restoration stele remain. 传统建筑 Traditional Architecture

传统民居最早用石块搭建，后逐步转变为土木结构。早期有柱廊，屋檐与窗户都有繁复的雕花，屋内陈设有八仙椅；后简化为檐廊且廊内距离缩小，雕饰也被简化。院落采用合院式布局，院内干净整洁种有花草，大门前种植竹子（寓意常青坚强乐观）。院墙大多一人高，夯土筑成局部兼用砖块。大门门头写有"耕第""读安祥居"等字样。

Traditional dwellings were first built with stone, later transitioning to earthy-wood. Early versions had column corridors with ornate eaves, window carvings, and 'eight-immortal' chairs; later simplified to eave corridors with reduced depth and simpler ornamentation. Courtyards are clean with flowers; bamboo planted before gates (symbolizing resilience). Walls are mostly head-high rammed earth with some brick. Gate inscriptions include 'Gengdi' (farming) and 'Du'an Xiangju' (peaceful study dwelling).

河西地区指张掖、武威、酒泉、嘉峪关，因位于黄河以西自古称河西，处于西北干旱区和青藏高原边缘，祁连山与北部山系间狭长天然走廊，亦称河西走廊。曾是佛教东传要道、丝路西去咽喉，先秦马家窑齐家文化，汉武帝初设武威酒泉二郡后分张掖敦煌，晋朝前后南北西大凉均在此建都。

Xiakou Village · 张掖市山丹县老军乡
历史地位古丝绸之路重要驿站，汉昭帝始元二年前85年置日勒都尉屯兵，明万历元年1573年重修加固，万历二年1574年都司赵良臣题"硖口古城堡"
核心建筑硖口古城(东西400多米南北300多米长方形,19万㎡,东西两门一条街中轴线)、汉明长城(近200公里平行延伸相距10-80米,国家文保,露天长城博物馆)、锁控金川摩崖(明嘉靖三十二年1553年陈斐题,高3米宽2米)、过街楼(明代,悬山顶"威镇乾坤"匾)、暗门(5处)
非遗九曲黄河灯阵、硖口宝卷(五种毛笔手抄本)、洗毡技艺

选址与格局

硖口村位于山丹县老军乡，焉支山北麓，距县城40公里，村域48平方公里，汉明长城与G30高速312国道平行排列。古丝绸之路重要驿站，中原通往西域必经之地，丝绸之路咽喉。汉昭帝始元二年（前85年）为防御匈奴置日勒都尉屯兵设防移民屯田，明清扩大防卫属山丹卫，明万历元年（1573年）巡抚都御史廖逢节重修加固，万历二年（1574年）都司赵良臣题名"硖口古城堡"。

Xiakou in Shandan Laojun: north foot of Yanzhi Mountain, 40km from county, 48 sqkm, Han-Ming Great Wall parallel with G30/Hwy 312. Key Silk Road post, only passage from Central Plains to Western Regions. 85 BCE: Rile Commander established against Xiongnu with troops and colonists; Ming-Qing under Shandan Garrison; 1573 Liao Fengjie rebuilt/fortified; 1574 Zhao Liangchen inscribed 'Xiakou Ancient Fort.'

传统建筑

硖口古城东西400多米南北300多米长方形加西关城外城共19万平方米，开东西两门仅一条东西走向街道纵贯全城为中轴线，将民舍衙府寺庙店铺营房一分为二。汉明长城在山丹境内绵延近200公里，壕式汉长城在北侧土筑明长城在里，相距10-80米平行延伸，不同年代同时并行至今留存完整国内绝无仅有，国家重点文保，专家誉为"露天长城博物馆"。石硖谷有汉壕沟现存烽燧10座。据《甘州府志》境内明长城五处留有暗门，暗门上建城楼，行人需守兵稽查出入。锁控金川摩崖在硖口城东约一华里石硖谷，明嘉靖三十二年（1553年）鄢陵进士陈斐观察河西兵防见两山对峙题"锁控金川"四字，镌于高3米宽2米垂直石崖，字各0.40平方米，羊鹿门石壁还有"天现鹿羊"大字和匈奴羌族牧人腰刀镌刻岩画。过街楼明代始建，南北中心街道偏南，路两旁石条砖石砌面黄土夯筑墩台间距4米，两台阶架过梁铺木板上建木结构小楼，悬大匾楷书"威镇乾坤"。

Xiakou Ancient City: 400+m E-W, 300+m N-S, rectangle plus west outer city, 190,000 sqm, only east-west gates and one street as central axis dividing dwellings/yamen/temples/shops/barracks. Han-Ming Great Wall: ~200km in Shandan, moat-style Han wall north, rammed-earth Ming wall inside, 10-80m apart parallel — unique in China, national heritage, 'Open-air Great Wall Museum.' Han moat in gorge with 10 surviving beacon towers. Per Ganzhou Gazetteer: five secret gates in Ming wall with gate towers, requiring guard inspection. 'Suokong Jinchuan' cliff inscription: 1553, Chen Fei (Jinshi from Yanling) inscribed four characters on 3m×2m vertical cliff, characters 0.40 sqm each; Yanglu Gate cliff also has 'Tianxian Luyang' and Xiongnu/Qiang shepherd rock carvings. Street-crossing tower (Ming): south of central N-S street, two rammed-earth platforms 4m apart with timber beams/planks and wooden pavilion, plaque 'Power Over Heaven and Earth.'

非物质文化遗产

九曲黄河灯阵山丹民间社火独有形式，上百年历史，正月十五灯阵内社火交流表演，形式复杂风格独特阵容宏大。硖口宝卷河西宝卷重要组成，约五种毛笔手抄本，唐代敦煌变文俗讲宋代说经基础发展，儒释道三教合一，散韵相间，庙会家庭院落说唱，电视出现前农村主要文化。洗毡马祥福手艺，洗毛毡毡帽毡袄毡鞋，几百年历史，改革开放前放牧人依靠毛毡挡风遮雨，老军陈户一带有声望。

Nine-bend Yellow River lantern array: unique Shandan shehuo form, 100+ years, 15th of 1st month performances in lantern array, complex and grand. Xiakou Baojuan: key part of Hexi Baojuan, ~5 hand-copied versions, from Tang Dunhuang transformation texts, three-religion syncretism, prose-verse, performed at temples/homes, main rural culture before TV. Felt washing: Ma Xiangfu's craft, washing wool felt/hats/coats/shoes, centuries old, essential for herders before reform era, reputable in Laojun/Chenhu.

三陶村

Santao Village · 武威市民勤县三雷镇
核心建筑瑞安堡(1936-38年建,王庆云私人庄堡,5036.85㎡,2394㎡,8院落7亭台140余间,国家级文保,小皇城)、徐氏民宅(1940年建,25间,民勤最完整普通民居)
建筑特征庄院式民居，厚墙围廊堡门锁喉，巡道环绕堡楼相护，暗道固可守破可逃
非遗民勤小曲子(镇番小曲,三小戏,地蹦子特色)

选址与格局

三陶村位于民勤县三雷镇，近代河湖冲积洪积淤积平原，最早移民居住，汉代移民戍边最早陶姓人认领开垦得名陶乡，清代扩大分上陶中陶新陶三陶四村，新中国成立后沿用。瑞安堡位于村西南3.5公里，民国二十七年（1938年）建成，原国民党地方保安团长民勤大富绅王庆云私人庄堡，俗称王团堡子，2006年国家级文保。距红崖山水库30公里青土湖65公里。

Santao in Minqin Sanlei: alluvial plain, earliest immigrant settlement, Han-era military colonists with Tao surname claiming land → Taoxiang; Qing expanded to four villages (Shang/Zhong/Xin/San Tao); name retained after 1949. Rui'an Fort, 3.5km southwest, completed 1938, private residence of Wang Qingyun (Kuomintang local security regiment commander, wealthy gentry), aka Wang Tuan Baozi, national heritage 2006. 30km from Hongyashan Reservoir, 65km from Qingtu Lake.

传统建筑

瑞安堡1936年动工设计约1935年，堡身坐北向南长92.25米宽54.6米占地5036.85平方米建筑面积2394平方米，大小院落8个亭台楼阁7座高脊瓦房140余间。主院三进：前院过院住卫兵长工置车马；中院四合院办公待客；后院内院主人生活娱乐。环庄院四周底部厚7米顶宽2.8米高12米土夯墙（黏土沙石灰混合分层夯实夹红柳枝大城砖加固），上筑门楼文楼武楼望月庭逍遥宫双喜楼瞭望楼及人行通道。平面三大防御特征：①厚墙围廊堡门锁喉——大门宽9.3米高10.8米条石镶基大城砖门洞洞窟形宽3.3米高3.9米门扇厚16厘米外包铁皮密施铁铆钉，门廊上面阔三间进深一间硬山顶门楼耸立围墙之上两侧角楼相护；②巡道环绕堡楼相护——围墙顶2米宽巡道全长300米，外侧0.5米厚2.2米高砖砌掩体女儿墙（对外射击孔）内侧0.7米护墙，四角和南西北中部分别角楼中楼，向外砖砌墙垛垛出墙面1.5-4米，堡楼墙垛射击孔形成第二道火力防御体系；③布置暗道固可守破可逃——沿围墙地下砖砌拱形暗道可供两人通行总长160多米，出入口分设围墙顶（西墙望月厅地下）、生活区（中后院厢房厕所粮房）、前院马厩、庄院外。曾被称为"小皇城"，是庄院式民居代表作品，河西走廊沙漠边缘抗风沙挡酷暑抵严寒宜人居的庄院式城堡。徐氏民宅1940年建（王庆云姐夫徐氏住宅），南北60米东西40米，前院后院小院3个院落房屋25间，前院草院后院生活区，后院回廊四绕堂屋面阔三间进深一间硬山顶青砖铺顶，高墙大院土木结构，民勤县域内保存最完整普通民居。

Rui'an Fort: construction began 1936 (design ~1935), south-facing, 92.25m×54.6m, 5,036.85 sqm site, 2,394 sqm floor, 8 courtyards, 7 pavilions/towers, 140+ high-ridge tile rooms. Three main courtyards: front (guards/workers/carriages), middle (office/reception siheyuan), rear (inner family living). Surrounding walls: 7m base, 2.8m top, 12m high, rammed with clay/sand/lime mixed with tamarisk branches, reinforced with large bricks; topped with gate towers, Wen/Wu towers, Wangyue Pavilion, Xiaoyao Palace, Shuangxi Tower, watchtowers, and walkways. Three defensive features: 1) Thick walls/gated throat: gate 9.3m wide 10.8m high, stone base, large brick cave-style portal 3.3m×3.9m, 16cm-thick iron-sheathed doors with iron rivets, gate porch with 3-bay flush-gable tower flanked by corner towers; 2) Encircling patrol path with mutually-supporting towers: 2m-wide 300m-long patrol path atop walls, outer 0.5m-thick 2.2m-high brick parapet with shooting holes, inner 0.7m wall, corner/central towers with outward brick buttresses 1.5-4m, forming second fire defense line; 3) Secret passages for defense/escape: brick-vaulted underground passages along walls for two people, 160+m total, entrances at wall top (under Wangyue Hall), living quarters (rear wing/toilet/granary), front stable, and outside fort. Called 'Little Imperial City,' representative manor dwelling on the Hexi desert edge resisting sand/heat/cold. Xu Residence (1940, Wang Qingyun's brother-in-law): 60m×40m, 3 courtyards, 25 rooms, front for grass/livestock, rear for living, rear courtyard with wraparound corridors, 3-bay flush-gable main hall with blue brick roof, high-walled earthy-wood compound — Minqin's best-preserved common dwelling.

非物质文化遗产

民勤小曲子最初称镇番小曲，曲调丰富优美动听，流行于民勤城乡的曲牌体地方小剧种，道白用民勤方言诙谐通俗亲切，表演带地蹦子特色（男角蹦蹦跳跳女角摇摇摆摆），最初仅小生小旦小丑三小戏，清末生旦净丑俱全，著名艺人曹开兴高培阁周玉文田志书陈生致等。

Minqin xiaoqu (originally Zhenfan xiaoqu): rich melodic tunes, qupai-style local opera popular in Minqin, dialogue in Minqin dialect (humorous, intimate), performance with dibengzi features (male roles bouncing, female roles swaying), initially only three small roles (xiaosheng/xiaodan/xiaochou), late Qing expanded to all roles, famous artists Cao Kaixing, Gao Peige, Zhou Yuwen, Tian Zhishu, Chen Shengzhi.

结语 Conclusion

甘肃传统村落地域特色鲜明：陇中地区多选在塬上，坐北向南双坡屋顶；陇东南地区择水而居背山面水，开敞式院落沿山势布局斜坡屋顶；甘南地区择群而居，墙体较厚开间进深较大平屋顶为主且屋顶可上人；河西地区首要考虑交通便捷，庄园式布局为主防御和保暖是共性。公共建筑见证着传统村落的变迁与发展史。

Gansu's traditional villages have distinct regional characteristics: Central Gansu on loess plateaus with south-facing double-slope roofs; Southeastern Gansu by water with mountains at back, open courtyards on slopes with sloped roofs; Southern Gansu clustered with thick walls, large bays, accessible flat roofs; Hexi Corridor prioritizing transportation with manor layouts emphasizing defense and insulation. Public buildings witness the transformation and development of traditional villages.

传统村落根植于地域环境中，"一带一路"倡议下甘肃城镇化进程加快，传统村落保护机遇与挑战并存。本书通过对57个传统村落的系统记录，为甘肃传统村落的保护与传承提供基础资料。

Traditional villages are rooted in their regional environments. Under the Belt and Road Initiative, Gansu's urbanization accelerates, bringing both opportunities and challenges for traditional village preservation. This book's systematic documentation of 57 villages provides foundational material for their protection and transmission.

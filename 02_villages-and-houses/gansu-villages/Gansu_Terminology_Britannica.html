堡寨

bǎo zhài · Fortified Village (Baozhai)
名词 n. · 聚落形态

为防御外患而筑的围墙集中居住的村落。甘肃堡寨多为夯土筑墙，设城门、马道、马面等防御设施。其形制可追溯至汉代屯军制度，明清时期达到鼎盛。永泰村龟城、红堡子村、天城村镇夷城均为典型堡寨。

A walled, concentrated settlement built for defense against external threats. Gansu baozhai typically feature rammed-earth walls with gates, ramps, and bastions, traceable to Han dynasty military garrison systems and reaching their peak in the Ming-Qing period. Yongtai Turtle City, Hongbaozi, and Tiancheng's Zhenyi City are representative examples.

另见 See also: 瓮城、马面、烽燧

瓮城

wèng chéng · Barbican (Wengcheng)
名词 n. · 防御设施

城门外的半圆形或方形护门小城，为古代城防体系中的重要组成部分。其功能在于当敌军攻破第一道城门后，仍被围困于瓮城之内，守军可从四面高处射杀。天城村镇夷城南门外设龙虎楼，门前置吊桥，内瓮城至中门用青砖砌成，门扇用铁皮包裹，门额上刻"天城锁钥"四字。

A semicircular or square protective enclosure outside a city gate, a key component of ancient city defense. Its function is to trap enemy forces that breach the first gate within the enclosure, where defenders can fire from elevated positions. Zhenyi City in Tiancheng has a Dragon-Tiger Tower outside the south gate with a drawbridge; the inner barbican is blue-brick with iron-sheathed doors inscribed "Tiancheng Key."

另见 See also: 堡寨、马面

马面

mǎ miàn · Bastion (Mamian)
名词 n. · 防御设施

城墙外侧凸出的墩台，用于防御时从侧面射击攻城之敌，消除城墙下的射击死角。红堡子村东墙中间有马面。瑞安堡围墙角楼和中楼位置向外砖砌局部加宽加厚的护墙墙垛，垛出墙面1.5-4米宽不等，起加固围墙的作用，使建筑外形更显浑厚。

Protruding platforms on the outer side of city walls for flanking fire against besiegers, eliminating blind spots at the wall base. Hongbaozi Village has a mamian at its east wall center. Rui'an Fort's corner and central towers feature outward-projecting brick parapets 1.5-4m wide, reinforcing the walls and enhancing the fortress silhouette.

另见 See also: 堡寨、瓮城

烽燧

fēng suì · Beacon Tower
名词 n. · 防御设施

古代边防报警的信号台，亦称烽火台。白天放烟称"燧"，夜间举火称"烽"。天城村烽燧有五种类型：沿边墩21座、口外墩11座、东路墩7座、西路墩7座、南路墩2座，现保存完好的有9座，其中一座为汉代所建，1962年被列为省级文物保护单位。硖口村汉壕沟壕垄现存烽燧10座。

Ancient border warning signal towers, also known as beacon mounds. Smoke by day ("sui"), fire by night ("feng"). Tiancheng Village has five types totaling 48 forts; 9 remain well-preserved, including one Han dynasty tower listed as provincial heritage in 1962. Xiakou Village has 10 surviving towers along its Han-era moat.

另见 See also: 长城、暗门

长城

cháng chéng · Great Wall
名词 n. · 防御设施

中国古代军事防御工程。山丹县境内汉明长城绵延近200公里，壕式汉长城和土筑夯打的明长城虽建于不同年代，但走向、长度完全相同，汉长城在北侧，明长城在其里，两者相距10-80米平行延伸，被专家誉为"露天长城博物馆"，为国家重点文物保护单位。天城村明长城段长约60里，明嘉靖二十七年（1548年）修筑。

Ancient China's military defense工程. In Shandan County, the Han and Ming Great Walls extend nearly 200km; the moat-style Han Wall and rammed-earth Ming Wall, though built in different eras, share identical alignment and length, running parallel 10-80m apart. Hailed as the "Open-air Great Wall Museum" and a national heritage site. Tiancheng's Ming Wall section is about 30km long, built in 1548 (27th year of Jiajing).

另见 See also: 烽燧、暗门

暗门

àn mén · Secret Gate
名词 n. · 防御设施

长城上的隐蔽出入口。据《甘州府志》记载，山丹境内明长城有五处留有暗门，除此其他地方均无豁口，暗门上建过城楼，南来北往的商客或行人通过时需经守兵稽查方能出入。

Concealed passageways in the Great Wall. According to the Ganzhou Prefecture Gazetteer, Shandan's Ming Great Wall has five secret gates with no other breaches; gate towers were built above them, and merchants or travelers required guard inspection to pass.

另见 See also: 长城、烽燧

暗道

àn dào · Secret Passage
名词 n. · 防御设施

瑞安堡沿围墙地下修有的砖砌拱形通道，可供两人通行，总长160多米。出入口分设在四个区域：围墙顶上西墙中楼望月厅地下、生活区中后院厢房厕所及粮房、前院马厩、庄院外。其设计理念为"固可守、破可逃"，体现了民国时期地方豪强对安全的极致追求。

Brick-vaulted underground passages along Rui'an Fort's walls, wide enough for two, totaling over 160m. Entrances in four zones: beneath the west-wall Wangyue Tower, in the rear wing/toilet/granary, in the front stable, and outside the fort. The design philosophy of "defensible when holding, escapable when breached" reflects the extreme security concerns of Republican-era local strongmen.

另见 See also: 巡道、庄院

巡道

xún dào · Patrol Path
名词 n. · 防御设施

瑞安堡围墙顶布置的2米宽环绕庄院四周的通道，外侧是厚0.5米高2.2米的砖砌掩体女儿墙（上砌对外射击孔），内侧砌0.7米高护墙，全长300米，可到达围墙顶部任何地方。巡道、围墙和堡楼墙垛上的射击孔在庄院平面上形成第二道火力防御体系。

A 2m-wide path atop Rui'an Fort's walls encircling the compound, with a 0.5m-thick 2.2m-high outer brick parapet (with outward shooting holes) and 0.7m inner wall; total length 300m, reaching every point of the wall top. The patrol path, walls, and tower parapet shooting holes form the second line of fire defense in the fort's plan.

另见 See also: 暗道、女儿墙

土木结构

tǔ mù jié gòu · Earthy-wood Structure
名词 n. · 建筑结构

以木材为承重框架、夯土或土坯为围护墙体的建筑结构体系。甘肃传统村落最普遍的结构形式，甘南藏区表现为"外不见木，内不见土"。这种结构体系就地取材，适应了甘肃干旱少雨、木材相对匮乏的自然条件。

A structural system using timber as the load-bearing frame and rammed earth or adobe as enclosing walls. The most common structural form in Gansu traditional villages; in Tibetan areas of Southern Gansu it manifests as "wood concealed outside, earth concealed inside." This system uses locally-sourced materials, adapting to Gansu's arid climate and relative timber scarcity.

另见 See also: 夯土墙、土坯墙、外不见木内不见土

砖木结构

zhuān mù jié gòu · Brick-wood Structure
名词 n. · 建筑结构

以砖为墙体、木材为梁架的建筑结构体系。甘肃清代及民国时期民居中常见，大墩村20世纪七八十年代建筑多为此种形式。相较于土木结构，砖木结构在防火和耐久性方面有所提升。

A structural system using brick for walls and timber for beam frames. Common in Qing dynasty and Republican-era dwellings in Gansu; many 1970s-80s buildings in Dadun Village adopt this form. Compared to earthy-wood structures, brick-wood offers improved fire resistance and durability.

另见 See also: 土木结构

穿斗式构架

chuān dǒu shì gòu jià · Column-and-tie Frame (Chuan-dou)
名词 n. · 建筑结构

中国传统木构架体系之一，柱间不施梁，以穿枋将柱子串接联成一榀房架。其特点是用柱较密，每柱上架檩，不施大梁，适合南方及西南地区。强曲村白马藏族建筑主体采用穿斗式构架，但柱间没有穿枋，而是单独立柱后将檩条直接放置于柱头之上——这是白马藏族对穿斗式的独特变体，主立面位于建筑的山墙面。

One of China's traditional timber frame systems, where columns are connected by horizontal tie-beams rather than beams. Characterized by dense columns supporting purlins without large beams, suitable for southern and southwestern China. Baima Tibetan buildings in Qiangqu use a variant with independent columns and purlins placed directly on column tops without tie-beams — a unique Baima variation with the main facade on the gable end.

另见 See also: 抬梁式构架

抬梁式构架

tái liáng shì gòu jià · Post-and-lintel Frame (Tai-liang)
名词 n. · 建筑结构

中国传统木构架体系之一，在柱上抬梁，梁上安短柱，短柱上又抬梁。其特点是可获得较大的室内空间，是北方官式建筑的主流构架方式。强曲村建筑主体以外的体量使用抬梁式构架。

One of China's traditional timber frame systems, where beams are placed on columns, short columns on beams, and additional beams on short columns. Characterized by larger interior spaces, it is the mainstream framing method for northern official architecture. The auxiliary volumes of Qiangqu Village buildings use post-and-lintel framing.

另见 See also: 穿斗式构架

四合院

sì hé yuàn · Courtyard House (Siheyuan)
名词 n. · 建筑形制

中国传统院落式住宅，由正房、东西厢房、倒座围合而成。甘肃四合院因地域差异呈现不同特征：陇中地区坐北向南双坡屋顶；河西地区高墙厚筑防御性强。天城村古代有钱人家的四合院比较讲究，是一种带前插（俗称雨廊）名曰四廊齐的房子，结构复杂、装饰豪华、雕梁画栋。瑞安堡主院三进，前院为一过院，中院为一四合院，后院是内院。

A traditional Chinese courtyard house enclosed by a main hall, east/west wings, and a reverse room. Regional variations: south-facing double-slope roofs in Central Gansu; high thick defensive walls in Hexi Corridor. Wealthy Tiancheng courtyards featured 'four-corridor' houses with front eaves. Rui'an Fort has three main courtyards: a front passage court, a middle siheyuan, and a rear inner court.

另见 See also: 三合院、庄院

三合院

sān hé yuàn · Three-sided Courtyard (Sanheyuan)
名词 n. · 建筑形制

由正房和左右厢房组成的院落式住宅，无倒座。甘肃传统村落中常见，堂屋居中，旁为两厢，前院后棚（前为居住，后为养殖）。天城村居住建筑均呈三合院形式。火烧寨村242号为三合院布局，堂屋土木结构有左右耳房，东西厢房砖木结构。

A courtyard house with a main hall and left/right wings but no reverse room. Common in Gansu villages, with the central hall flanked by wings and a front yard/back shed (front for dwelling, back for livestock). All residential buildings in Tiancheng are sanheyuan. No. 242 in Huoshaozhai is a sanheyuan with an earthy-wood main hall and brick-wood wings.

另见 See also: 四合院

庄院

zhuāng yuàn · Manor Compound
名词 n. · 建筑形制

河西走廊沙漠边缘的高墙厚筑院落式民居，抗风沙、挡酷暑、抵严寒。瑞安堡是庄院式民居中的代表作品，曾被称为"小皇城"。瑞安堡动工于1936年，在一般庄院式民居防匪防盗的基础上，更加凸现了军事防御特征，从平面布置角度具有厚墙围廊堡门锁喉、巡道环绕堡楼相护、布置暗道固可守破可逃三大特征。

High-walled, thickly-built courtyard dwellings on the Hexi Corridor desert edge, resistant to sandstorms, heat, and cold. Rui'an Fort (begun 1936) is a representative example, once called the "Little Imperial City," with three defensive features: thick-walled gated enclosure, encircling patrol paths with mutually-supporting towers, and secret passages for defense or escape.

另见 See also: 四合院、暗道、巡道

踏板房

tà bǎn fáng · Taban House
名词 n. · 建筑形制

宕昌县东峪村特色民居，土木结构，木板压卵石屋顶，夯土墙体，檐廊式结构。有三间单层和三间双层檐廊式两种，48户保存良好，高度低2.3米，开间小2.7米。这种低矮紧凑的形制适应了高海拔（2083-2169米）寒冷气候，屋顶木板压卵石的做法可防止大风掀翻屋面。

A distinctive dwelling in Dongyu Village, Tanchang County: earthy-wood structure, roofs of boards weighted by cobbles, rammed-earth walls, eave-corridor construction. Two types (three-bay single-story and two-story); 48 households preserve them, height 2.3m, bay 2.7m — a low, compact form adapted to high-altitude (2083-2169m) cold climate, with cobble-weighted roofs resisting strong winds.

另见 See also: 土木结构、井口院

井口院

jǐng kǒu yuàn · Well-shaped Courtyard (Jingkou Yuan)
名词 n. · 建筑形制

卓尼县拉力沟村传统民居形式，有二层井口院扇子房和一层井口院扇子房两类。其院落平面呈井字形，房屋围绕中心天井布置，适应了甘南藏区的聚居方式和宗教需求。

A traditional dwelling form in Laligou Village, Zhuoni County, existing as two-story and one-story 'fan-house' variants within a well-shaped courtyard. The courtyard plan is cross-shaped with houses arranged around a central skylight, adapting to the communal living and religious needs of Southern Gansu Tibetan areas.

另见 See also: 踏板房

悬山顶

xuán shān dǐng · Overhanging Gable Roof
名词 n. · 屋顶形制

中国传统屋顶形式之一，屋面双坡，两侧山墙凹进，檩木挑出于山墙之外。其挑出的檩木可保护山墙免受雨水侵蚀。蔡家寺村30余处建筑全为悬山顶。街亭村东城门瞻麓门为二层悬山顶，明代青砖砌筑。

A traditional Chinese roof form with two slopes and recessed gable walls, rafters extending beyond. The overhanging rafters protect gable walls from rain. Over 30 buildings in Caijiasi Village all feature overhanging gable roofs. Jieting Village's east gate Zhanlu Gate is a two-story overhanging gable structure of Ming-dynasty blue brick.

另见 See also: 硬山顶、歇山顶

硬山顶

yìng shān dǐng · Flush Gable Roof
名词 n. · 屋顶形制

中国传统屋顶形式之一，屋面双坡，两侧山墙与屋面齐平或略高于屋面。其山墙可有效防止火灾蔓延，是北方民居最常见的屋顶形式。哈达铺上街村义和昌药店为五檩四椽双坡四段椽硬山顶。瑞安堡大门门廊上布置面阔三间进深一间的硬山顶式门楼。三陶村徐氏民宅堂屋为硬山顶，房顶青砖铺就。

A traditional Chinese roof form with two slopes and gable walls flush with or above the roof. Gable walls effectively prevent fire spread and are the most common roof form in northern dwellings. Yihechang Pharmacy in Hadapu has a five-purlin four-rafter flush gable roof. Rui'an Fort's gate porch has a three-bay flush gable gate tower. Xu's Residence in Santao has a flush gable main hall with blue-brick roofing.

另见 See also: 悬山顶、歇山顶

歇山顶

xiē shān dǐng · Gable-and-hip Roof
名词 n. · 屋顶形制

中国传统屋顶形式之一，由一条正脊、四条垂脊和四条戗脊组成，又称九脊顶。其等级仅次于庑殿顶，常用于宫殿、寺庙等重要建筑。平堡村灯山楼1691年建为歇山顶，6.8米宽。八坊十三巷老王寺大殿殿顶呈歇山形，殿内雕梁画栋。东西街村城隍庙山门和大殿均为重檐歇山顶。

A traditional Chinese roof form with one main ridge, four vertical ridges, and four diagonal ridges (nine-ridge roof). Ranking second only to hip roofs in hierarchy, it is used for palaces and temples. Dengshan Tower in Pingbao (1691) and Laowang Mosque's main hall feature gable-and-hip roofs. Chenghuang Temple in Dongxijie has double-eave gable-and-hip gates and halls.

另见 See also: 悬山顶、硬山顶、攒尖顶

攒尖顶

zǎn jiān dǐng · Pyramidal Roof
名词 n. · 屋顶形制

中国传统屋顶形式之一，屋面在顶部交汇为一点，无正脊。常用于亭、阁、塔等建筑。合作寺米拉日巴佛阁为九层攒尖顶建筑，是安多藏区仅有的一座供奉藏传佛教各派宗师的高层建筑名刹。

A traditional Chinese roof where surfaces converge at a single point without a main ridge. Used for pavilions, towers, and pagodas. Hezuo Monastery's Milarepa Buddha Pavilion is a nine-story pyramidal-roof building, the only high-rise temple in Amdo Tibetan region enshrining masters of all Tibetan Buddhist schools.

另见 See also: 歇山顶、佛阁

平屋顶

píng wū dǐng · Flat Roof
名词 n. · 屋顶形制

屋面近乎水平的屋顶形式。甘南地区建筑墙体较厚，开间进深较大，以平屋顶为主，且屋顶可上人，一般用作晾晒用。王院村王家大院平屋顶上覆砂石，檐口红色板材。茨日那村民居都为素土夯实平屋顶，屋顶层高各不相同，之间有垂直交通相连，错落有致。

A nearly horizontal roof form. Southern Gansu buildings feature thick walls, large bays, and predominantly flat accessible roofs used for drying. Wang Family Compound in Wangyuan has a flat roof covered with sand/gravel and red eave boards. Cirina Village dwellings have compacted-earth flat roofs at varying heights connected by vertical circulation.

另见 See also: 土木结构、外不见木内不见土

清真寺

qīng zhēn sì · Mosque
名词 n. · 宗教建筑

穆斯林举行宗教活动的场所。甘肃清真寺融合中国传统建筑风格与阿拉伯特色，八坊十三巷有八座清真寺分属不同教派（格迪目、伊赫瓦尼等）。汪家咀村清真寺明洪武十三年（1380年）创建，采用中国传统四合院形制沿中轴线布置若干进四合院。南关大寺采用中国现代建筑与阿拉伯传统建筑相互融合形制，礼拜大殿建筑面积2160平方米，可容纳2000多人同时礼拜。

Places of Islamic worship. Gansu mosques blend traditional Chinese architecture with Arab features; Bafang Shisanxiang has eight mosques of different sects (Qadim, Ikhwan, etc.). Wangjiazui Mosque (1380) follows the traditional Chinese siheyuan layout. Nanguan Grand Mosque blends modern Chinese and Arab styles, with a 2,160 sqm prayer hall accommodating 2,000+ worshippers.

另见 See also: 邦克楼、拱北

邦克楼

bāng kè lóu · Minaret
名词 n. · 宗教建筑

清真寺中召唤穆斯林礼拜的塔楼，亦称宣礼塔。八坊十三巷老王寺邦克楼为五层六角木质塔式楼，彩饰华丽，为甘肃清真寺中最为高大雄伟的邦克楼。南关大寺殿顶设3个六角圆顶塔呈平面三角形配置，居前两个较小高15.75米，居后座中一个较大高26.8米，楼门饰有古兰经古体经文浮雕，3个圆顶顶端皆饰新月标志。

The tower in a mosque from which Muslims are called to prayer. Laowang Mosque has a five-story hexagonal wooden minaret with ornate decoration, the tallest in Gansu. Nanguan Grand Mosque has three hexagonal domed towers in triangular arrangement: two at 15.75m and one at 26.8m, decorated with ancient Quranic script reliefs and crescent motifs.

另见 See also: 清真寺

拱北

gǒng běi · Sufi Mausoleum (Gongbei)
名词 n. · 宗教建筑

伊斯兰教苏菲派先贤的墓庐建筑。八坊十三巷大拱北始建于清康熙五十九年（1720年），是嘎迪忍耶大拱北门宦始传人祁静一（1656-1719）的墓庐所在地，为中国伊斯兰教嘎德忍耶三大圣地之一，主要建筑有三层八卦墓庐和清真寺、牌坊、影壁、经堂院、碑厅、井厅、客厅、静室、花园等。现总占地面积约14580平方米，总建筑面积约2600平方米。拱北内砖雕林立，堪称临夏砖雕艺术宝库中的瑰宝。

Mausoleum buildings for Sufi saints. The Great Gongbei in Bafang Shisanxiang (1720) is the mausoleum of Qi Jingyi (1656-1719), founder of the Qadiriyya Great Gongbei order, and one of China's three major Qadiriyya sacred sites, with a three-story eight-trigram mausoleum, mosque, paifang, screen wall, scripture hall, and gardens, totaling 14,580 sqm. Its brick carvings are treasures of Linxia brick carving art.

嘛呢房

ma ne fáng · Mani House
名词 n. · 宗教建筑

藏区供奉转经筒的宗教建筑。尼巴村嘛呢房外夯土内木构，内置108个经筒，黄铜质地。拉力沟村嘛呢房最早建于清代，新中国成立后于原址重修，建筑面积300平方米。嘛呢房通常位于村落核心位置，是村民日常宗教活动的中心。

Religious buildings in Tibetan areas housing prayer wheels. Niba's mani house has rammed-earth exterior and timber interior with 108 brass prayer wheels. Laligou's mani house (Qing dynasty, rebuilt after 1949) covers 300 sqm. Mani houses are typically located at the village core as centers of daily religious activity.

经筒

jīng tǒng · Prayer Wheel
名词 n. · 宗教器物

藏传佛教中刻有经文的圆筒，转动即代表诵经。尼巴村嘛呢房内置108个经筒，黄铜质地。合作寺有嘛呢转经房。风嘛呢坊则利用风力自动转动经筒，尼巴村阳坡东端有百年历史的风嘛呢坊，风力叶片形似木瓢。

Cylindrical devices inscribed with scriptures in Tibetan Buddhism; turning them represents chanting. Niba's mani house contains 108 brass prayer wheels. Hezuo Monastery has a mani prayer wheel hall. Wind-powered mani houses use wind to automatically turn wheels; Niba has a century-old wind mani house with wooden ladle-shaped blades.

白塔

bái tǎ · White Pagoda
名词 n. · 宗教建筑

藏传佛教中的白色佛塔，通常为覆钵式塔。尼巴村白塔为村落核心，博峪村村落边界处有两座白塔是村民精神信仰和宗教活动场所。合作寺景区现有白塔。

White Buddhist stupas in Tibetan Buddhism, typically of the覆钵 (lamasery) style. Niba Village's white pagoda serves as the village core; Boyu Village has two white pagodas at its boundary serving as spiritual and religious gathering places. Hezuo Monastery scenic area also has a white pagoda.

佛阁

fó gé · Buddhist Pavilion
名词 n. · 宗教建筑

供奉佛像的楼阁式建筑。合作寺米拉日巴佛阁始建于清乾隆四十二年（1777年），原建楼阁已毁于文革时期，现存建筑重建于1988年5月，建筑面积4000平方米，历时四年落成，高九层，俗称九层楼。供奉以米拉日巴尊者及其弟子为主的藏传佛教各派的开宗祖师，有以金刚为主的四密乘的众多佛像、菩萨、护法神等各类佛像1270尊，是安多藏区仅有的一座供奉藏传佛教各派宗师的高层建筑名刹。

Pavilion-style buildings housing Buddha statues. Hezuo Monastery's Milarepa Pavilion (1777, destroyed during the Cultural Revolution, rebuilt 1988-1992) covers 4,000 sqm across nine stories, enshrining 1,270 statues of Tibetan Buddhist sect founders led by Milarepa. It is the only high-rise temple in Amdo Tibetan region enshrining masters of all Tibetan Buddhist schools.

囊欠

náng qiàn · Lama's Residence (Nangqian)
名词 n. · 宗教建筑

藏传佛教寺院中活佛的府邸。合作寺有赛仓活佛囊欠和色赤活佛囊欠。一世色赤坚赞僧格于1749年在合作寺建立法相讲闻之规，自此政教之权亦由色赤活佛系统掌管，经历代色赤活佛经营，合作寺成为甘南地区规模较大的寺院之一。

The residence of a reincarnated lama (living Buddha) in a Tibetan Buddhist monastery. Hezuo Monastery contains the Nangqian of both the Sercang and Setri incarnate lamas. The first Setri, Jiansan Sengge, established scriptural debate at Hezuo in 1749, after which the Setri lineage held both religious and secular authority, building Hezuo into one of Southern Gansu's largest monasteries.

辩经

biàn jīng · Debating Scriptures
名词 n. · 宗教活动

藏传佛教喇嘛攻读显宗经典的必经方式，按照因明学体系的逻辑推理方式辩论佛教教义，藏语称"村尼作巴"，意为法相。辩经多在寺院内空旷之地、树荫下进行，最早源于赤松德赞时期大乘和尚和噶玛拉锡拉的公开辩论。主要可分为对辩（藏语称作朗，二人问答）和立宗辩（藏语称当贾狭，一人立论待人辩驳）两种形式。农历正月十四上午十一时左右举行一年中规模最大的辩经。

An essential practice for Tibetan Buddhist lamas studying sutras, debating Buddhist doctrine using Buddhist logic (Tibetan: "cünizo ba," meaning dharma aspect). Held in open spaces or shade within monasteries, originating from the 8th-century public debate between the Chinese monk Heshang and the Indian scholar Kamalashila. Two forms: paired debate (two people, one asks one answers) and doctrinal debate (one person establishes a thesis to be challenged). The largest annual debate is held around 11 AM on the 14th day of the first lunar month.

另见 See also: 法舞、囊欠

法舞

fǎ wǔ · Cham Dance
名词 n. · 宗教活动

藏传佛教宗教法会上的面具舞蹈，俗称跳神。舞者佩戴面具，通过面具形象地介绍人物身份善恶角色，跳跃幅度大，旋转多，借鉴了民间舞蹈风格，展现了高原民族宽大胸怀豪放气质和疾恶如仇视死如归性格。合作寺最大最为隆重的宗教法会跳法舞在大经堂前举行，意在祈愿新的一年吉祥如意。

Masked dances performed at Tibetan Buddhist religious ceremonies, commonly called 'god dancing.' Dancers wear masks identifying characters' identities and moral roles, with large leaps and spins drawing on folk dance styles, expressing the plateau people's broad-mindedness, boldness, and courage. Hezuo Monastery's most grand cham dance is held before its main scripture hall, praying for auspiciousness in the new year.

另见 See also: 辩经、风马旗

风马旗

fēng mǎ qí · Wind Horse Flag (Lungta)
名词 n. · 宗教器物

甘南藏区有在隘口与山顶放飞纸风马旗的习俗，适逢祭祀节日寒风劲吹时纷纷扬扬飘入云端娱悦天神。风马旗还有一项不易见到的功能：遇有活佛尊者圆寂，家家户户须将房顶上的风马旗置放倾斜以示致哀。藏族人每每将自己或逝者的手镯、帽子、须发或一团羊毛系于风马旗上，希冀能借助其力牵引升腾，使运气增长、福星高随。

The custom of flying paper wind-horse flags at passes and summits in Southern Gansu Tibetan areas, released during festivals to delight the gods. A lesser-known function: upon a lama's passing, households tilt rooftop flags as mourning. Tibetans tie bracelets, hats, hair, or wool to the flags, hoping their fortunes will rise with them.

保安腰刀

bǎo ān yāo dāo · Bonan Waist Knife
名词 n. · 非物质文化遗产

保安族传统手工艺制品，与藏刀、蒙古刀齐名。工序多者达80多道，少者也有30-40道，一般是先把择好的铁反复锻打，然后劈开加钢，最后淬火而成。其中制坯时的加钢、炼烧后的淬水至关重要。著名品种有什样锦、什样锦双刀、雅王其、波日季、一刀线、双落、满把、扁鞘、蒙古刀、哈萨克刀、鱼刀等。1987年获国家民委国家轻工部优质产品称号，1991年获北京国际博览会金奖。周恩来总理曾将保安腰刀作为礼物馈赠给外宾。

The traditional handicraft of the Bonan people, as renowned as Tibetan and Mongolian knives. Production involves 80+ steps (minimum 30-40): repeated forging, splitting and inserting steel, then quenching. The steel insertion and quenching are critical. Famous varieties include Shiyangjin, Shiyangjin double knife, Yawangqi, Boriji. Won the 1987 State Ethnic Affairs Commission/Light Industry Quality Award and 1991 Beijing International Expo Gold Award. Premier Zhou Enlai gifted Bonan knives to foreign guests.

保安族主要居住在临夏境内的积石山县大河家镇，现有人口11000多人，信仰伊斯兰教。The Bonan people mainly live in Dahejia Town, Jishishan County, Linxia, with a population of over 11,000, practicing Islam.

池哥昼

chí gē zhòu · Chigezhou Mask Dance
名词 n. · 非物质文化遗产

白马藏人传统祭祀活动和民间舞蹈。正月初五预习面具舞，正月十三正式跳，正月十六结束。九位男性表演：四人扮山神池哥四弟兄，两人扮菩萨池姆，两人扮夫妻池玛，十多岁儿童扮猴娃子。白马藏族是公元6世纪吐蕃王朝东征大军与唐朝部队在九寨沟相遇后形成的族群，"白马"古藏语意为"藏族的兵"。

The traditional sacrificial activity and folk dance of the Baima Tibetan people. Mask dance rehearsals begin on the 5th day of the first lunar month, formal performances from the 13th to the 16th. Nine male performers: four as mountain gods (Chige brothers), two as bodhisattvas (Chimu), two as a couple (Chima), and a young boy as a monkey child. The Baima Tibetans formed from 6th-century Tibetan expansion forces encountering Tang armies at Jiuzhaigou; "Baima" means "Tibetan soldiers" in ancient Tibetan.

宝卷

bǎo juàn · Precious Scroll (Baojuan)
名词 n. · 非物质文化遗产

流行于民间的说唱文学，由历史人物、民间神话、传说和戏曲故事演变而来，内容主要是教化人心，散韵相间。硖口宝卷是河西宝卷的重要组成部分，在硖口境内流传的约有五种，均为毛笔手抄本。宝卷是在唐代敦煌变文、俗讲以及宋代说经的基础上发展而成的一种民间吟唱的俗文学，受到话本、小说、戏曲等的影响，内容包含儒、释、道的三教合一及各种秘密宗教。在电视文化出现以前，文化落后、交通闭塞的农村是宝卷流行的主要场所。

A folk narrative-singing literature evolved from historical figures, myths, legends, and opera stories, primarily for moral instruction, alternating prose and verse. Xiakou Baojuan, an important part of Hexi Baojuan, has about five hand-copied versions. Developed from Tang dynasty Dunhuang transformation texts and Song dynasty scripture preaching, it incorporates Confucian, Buddhist, and Daoist syncretism. Before television, remote rural areas were the main venues for Baojuan.

花儿

huā ér · Hua'er (Musical Flower)
名词 n. · 非物质文化遗产

流行于甘肃、青海、宁夏等地区的山歌形式。汪家咀村花儿19世纪传入，洮州花儿流传甘南临潭卓尼部分地区及岷县康乐临洮小块地区。东西街村1980年前后花儿是主要娱乐活动，2000年后渐渐不受年轻人欢迎，目前接近消亡状态。临夏每年在莲花山、松鸣岩等地举行花儿演唱比赛，规模宏大影响深远，有花儿之乡之称。

A mountain song form popular in Gansu, Qinghai, and Ningxia. Hua'er was introduced to Wangjiazui in the 19th century; Taozhou hua'er spreads in parts of Lintan and Zhuoni plus small areas of Minxian, Kangle, and Lintao. In Dongxijie it was the main entertainment around 1980 but declined after 2000 and is now near extinction. Linxia holds large-scale hua'er competitions at Lianhua Mountain and Songming Rock, earning it 'Home of Hua'er.'

皮影戏

pí yǐng xì · Shadow Puppetry
名词 n. · 非物质文化遗产

艺人们在白色幕布后面一边操纵戏曲人物一边用当地流行曲调唱述故事的戏剧形式，有时用方言同时配以打击乐器和弦乐。父坪村韩山社皮影戏文字考证于民国十年（1921年）线装戏本，传承约六代人，流传范围南至礼县北门北至武山县西门。乐器配有四弦、三弦、二胡、板胡、大小锣鼓、芽板、牛铃等，唱法分上音和下音，风格悠扬凄凉。其由来有四川和山西传入两种说法，以演唱风格来看从陕西北部一带传入较为可信。

A theatrical form where artists manipulate figures behind a white screen while singing in local tunes (sometimes in dialect) with percussion and string instruments. Hanshan She shadow puppetry in Fuping is documented to 1921 thread-bound scripts, passed down six generations, ranging from Lixian north gate to Wushan west gate. Instruments include four-string, three-string, erhu, banhu, gongs/drums, and cow-bells; singing divided into upper and lower tones, melodious and melancholic. Origin theories include Sichuan and Shanxi; the singing style suggests northern Shaanxi.

社火

shè huǒ · Shehuo (Festival Parade)
名词 n. · 非物质文化遗产

中国民间在节日期间的综合性庆祝表演活动。天城村社火秧歌由"地蹦子"与"凤翔秧歌"组合而成，地蹦子由15人组成：4个男鼓子、4个女鼓子、4个棒槌和尚，以及膏药匠、傻公子、大肚子丑婆娘。表演时配合舞龙、赶旱船、踩高跷、竹驴竹马、大头和尚等，表演技巧繁多、内容丰富。永泰村有社火、背鼓子舞、景泰滚灯、景泰拉花、寿鹿山道教音乐等5项非遗。

A comprehensive folk celebration during festivals. Tiancheng's shehuo combines 'dibengzi' and Fengyang yangge, with 15 performers (4 male drummers, 4 female drummers, 4 monk-stick dancers, a medicine man, a fool, and a fat ugly woman), plus dragon dance, land-boat, stilt-walking, bamboo donkey/horse, and big-head monk. Yongtai Village has 5 ICH items including shehuo, back-drum dance, Jingtai rolling lanterns, flower pulling, and Shoulu Mountain Daoist music.

砖雕

zhuān diāo · Brick Carving
名词 n. · 装饰工艺

在青砖上雕刻图案的装饰工艺。八坊十三巷融合回族砖雕、汉族木刻、藏族彩绘，集民族特色于一体。东宫馆砖雕艺术容纳汇集了河州雕刻艺术之精华，堪称砖雕作品的大观园，各院甬道、看墙、影壁、廊心墙都是磨砖对缝砖雕镶嵌而成。大拱北内砖雕林立，雕刻内容丰富形象逼真，堪称临夏砖雕艺术宝库中的瑰宝。

The decorative art of carving patterns into blue bricks. Bafang Shisanxiang blends Hui brick carving, Han wood carving, and Tibetan painting. Donggong Mansion's brick carvings collect the essence of Hezhou carving art, hailed as a 'grand garden of brick carving,' with all corridors, walls, and screen walls inlaid with finely-jointed carved brick. The Great Gongbei's brick carvings are rich and vivid, treasures of Linxia brick carving art.

另见 See also: 木雕、拱北

木雕

mù diāo · Wood Carving
名词 n. · 装饰工艺

在木材上雕刻图案的装饰工艺。大墩村保安族民居常用花凿墙雕纹饰，在木头上雕刻各种花纹用来装饰门梁等建筑。瑞安堡木雕精美，是其四大特点（设计独特、布局巧妙、木雕精美、防御功能强）之一。八坊十三巷大旮巷80号砖雕木雕雕工细腻，飞椽画栋，门庭高深。

The decorative art of carving patterns into wood. Bonan dwellings in Dadun commonly use ornate chiseled wall carvings with floral patterns on door beams. Rui'an Fort's exquisite wood carving is one of its four defining features (unique design, clever layout, fine wood carving, strong defense). No. 80 Daga Lane in Bafang Shisanxiang has delicate brick and wood carving with flying rafters and painted beams.

另见 See also: 砖雕、瑞安堡

夯土墙

hāng tǔ qiáng · Rammed Earth Wall
名词 n. · 建筑构件

用黏土、沙、石灰混合拌料，分层夯实，分层夹入柔韧度极好的红柳枝条筑成的墙体。瑞安堡围墙底部厚7米、顶宽2.8米、高12米，重要位置由大城砖加固。古战村牛头城遗址城垣夯筑方法为红壤土黏性强堆筑后浇水拍打，水分蒸发后即板结成型。永泰村龟城城墙外有护城河6米宽2.5米深。

Walls of mixed clay, sand, and lime, compacted in layers with flexible tamarisk branches. Rui'an Fort walls: 7m base thickness, 2.8m top, 12m high, key positions reinforced by large bricks. Guzhan Village's Niutou City ruins used sticky red loam piled and watered then beaten, hardening as water evaporated. Yongtai Turtle City has a moat 6m wide and 2.5m deep outside its walls.

土坯墙

tǔ pī qiáng · Adobe Wall
名词 n. · 建筑构件

用土坯（晒干的泥砖）砌筑的墙体。甘肃陇上民居采用土坯墙和砖基的建筑格式，屋子冬暖夏凉，外观华美，坚固实用。石榴村16座清中晚期建筑采用土坯墙和砖基，屋脊簪花坐兽，廊檐宽阔，雕梁画栋，檐下四门八窗，刻字镂花，青埂石条铺砌门廊台阶，院落石铺图案祥瑞美观。

Walls built with adobe (sun-dried mud bricks). Gansu loess-plateau dwellings use adobe walls on brick foundations, warm in winter and cool in summer with an ornate yet durable appearance. Shiliu Village's 16 mid-late Qing buildings use adobe walls on brick bases, with ornate ridge ornaments, wide eaves, carved beams, four doors/eight windows with engraved patterns, and stone-paved courtyards with auspicious designs.

女儿墙

nǚ ér qiáng · Parapet Wall
名词 n. · 建筑构件

建筑物顶部外侧的矮墙。瑞安堡巡道外侧砖砌掩体女儿墙厚0.5米高2.2米，上砌对外射击孔，巡道内侧砌0.7米高护墙。女儿墙在传统建筑中既有安全防护功能，也有装饰作用。

Low walls along the outer edge of a building's top. Rui'an Fort's patrol path outer parapet is 0.5m thick and 2.2m high, with outward shooting holes, and a 0.7m inner wall. Parapet walls serve both safety and decorative functions in traditional architecture.

影壁

院落内或大门外的屏蔽墙壁，亦称照壁。八坊十三巷民居院落一般由影壁、正房、左右厢房、下房等组成。东宫馆正北为三开屏式大影壁，上雕江山图一幅，东北西北两边角门有插着牡丹的砖雕宝瓶一对。大拱北有牌坊式墓庐照壁和一字亭牌坊式大门。

The Great Gongbei has a paifang-style tomb screen wall and a one-character pavilion gate.

雀替

què tì · Sparrow Brace (Queti)
名词 n. · 建筑构件

中国传统建筑中置于梁枋与柱相交处的装饰性构件，兼具结构加固和装饰作用。梅江村假脊假带七云头雀替，佛堂院积善德。雀替的纹样丰富多样，常见的有云纹、卷草、花卉、动物等。

Decorative brackets placed at beam-column intersections in traditional Chinese architecture, serving both structural reinforcement and decoration. Meijiang Village features false ridge/false band with seven-cloud-head queti in its Buddhist hall courtyard. Queti patterns are diverse, commonly including cloud motifs, scrolling foliage, flowers, and animals.

外不见木内不见土

wài bù jiàn mù nèi bù jiàn tǔ · Wood Concealed Outside, Earth Concealed Inside
名词短语 n.phr. · 建筑特征

甘南藏区传统土木结构建筑的典型特征：外部以夯土墙围护，看不到木构件；内部以木结构为承重框架，看不到土体。尼巴村百年藏寨、扎尕那村、茨日那村、王府村民居均体现此特征。这种做法既保护木材免受风雨侵蚀，又利用土体保温隔热，适应了甘南高寒气候。磨沟村传统土木结构也遵循此原则，部分灾后重建房屋添加阳光间以改善采光取暖。

A defining feature of traditional earthy-wood buildings in Southern Gansu Tibetan areas: the exterior is enclosed by rammed earth with no visible timber, while the interior uses a timber load-bearing frame with no visible earth. Seen in Niba's century-old Tibetan stockade, Zhagana, Cirina, and Wangfu dwellings. This protects timber from weather while utilizing earth's thermal insulation, adapting to Southern Gansu's alpine climate. Mogou Village's traditional earthy-wood structures follow this principle, with some post-disaster reconstruction adding sunrooms for improved heating.

过街楼

guò jiē lóu · Street-crossing Tower
名词 n. · 公共建筑

横跨街道上方的楼阁式建筑，下部为通道，上部为楼阁。硖口村过街楼始建于明代，坐落于南北中心街道偏南，路两旁筑石条砖石砌面的黄土夯筑墩台，间距4米，两台阶架过梁铺木板，上建木结构小楼，悬一大匾楷书"威镇乾坤"四个大字，古朴苍劲，端庄秀逸。

A pavilion-style building spanning above a street, with a passage below and a pavilion above. Xiakou's Ming-dynasty tower stands south of the central north-south street on two rammed-earth platforms 4m apart, with timber beams and planks forming a bridge and a wooden pavilion above bearing a plaque inscribed 'Power Over Heaven and Earth' in vigorous, elegant regular script.

另见 See also: 硖口村

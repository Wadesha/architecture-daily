About — 平台定位（英文原文全文）

中文导读Architectura Sinica 正在积极建设中，旨在成为一个面向中国传统文化建筑的、公开的浏览器端研究门户。建成后将由四部分组成，各部分既支持后续研究，也欢迎他人贡献：
原文

Currently in active development, Architectura Sinica is designed to be a public, browser-based research portal for the study of China's traditional architecture. When complete the site will consist of four parts, each designed to support research and the contributions of others:

中文导读(1) 动态遗址档案：收录大小遗址与单体建筑，可按遗址名称（古/今名）及描述单座建筑构件的技术术语检索；检索词含中文、现代汉语拼音与英文译名，并与地图工具及照片档案联动，可按中国现代地图定位检索结果。
原文

1. A Dynamic Site Archive of historic monuments, both larger sites and individual structures, searchable by name of site (ancient and modern) and by technical terminology used to describe individual elements of single buildings. Search terms will be in Chinese, pinyin Romanization of modern Mandarin, and English translation, and entries will be fully integrated with a mapping tool and a photographic archive, and will locate search results in a map of the People's Republic of China.

中文导读(2) 术语词典与索引典（ATTCAT）：收录描述中国传统建筑的技术术语条目，多为“中国传统建筑术语的注释与翻译”项目成果；项目每年举办工作坊，由合作院校的学者与研究生共同研讨、评价并注释已有译本。工作坊由陈薇、诸葛净、左拉拉、梅晨曦主持，获南京东南大学建筑学院与（2021 年起）上海纽约大学资助。
原文

2. A encyclopedic dictionary and thesaurus of technical terminology used to describe Traditional Chinese Architecture. Most entries are the product of the ATTCAT (The Annotation and Translation of Traditional Chinese Architectural Terminology) Project; this project sponsors annual workshops in which scholarly specialists, generally faculty and graduate students from partner institutions, come together to discuss, evaluate, and annotate pre-existing translations. Workshops are led by Chen Wei 陳薇, Zhuge Jing 諸葛净, Zuo Lala 左拉拉, and Tracy Miller 梅晨曦, and are sponsored by Southeast University School of Architecture in Nanjing and, since 2021, NYU Shanghai.

中文导读(3) 书目：收录中国传统建筑研究文献，重点为条目所引来源，也含建筑传统史论文献。
原文

3. A bibliography of sources on Traditional Chinese Architecture, focusing on sources cited in the entries, with additional bibliography on the history of building traditions.

中文导读(4) 铭文：收录遗址所见碑刻题记的照片与转录文字，建成后可用繁体字检索碑铭题名与撰者。
原文

4. Photographs and transcriptions of epigraphic information found at a given site, searchable, when complete, by traditional characters in the title and in the author of the inscription.

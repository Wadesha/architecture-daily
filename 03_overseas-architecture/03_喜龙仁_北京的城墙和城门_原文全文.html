序言 · Preface（全文）

中文导读本书的缘起，在于北京城门之美——它们是中国都城若干最动人景致中极具特色的组成部分；它们与古老建筑、葱郁树木和斑驳的城垣相互映衬，构成奇妙的背景；它们具有独特的建筑性格，无论从历史上还是地形上说，都仍可称为北京的标志。城门连同相邻的城墙，映照出这座伟大城市早期历史的许多篇章；它们与其所处的街巷和景观一起，构成了最具特色、也最美的风景点。
原文

The origin of this book is the beauty of the city gates of Peking; their importance as characteristic elements in some of the finest views of the Chinese capital; their wonderful setting amidst old buildings, fine trees and decaying masonry; their distinctive architectural character, once these gates may still be called landmarks of Peking, historically as well as topographically; they reflect, together with the adjoining walls, much of the early history of this great city, and they form, together with the streets and landscapes in which they are set, the most relevant spots of characteristic and beautiful scenery.

中文导读正是这些印象，促使我投入数月时间专门研究北京城门，力求用一组照片再现它们的美。至于做到几分，留待读者评判——本书选印了其中 109 幅照相凹版图版。
原文

Such were the impressions which led me to devote months of special study to the Peking gates with the aim of reproducing their beauty in a series of photographs. To what degree this has been accomplished may be left to the reader to judge; a selection of these photographs are reproduced in the present work on 109 heliogravure plates.

中文导读对城门艺术性格的兴趣，逐渐唤起了我了解它们作为历史遗迹之价值的愿望，促使我进一步深入它们营建与历代变迁的历史。不仅城门本身，它们的周边环境以及由城门与墙体连成的长垣，都引起了我作为历史与建筑研究材料的兴趣；我越沉浸于这些材料，就越意识到其中藏着中国历史若干重要篇章的钥匙。
原文

The interest in the artistic character of the gates gradually awakened the desire to know something about their importance as monuments of the past, to penetrate further into the history of their construction and their modifications in various ages. Not only the gates but their surroundings and the long walls of which they form part attracted my interest as material for historical and architectural studies; and the more I dwelt on this material, the more I realized that it contained the keys to some important chapters of Chinese history.

第一章《华北的城墙城市》开篇

中文导读人人都听说过中国的万里长城，但大多数人只把它当作一件纯粹的历史遗物……这是完全的误解。长城是少数几处得到妥善保存的中国建筑之一。数百年间，它被一再修缮、重建，直到很近的年代，它作为防御工事与边界的意义都相当重大……事实上，中国人对“城郭围合”根深蒂固的信念，正是通过长城得到了最充分、最持久的表达。
原文

Everyone has heard of the Great Wall of China, but most people think of it as a mere historical relic, the stately ruin of an enterprise which sprang from an ancient emperor's overheated fancy. The prevailing impression about it is that it has fallen naturally into decay like other things in China, and that for hundreds of years past its practical importance has been largely a delusion. This is a complete misapprehension. The Great Wall is one of the few Chinese buildings which have been properly preserved. It has been repaired and reconstructed over and over again in the course of the centuries, and its importance both as a defence-work and as a boundary has been very considerable until quite recent years; certainly this is the opinion of the Chinese themselves. In it, in fact, the deep-rooted belief of the Chinese in walled enclosures finds its fullest and most enduring expression.

中文导读城墙、城墙、还是城墙——可以说，城墙构成了每一座中国城市的骨架或框架……华北没有一座真正的城市是没有城墙环绕的——这一点甚至体现在语言上：中国人用同一个字“城”，既指城市，也指城墙。
原文

Walls, walls, and yet again walls form, so to say, the skeleton or framework of every Chinese city. They surround it, they divide it into lots and compounds, they mark more than any other structures the communal basis of these Chinese communities. There is no real city in Northern China without a surrounding wall, a condition which, indeed, is expressed by the fact that the Chinese use the same word Ch'eng for a city and a city-wall.

第七章《鞑靼城的城门》引言（全文）

中文导读不妨把城门称作城市的嘴。这座巨大的、被城墙围合的身体——五十万乃至更多生灵栖居其间——正是通过这些开口呼吸吐纳。全城的生活都汇聚于这些狭窄的开口……在城门处，可以感到整座城市的心跳，仿佛城市的生命与意志正从这些狭窄的开口中流过——这一阵阵脉搏，为这座名为北京的、极其复杂的有机体，标定了生命与活动的节奏。
原文

The gates may be called the mouths of the city; they are the openings through which this huge walled-in body of half a million or more organisms breathes and aspires. The life of the whole city becomes concentrated at these narrow openings; everything that goes out of it or in it must pass this narrow opening. And that which passes in and out is not simply a mass of vehicles, animals, and human beings, but thoughts and desires, hopes and despair, death and new life, in the shape of messages, and human feelings. At the gates can be felt the pulse of the whole city, as if its life and purpose flows through the narrow openings—a pulse-beat which gives the rhythm of the life and activity of this highly complex organism which is called Peking.

中文导读入夜，这脉搏变得微弱而几乎难以察觉——城门关闭了，或者说过去一向如此，正值居民安睡之时。日出时分，当最早的旅人乘着大车或骡轿踏上长途，沉重的木门被缓缓推开，发出呻吟，仿佛不愿被唤醒的巨人。渐渐地，乡下人开始涌入……随着日头升高，城门处的车流与人流逐渐增大、愈益多样……近黄昏，人流又渐渐稀疏；暮色渐浓成夜，流动终于停止。（不过如今北京主要城门的关闭，已远不如从前、也不如大多数省城那样严格了。）
原文

At night it becomes faint and almost imperceptible; the gates are closed, or used to be so, during the sleeping hours of the citizens. At sunrise when the first travellers start on their long journeys in carts or mule-litters, the heavy wooden doors are slowly pushed back, groaning like giants unwillingly aroused. Gradually the country people begin to pour in with their wheelbarrows or baskets filled with the products of the soil swinging from the ends of springy poles resting on their shoulders. And as the sun rises higher, the traffic and the movement at the gates gradually increase and become more varied; the stream of porters, wheelbarrows, and donkey carts is mixed up with rickshaws and automobiles which keep up an excessive and futile noise with their sirens. The main rhythm of the movement focussed at these narrow passages is not to be disturbed by any threatening sounds. It may become highly intensified but not accelerated; it may even be brought to a temporary standstill when too many wheelbarrows and rickshaws are trying to pass through in opposite directions, and the main gates are the centres of this circulation about the time of the midday meal, when everybody is out for a little. Towards evening the stream grows thinner again, and as the twilight deepens into night the flow gradually ceases. (Though nowadays the closing of the main gates of Peking is by no means as strictly carried out as it used to be and still is in most of the provincial towns.)

第七章 · 正阳门与崇文、宣武门

中文导读在这些城门间搏动的生命，不仅随时间推移而变化，也随城市的不同区域与郊区的性格而变化。南面是城市的主正面，也是交通与商业的中心所在，矗立着三座纪念性城门。居中的正阳门（字面义即“前面的门”）比其余两座大得多、也高得多。它从前是皇帝的城门，如今有时被称为“国门”……它依然是这座首都搏动生活的真正中心。
原文

The life that pulsates through these gates varies not only with the hours of the day but also according to the different quarters of the city and the character of the suburbs. On the south side, which is the main front of the city and where the greatest centres of traffic and business are found, there are three monumental gates. The central one, Ch'ien men (literally: the front gate), is a good deal larger and higher than the others. It used to be the emperor's gate, now it is sometimes called 'the Nation's gate' (Kuo men), and though much has been done to destroy its architectural grandeur and deprive it of its ancient characteristic surroundings, it is still the actual centre of the throbbing life of the capital.

中文导读在东、西两侧稍远处，分别矗立着哈达门（崇文门）与顺治门（宣武门）——这是通常的叫法，其正式名称不同。前者是南北直贯大街的入口，民间有时称作“青门”，即光明与昌盛之门，人人可用，连皇帝也不例外；与此恰成对照的是，西边的顺治门则被视为“厄运之门”。
原文

At some distance to the east and to the west stand respectively Hata men and Shun Chih men, as they are usually called, though their official names are different. The former are the entrances to the main streets running straight through south-north. The former was sometimes popularly called Ch'ing men, the gate of brightness and prosperity; it could be used by everybody, even the emperor. In direct opposition to this the western gate, Shun Chih men, was looked upon as the gate of adversity.

第七章 · 正阳门改建记 1915–16

中文导读为改造北京这座大中央城门而拟定的这些极为重要、影响深远的方案，于 1915 年制定并逐步实施，1916 年城门便大体呈现出现今的面貌。凡有幸见过正阳门旧观——巨大的瓮城、侧门与如画的庭院——的人，无不痛惜旧构被这样大面积拆毁；但与此同时，他们也承认旧时的状况无论从卫生还是交通的角度都已无法忍受。
原文

These very important and far-reaching plans for the modernization of the great central gate of Peking were prepared in 1915 and gradually put into effect, so that the gate actually acquired its present appearance in 1916. Those who have been fortunate enough to see Ch'ien men in its original state with the huge barbican, the side gates and the picturesque yard, are unanimous in deploring the wholesale manner in which so much of the old structure was demolished, but at the same time they admit that the old conditions were unbearable both from a hygienic point of view and from that of traffic.

中文导读瓮城城墙被完全拆除，原本封闭的门庭院落变成一片开阔地——一个长方形广场，南端立着一座独立而宽大的外城楼。旧内门两侧的城墙上各开两个新门洞，并辟新的大街，便利通往城门东西两侧的火车站。这些街道就沿旧瓮城墙外侧而行，汇合于跨越护城河的那座大桥。
原文

The barbican wall was entirely demolished and the closed gate-yard became an open space or an oblong with a broad detached outer tower at its southern end. Two new openings were made through the main wall on both sides of the old inner gate and new broad streets were arranged here, facilitating access to the stations on the east and west sides of the gate. The streets run just outside the old barbican wall and join at the broad bridge which leads over the moat.

第八章《中国城的城门》第 204–205 页 · 齐化门内/外楼测绘

中文导读……其高度即可说明一切。墙身长 13.8 米、宽 6 米，外廊 18 米见 10 米，与之相比，室内显得异常狭小。城楼自墩台以上高 17.6 米，自地面至屋脊之巅共 26 米。外观照例分为三层，以屋顶强调之，而室内仅有一层楼板，顶层只是容纳屋梁的敞开阁楼。承重立柱不多而纤细，正面仅四根包砌于砖墙之内。外廊为 5×3 跨的细柱，仅在转角处加倍。各部分保存仍属良好，几乎令人以为乾隆之后此处曾经过某种修缮；所缺者唯有阳台栏杆与二层门扉。部分梁上彩绘尚可辨识，屋顶完好，唯长满青草。
原文

…us by its height. The dimensions of the walls are 13.8 by 6 metres, and of the outer gallery 18 by 10 metres. The room is thus uncommonly small in proportion to the surrounding gallery. The height of the building above the bastion is 17.6 metres and from the ground to the top of the roof ridge 26 metres. It has the usual outward division into three stories, accentuated by the roofs, while there is only one dividing floor inside, the top story being simply an open loft for the roof-beams. The supporting columns are rather few and slender, and only four are encased on each front in the brick walls. The gallery has 5 by 3 spans of slender columns, and it is doubled only at the corners. The various parts are still well preserved, so that it almost seems as if some restorations had been made here after Ch'ien Lung's time; only the balustrade of the balcony and the doors of the second story are missing. The painted ornaments are still visible on some of the beams, and the roofs are complete, though overgrown with grass.

中文导读外楼仅约为内楼一半之高；自墩台以上 7.8 米，自地面计 16.6 米。平面为 13 米乘 6.6 米；两侧墙身各以两根包砌立柱支撑，正面与短边各开两排射孔，入口门开于背面。此即华北各地常见之“大楼”类型——通常建于券门之上。
原文

The outer tower is only about half as high as the inner; it measures above the bastion 7.8 metres and 16.6 metres from the ground. The plan is 13 by 6.6 metres; the walls are supported by two encased columns on each side and provided with two rows of loopholes on the front and on the short sides, while the entrance door is at the rear. It is [of a type that may be seen also elsewhere in North China—the so-called 'ta lou', or great tower, which usually rises above the vaulted gateway].
